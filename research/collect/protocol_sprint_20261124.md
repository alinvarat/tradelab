# Crypto collector forward studies (locked 2026-11-24)

Evaluate three independent simulated strategies on collection decisions in [2026-11-25 00:00, 2026-12-25 00:00) UTC. Run them against separate random controls and separate $147 starting wallets. Start each wallet in cash, with one position at a time, no borrowing and no deposits. A strategy risks up to 20% of current equity per entry, bounded by cash. Allow the predeclared exits to settle after the window. All parameters below are fixed before the first eligible snapshot. Keep deployment gaps in the coverage denominator.

## Cross-venue lead–lag (venue-v1)

- Use Coinbase BTC-USD and ETH-USD as leaders, Kraken XBT/USD and ETH/USD as execution markets. Map Kraken pair aliases explicitly. Use complete venue rows with finite positive uncrossed books, `rx-t <= 3s` and Coinbase/Kraken spreads at most 0.10%. The whole row becomes available at `rx`; no inference about intra-row ordering.
- Compare with the latest complete row at or before `rx-15s` and no older than 22s. Buy on Kraken when Coinbase mid is up at least 0.50%, Kraken mid is up at most 0.15%, and Coinbase bid is at least 0.45% above Kraken ask. If both assets qualify, prioritize greater percentage gap, then BTC.
- Buy at the first usable Kraken ask from a new row received at least 5s after the signal and no later than 20s. Cancel if the new Kraken ask erases the signal's original 0.45% gap. Sell when valid quotes show the gap has narrowed to 0.10% or less, or at 120s, whichever is first. Book the first valid bid received at least 5s after the exit condition. Skip new signals during positions and 15 minutes after closing.
- Apply 0.40% per side plus 0.05% slippage per side, with fees doubled and 10s decision latency in stress replay. Stake in dollars inclusive of fees. Require a minimum $10 order.

## Stablecoin re-peg (stable-v1)

- Use only USDT-USD on Coinbase and Kraken, where both quote the same token directly against USD. A new depeg episode begins when both mids are at most $0.995 on three consecutive complete rows at least 5s apart with gaps no larger than 10s. After an episode begins, track the lowest midpoint separately for each venue. Permit one entry when each mid has recovered by at least $0.002 above its own low and Coinbase ask remains below $0.997. Reset the episode only after both mids stay at or above $0.999 for 30 minutes.
- Enter on Coinbase at the first available new ask within 5–20s and below $0.997. Exit at the first valid bid at least 5s after the bid reaches $0.9995 or the 24-hour holding limit. Require valid books and the same minimum order and completeness rules as venue-v1. Fees are a fixed 0.10% per side and slippage 0.025% per side; double fees and use a 10s delay in stress replay.
- Issuer redemption and transfers are outside the collector. The test estimates secondary-market recovery of a discounted USDT quote. Record prolonged depegs and missing bids. There is no assumed redemption at $1.

## Deribit option-smile dislocation (smile-v1)

- Use inverse BTC and ETH contracts with 3–14 days to expiry at signal, calls with strikes from 1.02–1.12 times the recorded forward price and puts from 0.88–0.98. Require finite positive bid, ask and forward price, bid at most ask, full spread at most 20% of mid, and positive open interest. The strike and expiration are parsed from instrument ID `i`. Option prices are in the base coin. USD conversion uses the recorded forward `u` for that instrument as a proxy. Settlement units and minimum size are specified by Deribit. [Deribit inverse option specification](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/31424939096093-Inverse-Options)
- Interpolate the two nearest valid strike-neighbor mid prices for the same currency, expiry and side, linearly in strike. Require a neighbor on each side and each neighbor within 3% of the candidate strike. Signal when candidate ask is at most 85% of the interpolated mid. Choose largest percentage discount and break ties by instrument ID. Use the same-time forward in both signal and matching. This is a strike-price residual, with no use of future IV values.
- Signal information is usable 60s after the recorded batch time. Enter at the next valid snapshot from 25–45 minutes after the signal, while the discount still qualifies. Buy exactly one minimum order (0.1 BTC contract or 1 ETH contract) if its USD premium plus fees fits cash and stake. Sell at the first valid bid following a new observed bid reaching 95% of its contemporaneous interpolated mid, or following 24 hours from entry. Use the first subsequent valid snapshot and only bids observed at least 60s after the trigger snapshot's nominal timestamp. There is no replacement contract. On missing exits keep the position open and mark it at zero until a valid bid arrives; assign zero at expiry when the settlement feed is missing.
- Charge `quantity * min(0.0003, 0.125 * option_price) * u` per side in USD and multiply fees by two in stress replay. Use the recorded spread and apply an additional 2% reduction to exit proceeds in stress replay. No extra return is credited for the account's settlement currency. [Deribit fees](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944746248989-Fees)

## Controls and reporting

- Make the control draw without inspecting post-entry returns. A control shares the strategy's execution venue, fee, delay, exit and sizing rules. Match on asset and UTC day. Venue-v1 uses a randomly selected eligible timestamp with Kraken's spread and trailing 15s absolute mid change in the same bins (spread 0–5/5–10 bps, change 0–10/10–25/25+ bps), omitting the leader trigger. Stable-v1 randomly selects another initially eligible timestamp from the same episode with an ask in the same 0.001-wide price bin, omitting the recovery trigger. Smile-v1 randomly selects a different eligible contract from the same batch, currency, side, expiry and 0.02-wide strike/forward bin, omitting the smile trigger. Use ascending timestamp/instrument ordering, a deterministic SHA-256 rank with seed `tradelab-sprint-20261124` and signal ID, and no reused control starts. Unmatched trades remain in the strategy wallet and are counted separately.
- Across arms, require the stated quote eligibility and replay timestamps, use ask for entry and bid for exit, and never backfill a missing exit with a past quote. At final cutoff, mark unresolved positions conservatively at zero. Venue snapshots give no order-book depth and Deribit snapshots give no executable size; interpret returns as paper quote replays and report this limitation.
- Report signal count, fill count, coverage/missing snapshots, paired count, ending equity, worst marked drawdown and full-window return under both cost settings. Use daily-v1 (sequential protocol in PR #77) per strategy after that protocol is available, with one UTC exit-day unit and complete pairs for the matched series. To qualify as a research candidate, the locked window must end ahead of its matched control, remain positive under high cost and show a positive interim signal under daily-v1. Separately report whether the continuous wallet reached $441 after costs and beat its random control. Report insufficient coverage or no qualifying result where applicable.
