# LLM trader (paper) - v4 RULES (2026-11-24, before any v4 decision)
Owner direction: follow news and social attention across the whole market, not only large-cap US stocks; no fixed holding period; the single pinned DeepSeek model decides everything including leverage. Simulated money and public data only.
- **Account:** a NEW paper account (`data/llm_trader_v4.json`, log `data/llm_trader_log_v4.jsonl`). The v3 account/log are archived on the volume and are never mixed into v4 evidence.
- **Decider:** `openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` only, pinned; bull/bear and decision calls (headline scoring keeps its own locked comparison). Model id logged with every call.
- **Universe (217 symbols):** US stocks (148, the 104 large caps plus liquid growth/mid caps), ETFs (34), commodity futures (16: gold, silver, crude, Brent, gas, copper, grains, softs, platinum, palladium, cattle, hogs), FX (8 majors/CNY), crypto (11). Symbols and classes are in `core/universe.py`; the model may trade any of them.
- **Triggers:** (1) a class-specific shock — stock 5%, ETF 3%, commodity 2%, FX 1%, crypto 5% versus previous close; stocks/ETFs also need day volume >= 0.5x the 50-day average; (2) a linked archived headline first seen in the last 45 minutes (stock link or the macro keyword map); (3) a social-buzz surge in the last 2 hours whose ticker is in the universe; (4) a scheduled scan at least every 15 minutes while any venue is open. Minimum 60 s between cycles, 300 cycles/day runaway cap.
- **Candidates:** up to 12, from shocks, recent news, buzz and the largest movers; the context also lists the top movers per class so a scheduled call has something concrete to inspect. The model may buy any universe symbol, including ones not shown as candidates.
- **Context:** time, SPY, VIX, dollar index, US 10-year yield, gold, oil, BTC, account, open positions with class and margin, track record, the full universe and class counts, each candidate with move, move vs SPY, volume ratio when the source has volume, why it is a candidate, linked headlines and AI/lexicon scores.
- **Rules the model controls:** whether to trade, margin 0-100% of equity, leverage 1-20 (margin = notional / leverage), market or limit entry (5-1440 min), stop 1-25%, optional target up to 300%, horizon, review time 5 min-7 days, add to or cut a position.
- **Physical limits:** margin/cash and fresh open quote; total notional <= 20x equity via margin; engine liquidation at margin level 50%; financing 8% a year on the borrowed part charged to model and control and included in the trade/pair P&L; 180-day safety exit; pause only when the account is ruined (<5% of start); long only.
- **Costs:** the engine charges 0.05% per side, 0.02% slippage and a simulated spread (stock 0.04%, ETF/futures 0.03%, FX 0.01%, crypto 0.05%); the borrowed part pays 8% a year. These are conservative for FX and futures; results at higher cost are reported.
- **Control:** on every fill a random liquid symbol from the SAME asset class (not a shock, not the model's symbol) gets the same notional, leverage and stop; it exits when the model exits or by its own stop/target/liquidation. Per-class comparisons are reported descriptively.
- **Evaluation (unchanged criterion):** nothing concluded before 30 closed pairs; then edge candidate only if mean model return > 0, mean paired difference vs control > 0 and bootstrap p(diff <= 0) < 0.10 (2000 resamples, seed 20261123), and even then a second non-overlapping window is required. Every shown candidate is also scored at +1h/+1d vs SPY. Report ending equity, worst drawdown, results at higher cost and per class; "not found" is the expected result unless the numbers say otherwise.
- **Data limits:** Yahoo quotes for stocks/ETFs/futures/FX (FX and futures can be delayed and FX has no volume), Binance for crypto. Leverage can wipe the account in minutes; an all-in outcome is a valid result. No conclusion is drawn from a single gain, a top-up or a depleted account.

# LLM trader (paper): DeepSeek v4.1 flash 1017 decides, the system supplies data and enforces limits
Locked 2026-11-23 before the first decision. Simulated money only (5,000 THB = USD at the live rate). No real orders, no keys in code.
Question: do the model's buy choices on large-cap shocks beat a same-time random control?
Decider: `openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` (through the opencode CLI, or the OpenRouter API when OPENROUTER_API_KEY is set). One decision cycle (bull call, bear call, decision call) when new shocks appear (candidates = up to 5 stocks with |move vs previous close| >= 5% and day volume >= 0.5x its 50-day average, market open, fresh quote) and one position review per hour while a position is open; minimum 3 minutes between calls, 80 decision cycles per UTC day.
Pipeline (adapted from the TradingAgents research framework, https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents, which we did not install or copy): (1) context from our own data; (2) a BULL and a BEAR research call give the strongest case for and against buying each candidate; (3) the decision call sees both cases plus its own track record; (4) risk limits are enforced by code, not by a model. Not adopted: separate analyst agents for fundamentals/social media (no clean point-in-time data for them here), a portfolio-manager model (the code limits do that job), and any claim about its published results (the project itself says results are not guaranteed).
Track record: the last 10 decisions that have a +1 day result are shown to the model with the stock's SPY-relative return, as the TradingAgents memory log does.
Scoring of every candidate: each stock shown to the model is scored afterwards at +1h and +1d against SPY, whether the model bought or passed. This gives many more observations than the trades alone and is reported as a descriptive check (mean SPY-relative +1d return of bought vs passed); it does not replace the paired-trade criterion below.
Input to the model: time, SPY move, VIX, account (equity, positions with hold time, limits), and for each candidate: price, move, move vs SPY, volume ratio, linked headlines (archive headlines with lexicon score and AI scores when available, else live headlines).
Output: JSON only. Code accepts buys only for candidates it was shown, sells only for held positions, size 0-5% of equity (a size under 0.5% is ignored), and clips every number. Headline text is untrusted data; the model has no tools and cannot touch anything except proposing paper trades.
Enforced by code, not by the model: 5 positions max, 30% total exposure, 8% stop, forced exit after 5 days, no new buys after a 3% loss in a UTC day or while paused, stale or closed-market quotes skipped, long-only, costs from the engine (0.05% fee + 0.02% slippage + simulated spread).
Control: when the model buys, a random stock with |move| < 5% is bought at the same time with the same dollar size, stop and exit time (it is sold when the model sells or at the forced exit), in a separate account.
Logs (append-only `data/llm_trader_log.jsonl`): every call with the full context sent, raw reply, parsed decisions and rejected items; every decision with its stated reason; orders, skips with reason, exits, and closed pairs.
Evaluation (locked): nothing may be concluded before 30 closed pairs. Then edge candidate only if mean model return > 0, mean paired difference vs control > 0 and bootstrap p(diff <= 0) < 0.10 (2000 resamples, seed 20261123); even then it needs a second non-overlapping window. Otherwise "no edge shown". Reasons given by the model are for audit, not evidence.
Limits and honest expectations: LLM judgement cannot be back-tested (its training data overlaps the past), so only forward results count; quotes are Yahoo, possibly delayed; few shocks per day means months to reach 30 pairs; the model may be nondeterministic and the provider may change it (model name stored per call). Earlier research found no edge from buying after big news, so "no edge shown" is the expected result.

## Protocol v2 (2026-11-23, before any live decision; replaces the earlier fixed rules where they differ)
Owner direction: no fixed holding period (scalp to months, whatever makes money), the whole market not a short list, near-real-time operation, and it must run without any personal computer (Kuber only).
- Holding period: chosen by the model per trade (horizon label, stop 1-25%, optional target up to +300%, own review time 5 minutes to 7 days). Code keeps only a 180-day safety exit, a daily 3% loss halt, max 8 positions, 5% size, 40% exposure. The control account is sold when the model sells (same horizon by construction).
- Data speed: a measurement on 2026-11-23 showed Yahoo US quotes are only ~1-6 s old (median 6 s, worst ~40 s for thin names), so the old "delayed ~15 min" caveat was wrong. Universe scan every 10 s (104 stocks in ~3 s, no rate-limit errors in a 4-round test), open positions re-quoted every 2 s, social-buzz scan every 2 min, news every 30 s; on source failure it backs off 30 s. Horizons under ~30 minutes remain unreliable (quote jitter, spread).
- Evaluation is unchanged (30 closed pairs, bootstrap p < 0.10, then a second window). Because horizons now vary, the descriptive scoring of every shown candidate at +1h/+1d is the main extra evidence; it is not a substitute for the paired criterion.
- Candidates stay the large-cap shocks until the buzz event study has produced a size-matched control source for small caps; adding buzz candidates before that would compare small-cap trades with large-cap controls, which is not a fair test.

## Protocol v3 (2026-11-24, before the next live decision): the model decides everything
Owner direction: the model may decide leverage, entry price, size, number of positions and everything else; "no restrictions".
- Removed our own limits: no cap on position count (technical 100), no cap on size (margin up to all equity), no daily loss halt, no risk-per-trade shrinking, no total exposure rule. The model picks leverage 1-20 per trade, market or limit entry (with a validity window up to 24 h), stop 1-25%, optional target, horizon, review time, and may add to a position it holds (the control account adds the same notional to its own stock) or buy any stock of the 104-stock universe, not only the shown candidates.
- Physical limits that remain (they are simulation realism, not strategy rules): margin/cash (total notional <= 20x equity), a fresh quote from an open market, the engine's liquidation when margin level falls under 50%, a financing charge of 8% a year on the borrowed part (charged to the model and to its control), and a pause only when the account is ruined (< 5% of start).

- Language: the model writes reasons, market view and bull/bear arguments in Thai (tickers, numbers, JSON keys in English). Thai text can be less precise than English; the decision fields are numeric and validated, so only the explanations are affected.
