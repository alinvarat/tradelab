# Wikimedia attention test plan

Written before downloading or inspecting signal and return series. This is a retrospective fixed-rule exercise, with a chronological holdout, not a prospective registration or a blind test of common knowledge.

- Hypothesis: sudden retail attention to Bitcoin creates continued spot buying. A publicly downloadable daily number is too slow to buy the initial reaction, so only test later drift.
- Primary pair: English Wikipedia main article `Bitcoin`, human-classified user traffic across access methods, and BTC-USD. Secondary asset robustness: English `Ethereum` and ETH-USD. Both were chosen as established assets, so results cannot speak to smaller or dead coins. No group of surviving tickers is used as a proxy for all crypto.
- Signal: aggregate views on UTC day D are at least twice the median of the prior 28 calendar days. Require every prior day, do not fill missing traffic or prices. Data from the source are final historical values and can have revisions. Public data are usually delayed; conservatively use UTC open D+3 and never enter until 48 hours have elapsed since the end of day D. The API's actual historical arrival times are unknown. A live implementation must log actual first-seen times and skip late data.
- Trade: spot long with no leverage; buy at the entry open and sell at the open 5 calendar days later. Ignore new signals before the planned exit. In a back-to-back trade, charge both the sale and fresh buy. Assume a whole-account position and zero interest in cash. Test D+4 as a distinct slower entry and 2.00% all-in round-trip cost without changing horizon. Daily adjusted Yahoo crypto OHLC is treated as UTC. If Yahoo cannot be reached, use exchange spot OHLC opens for the same calendar and label the quote currency.
- In the main test, each side charges 0.25% of traded notional (0.10% fee, 0.05% half-spread, 0.10% slippage). Stress: 1.00% per side. Funding and borrowing are zero for unleveraged spot. Tax depends on owner-specific circumstances and is outside this asset-return test. THB conversion, withdrawal and venue-specific minimums are separate from per-trade USD P/L because neither a venue nor a conversion schedule was supplied. Report the cost assumption and sensitivity, and no claim of exact realized costs.
- Periods by entry and exit dates, both must fall inside each window. Development/reference 2018–2020; locked 2021–2025. The holdout subdivisions are 2021–2022 and 2023–2025. Subdivision metrics use the full-window executions and keep only trades whose dates both fall within the subdivision to avoid a fresh start creating new signals. No threshold tuning. Three distinct configurations are planned: primary BTC, delayed BTC, and Ethereum external asset. Cost stress is deterministic revaluation of the same trades. Subperiods and subdivisions are diagnostics of each configuration.
- For every metric period use cost-identical random entries on the same asset and with the same year/month and UTC weekday distribution. The random universe excludes qualifying signal dates and respects the same entry/exit boundaries. Match month and weekday when possible; relax only within the same month if necessary and report relaxation. Generate 10,000 independent matched draws per period with fixed seed 271828. Report null expected mean, raw matched randomization upper-tail p, and family-adjusted max of three times p and its lower numerical bound, capped at 1. Draws can overlap across controls, and signal runs can cluster even after the no-overlap execution rule. Treat p-values as approximate and diagnostic. Record count, yearly frequency, mean/median net return, win rate, worst trade, drawdown on daily close marks, month-end 6× hit count, and best month. Confirm a paper candidate only if ≥30 holdout trades, positive net mean, beats matched random at adjusted p < .05, is positive and beats matched-random in both holdout subdivisions at normal cost, remains positive at stress cost, and delayed and ETH tests both preserve a positive edge vs their own baselines in holdout. A research failure does not imply no relationship on a different latency or horizon.
