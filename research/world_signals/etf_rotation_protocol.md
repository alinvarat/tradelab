# Rotation across four ETF exposures
Freeze before scoring. Use cached actual overlapping US sessions July 2023 to October 2026, GLD, USO, UVXY and BITX, and prices known by the previous US close. Compute each fund's product of the last five open-to-close gross multipliers (excludes overnight split adjustments). If the top product is at least 1.05, buy that fund next open and sell close, ties by symbol. Cash otherwise. Net modeled .25% each way or .50% stress, one FX conversion each way at .5% and fractional shares. Compare 1,000 deterministic same-date uniformly random fund paths. Report isolated 2023–24, 2025–Oct 2026 and a continuous path. Additionally report the hindsight upper bound picking the most profitable fund or cash each session, with same costs. Known history and reused snapshots make this retrospective only.
