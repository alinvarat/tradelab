# FOMC policy-rate surprises — rules recorded before computing returns

- One primary decision: when the roughly one-year-ahead Eurodollar/SOFR futures implied rate (ED4 in SF Fed USMPD statement sheet) falls at least 5 basis points in the Fed's statement window, buy SPY at the first full subsequent US session's open, sell that session's close. Ignore the pre-announcement and immediate reaction entirely. Skip missing rate observations; no imputation, leverage, shorting, or parameter search.
- Timing: statement window ends on the event's recorded New York calendar day and is complete before the next day's full session. The retrospective database reconstructs the observable futures change; its publication date is not a live signal. Real-time/forward logging needs licensed or otherwise available timestamped bid/ask futures data and exact Fed-window definitions. Date alignment uses ETF daily adjusted OHLC from research.events.bars. Secondary fixed asset is QQQ. This is a transfer check, not a selection rule.
- Pre-2021 diagnostic period: 2010–2020. Held-out evaluation: 2021–2025, with 2021–2022 and 2023–2025 diagnostic halves. Historical rule lock is procedural; these dates are public history, not a prospective test. Eligibility and exits must fall inside each evaluation period. Exclude incomplete/missing bars.
- All-in execution budget 0.30% round trip (0.20% commissions/spread, 0.10% slippage); stress 0.90%. No financing/borrow under unlevered long; dollar-to-baht conversion and country-specific tax are account-level, stress applies to execution frictions only. Account assumes USD already available, fractional shares, zero fixed minimum on fills. Treat same-session entry/exit as a single independent trade.
- Compare with 10,000 fixed-seed draws of entry sessions matching ETF, calendar year, quarter and weekday, excluding all known statement sessions mapped to their first subsequent tradable day. Charge identical costs to control. Report one-sided randomization p, sample size, net mean, win rate, event frequency, worst trade, realized trade-close drawdown, and calendar-month 6x reach at closed positions only. Pass requires positive held-out net mean, outperformance at adjusted 5% (4 planned primary signal families in this project, p<=0.0125), positive edge in each held-out half, positive net mean at stress cost and on QQQ, and at least 30 primary held-out events. No bot if this gate fails. ETF exposure is preselected, not a stock-survivor claim.

Implementation audit before final evaluation: legacy `bars()` labels Yahoo timestamps after adding 12 hours. For US session opens that rolled dates forward a day, so recover the date from Yahoo's raw timestamp in America/New_York and regenerate the snapshot. The trigger, holding period and cost gates stayed fixed. Workbook documentation gives ED4 in percentage points, with Eurodollar through 2021 and SOFR from 2022, and states the window runs from 10 minutes before to 20 minutes after release.
