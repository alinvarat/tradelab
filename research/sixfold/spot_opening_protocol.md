# Opening flow: four-hour decision, same-day close
Lock before fetching hourly data. Use October's fixed top 60 universe from spot_november and first 22 full November 2026 UTC days. Observe hours 00–03 UTC, require net rise >= 4%, close at least 75% up the four-hour price range, and taker-buy quote volume >= 60% of total. Choose the highest taker-buy share, ties by symbol. Enter at 04:00 open and exit at that day's last close; uninterrupted 24 bars required for a modeled round trip, without filtering candidates on post-decision data (unavailable entry stays cash; after-entry missing hour/exit valued at zero). Standard base/stress costs and continuous wallet. Random controls use the same active days and all assets with the four signal hours. A partial month and retrospective hypothesis on fresh hourly fields, not a deployment test.
