# Available-strike inverse options transfer — fixed before computing returns

The previous exact-strike BTC study saw one isolated 8× month and no listed contract in many other months. This experiment predefines a different rule that checks whether dropping the exact grid requirement materially helps. BTC 2024–25 is an exploratory re-use of a viewed period. ETH 2024–25 is an unseen asset transfer, and Jan–Oct 2026 is a new historical evaluation period for this option strategy. No outcomes for these contracts were consulted before freezing. Source: Deribit public archived inverse BTC/ETH trades, listing metadata and official settlement indices.

- At 09:00 UTC on day 1, get the latest reported underlying index carried by the prior complete five minutes of Deribit perpetual trades, and the same time seven days earlier. If unavailable or unchanged, stay in cash. Buy Call for rising index and Put for falling index, expiry at 08:00 UTC last Friday of that UTC month.
- Before accessing option prints or expiry outcome, consider only the contracts listed at the decision time, with distance 15% to 25% out of the money. Use the available strike closest in absolute percentage points to a 20% distance, ties choose the further OTM contract. Venue strike discovery uses the expired metadata catalogue if the month lies in its supported range. Otherwise probe every $1,000 BTC or $25 ETH grid strike in the band. This documented grid fallback does not prove the complete universe.
- Use first buy-initiated outright trade in next two hours that meets the contract minimum as a price proxy. Ignore block/combo/liquidation flags. Use trade size as an upper bound, floor units to contract minimum, enforce current balance and per-trade budget; missing print is cash. Pay print plus 15% (stress plus 35%) and Deribit taker fee and delivery fee with cap, and charge 0.25% at each currency conversion. Settle only on official delivery index, no interim mark targets. Work in $147-equivalent cash.
- Score 2024–2025 by asset, and separately score Jan–Oct 2026 by asset. Compute fresh monthly reset hits, loss >=50%, median, and single continuously funded account with contract minimums. Alternative asset and history are not pooled as independent months. Compare fixed, 3,000 random-direction draws using the same strikes, friction and eligible prints per month. Disclose full print/strike coverage and missing depth. No paper deployment without multiple independent 6× months on the new period, beating random, avoiding half-loss more often than target hits, robustness to high costs and repeat evidence outside BTC. Incomplete November excluded.
