# Kalshi Formula 1 winner, qualifying rank signal

Locked before measuring signal returns. Sample a market and API response to inspect the schema. Pricing/returns remain withheld until below rules are implemented. Paper only.

- Fit empirical race win frequency by official main qualifying rank (1–5) using 2014–2024 inclusive. Score 2025 and 2026 through October separately and run a continuous $147 starting wallet with no deposits. Join Kalshi main race events to the official published start and qualifying schedule, then map driver by unique name or unambiguous driver code. Record every missing race. Compare published race winner with Kalshi payout. Do not use starting grid, superseding penalties, or race outcomes for selection.
- At the boundary of the second complete UTC hour before scheduled race start, require qualifying session to be scheduled at least 3 hours earlier, and take the preceding hourly Kalshi yes ask close for each offered top-five qualifier. Choose the candidate with largest empirical probability less estimated all-in entry cost (raw ask + $0.01 + unrounded standard taker fee). Require at least 3 percentage points of modeled edge. Use the following hourly yes ask open for paper entry if it still offers 3 percentage points of modeled edge based on baseline cost. Missing/failed selected entries skip, with no substitution. Report stale/zero volume; require positive volume of prior hour, and cap modeled size to observed entry-hour volume. An hourly candle still does not establish depth or when in the hour the first quote was observed.
- Commit at most 50% of cash including rounded Kalshi taker fee; integer contracts, 0.25% conversion each way once per wallet, no reinvestment before official settlement. Require candidate quoted ask <= $0.32 for upside, compute outcomes after fees with $0.01 baseline and $0.02 stress per contract, retaining selection in stress. Start separate monthly reset accounts for diagnostics only; report monthly targets (3×/6×), no trades, half losses, cash, and settlement across month boundaries. Benchmarks draw uniformly from the same race's executable top-five qualifier contracts subject to the $0.32 ask cap and same costs. Report number of races with eligible candidates and with trades, mean and win rate, and compare the original-case results to 1,000 fixed random controls for after-cost account outcomes.
