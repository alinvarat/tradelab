# Independent transfer check: public Bybit spot-removal announcements

The Binance discovery sample showed several very large spots moving near delistings. This follow-up uses another exchange, after those Binance observations. Some projects can overlap across venues, so remove every Binance example named in the exploratory result (ALPACA, PDA, VIB, WING, BAKE, HIFI, SLF and HYPER), and describe the residual as an exchange transfer test. Bybit prices and strategy outcomes have not been inspected when this rule is recorded.

- Source universe: unauthenticated Bybit announcement catalog (locale en-US, category delistings), publication timestamp `publishTime`. Study 2023–2025, with 2023–2024 as reference and 2025 as the held-out year. Evaluate only unambiguous announcements: title or first description explicitly includes `TOKEN/USDT`, or immediately after “to delist” contains an uppercase ticker or list of uppercase tickers with separators followed by “on”, “spot”, end of title or listed effective date. Exclude announcements labeled derivatives, options, convert or non-spot. Actual spot-pair history must be available and contain a valid trading bar before the timestamp. Deduplicate by symbol and earliest public announcement, never infer the coin from its later price move. Report coverage and indeterminate ticker titles.
- At the first complete UTC day's open after publication, buy the single pair if one approved pair occurred or split equally across pairs if the same announcement lists several. For the monthly simulation, take the earliest unambiguous tradeable announcement each month, breaking simultaneous announcements lexically by URL. Use a seven calendar day max hold, exit on last UTC day of month if earlier. Keep cash if next-day open is unavailable, count failed coverage. No leverage, no re-entry. If a valid open occurs and then a bar disappears before exit, model that position as zero proceeds unless target was completed. Calculate buy at open, conservative target using high crossing 6× of each allocation net of costs and 0.25% buffer (targets fill at 6×), sell other positions at scheduled close. Round trip cost 0.70% (0.35% each, fee/spread/slippage) and 2.00% high. Report post-listing collapse and venue transfer coverage explicitly. Test alternative robustness of same-day close proxy only if evidence of public before the close and data coverage allows it.
- For surviving pre-announcement price coverage, compare with a week exactly 14 days earlier per pair, costs and holding the same except month-boundary trimming to the same length, treating this as descriptive because it may contain other marketplace news. With fewer than 10 verifiable holdout announcements or more than 20% missing candidate history, holdout verdict is data-limited. Do not replace missing Bybit bars with Binance bars for the independent test. Preserve announcement snapshot and prices/coverage errors. No threshold tuning and no real accounts or orders.

Acquisition note: the public catalog and spot klines were reached at `api.bytick.com`. The alternate spot archive host `public.bybit.com` and the article host `announcements.bybit.com` did not resolve in this session. The catalog supplies publication timestamps where present. Cases with no usable price series remain in coverage counts.
