# Trade-count attention before a large price move
Lock one rule before evaluating. Use the same historical Binance daily universe and contiguous liquidity gates as spot_volume. Require median previous trade count of at least 1,000; signal count at least three times its 29-day median; absolute close-to-prior-close return at most 3%. Rank by the largest trade-count ratio. The mechanism is that participation may precede upward demand while close-to-close price is still quiet. Buy at next daily open and sell at the same close. Use original one-wallet costs and same-date random controls; score 3× and 6× and concentration. This is the already studied archive, a new retrospective hypothesis only.
