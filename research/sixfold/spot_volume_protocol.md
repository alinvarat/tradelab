# Spot activity breakout from a quiet week
Predefine before scoring. Use archived Binance pairs including inactive pairs. Every UTC close, require 30 uninterrupted days, past 29 days median quote volume at least $1m and current trade count 1,000. Require the seven closes before the signal to span at most 10% from low to high, today's close at least 8% above yesterday's and in the upper quartile of today's range, and volume at least 3× median. Choose highest volume ratio. Hold from next open to next close without an intraday limit. Use one $147 wallet, no top-ups, .35% per side or 1% stress. Unavailable entry means cash, missing exit after entry means zero. Compare with random liquid available-to-select pairs on the same dates (identical missing entry treatment). Score 2021–23, 2024–25, Jan–Oct 2026 and full path. This is a retrospective rule using a dataset examined for other rules, and any positive result needs a truly untouched period and transaction checks.
