# Sixfold tail test • Kalshi NYC daily maximum temperature

Frozen before retrieving the market result history or calculating strategy returns. Availability/schema probes were limited to Jan 1, 2025. Paper only. This is a public weather forecast mispricing test, with named contract settling on the Central Park reading, not a bins-only calibration exercise.

- Universe: historical KXHIGHNY brackets, exact yes outcomes from Kalshi, all dates in 2024–2025 inclusive. 2024 is a reference year and 2025 is the retrospective lockbox. Record missing event dates and quote coverage. No fitting or selecting thresholds based on betting returns.
- Independent signal at 08:00 New York time on event date: take maximum modeled temperature across Central Park's event-day hourly NCEP GFS values from the Open-Meteo Previous Runs archive, temperature_2m_previous_day2. At 48-hour lead, even the latest hour's forecast existed at least 24 hours before entry. Use a fixed-offset EST climate day (05:00 UTC to following 05:00 UTC) to exclude the hour moving with daylight savings. Use 2022–2023 NOAA Central Park GHCN-D daily TMAX to fit only weather errors (mean and sample standard deviation of observed-minus-model daily highs). Assume normal residuals, using half-degree boundaries around integers including/excluding strike per contract rules. Need 90% complete days in model fit and all 24 hours for any signal. A model YES probability of at least 12% qualifies for quote checking.
- Use the recorded YES ask close in the hour ending 08:00 ET as signal-time offer. Use same day's hour ending 09:00 ask open as a fill proxy. Require a positive trade-volume record in the prior or entry hour to reduce use of stale bars, ask positive and at most $0.15 before fees in the signal hour and at execution. Buy at most one bracket per day, choose the largest forecast probability minus break-even (signal ask + $0.01 slippage + fee before rounding), require at least +3 percentage points. After ranking, if chosen quote has missing or too expensive entry, skip; no lookahead substitution. At actual entry require all-in expected unit cost at most 1/6 both tests. Use $0.01 additive baseline slippage and $0.02 for stress (computed without re-ranking). Charge taker fee rounded up to cent, 0.07 × count × execution price × (1 − execution price), no settlement fee. This models quotes, not market depth. Trade-volume condition from entry hour is ex-post fill screening only. Report candle coverage and draw no guarantees about tradeability from any hit.
- Simulate independent calendar months from $147 in cash, using at most one first qualified entry in each calendar month (all available capital, integer units). Positions settle as $1 each if YES, zero if NO. Credit settlement at its real timestamp if still in the calendar month (New York time); if it settles in next month, excluded from count as a one-month target and listed separately. Do not refill or redeploy during a month. Record hit ≥6x (cash and realized settlement), equity ≤0.5x, median, arithmetic mean, no-trade and quote failures. Show unlevered event outcomes separately and winner payout ranges at modeled fills. Simulated USD with no actual fx/withdrawal; household tax and jurisdictional eligibility untested.
- Matched random benchmark: each active month choose one uniformly random executable qualifying cheaply priced bracket/datetime from the identical scanned, probability-filtered universe for that month. Price limits and cost stress unchanged but no positive model edge requirement; this controls the shared season and filter, and the random draws do not assert independence. Use 5,000 simulations with seed 602325 and upper-tail p for count of sixfold months. Report month-level Wilson 95% interval for hit frequency, with explicit small-sample caution, and the hit/loss counts for each half of 2025. Deployment gate: positive net average, 6x in >50% of twelve lockbox months, severe loss no more than 25%, higher hit count than random at p<=0.05, and stress retains these properties. Any single 6x finish counts as historical possibility only.
- Sources: https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/historical/get-historical-markets, https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/historical/get-historical-market-candlesticks, https://kalshi.com/fee-schedule, https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api, https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/search/documentation/data-service/, https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/filings/orgrules/23/04/rules042623979.pdf .

Implementation note before calculating returns: the official KXHIGHNY archive query also returns legacy HIGHNY-prefixed events in 2024. The parser accepts both prefixes within this same catalog; the date suffix, contract strikes and actual settlement remain the authoritative fields.

Archive transport: bulk live candlesticks returned no archived markets, and historical single-ticker requests at excessive parallel load returned HTTP 429. The download rate is now bounded, backs off on 429, and saves every successful quote response for resumption. Catalog, dates, forecast rule and qualification are unchanged.

Fee basis and coverage checks: [official published taker formula and rounding](https://kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/regulatory/notices/Kalshi%20Fee%20Schedule%20Filing%20for%20Posting.pdf). [Archived trade documentation](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/historical/get-historical-trades), checked against three specific candle windows in `tail_coverage_probe.json`. Archive prints can corroborate activity and do not replace missing executable offer prices. The public archive advertised a 50-token cost and roughly a quarter-second replenishment for one candle query, and the final acquisition uses one new request every 0.27 seconds with backoff after a retryable error.
