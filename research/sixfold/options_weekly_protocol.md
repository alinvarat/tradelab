# Weekly BTC and ETH option cadence — record before acquiring weeklies

Test the previously recorded lack of monthly entries. Use fresh weekly strikes and trade-tape observations from 2025 and Jan–Oct 2026, then score the periods independently. Prior long-dated option outcomes and broad market history were already seen. The weekly outcomes were not consulted before this cutoff, but this is a retrospective study, not a live forward trial.

- Check every Monday 09:00 UTC with Friday settlement at 08:00 UTC in the same calendar month. Read the latest index value from Deribit trades before the decision, versus the same time seven days previously. Choose Calls for up and Puts for down, cash if ties or unavailable. Scaling 20% for monthly options by square-root time gives roughly 8% for a four-day position. Choose an already listed inverse option closest to 8.5% OTM among listed 6–11% OTM strikes. Ties farther OTM. No second strike choice if the first has no valid entry.
- Use the first unflagged outright buy print in two hours, at or above contract minimum, plus 15% base or 35% stress and official-model fees and conversion. Cap the hypothetical order at that print's size and current cash. Reinvest available cash across weeks. From 15 minutes after the entry, use the first sufficient observed sell-aggressor print that could meet the calendar-month cash target after 5% baseline or 15% stress sale haircut and costs, otherwise use official expiry settlement. No overlapping trades or margin. Retain the same safety assumption as previous expiry/early-closure experiments: prints verify prices and quoted amounts but not the historical queue.
- Set 3× minimum or 6× stretch relative to that month's cash at start, separately. Stop entering for the month after realizing the target. No intra-period tuning. Calculate fresh monthly $147 resets and a continuous account incorporating order minimums, severe losses (half or worse) and random-direction control. Random control flips direction on the same observed dates with the same selection, constraints and costs; individual months are not assumed independent across BTC/ETH. Do not claim serial repeatability from overlapping market events or a sparse sample.
