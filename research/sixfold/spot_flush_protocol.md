# Daily sell-off with intraday recovery
Predeclare one rule before evaluating. Use the same 30-day continuous history and liquidity gate as spot_volume. Require the signal daily low to be at least 20% below the previous close, the signal close to remain at least 8% below the previous close, and to finish in the upper half of its own range. Require quote volume at least twice its 29-day median. Rank by largest low-to-prior-close decline. Buy next open, sell same-day close. Reuse continuous wallet, 0.35% and 1% per-side cost, same-date random pairs and locked retrospective periods. No reusing outcomes as independent evidence. Both 3× and 6× monthly counts.
