# Intraday sell imbalance rebound
Freeze before scoring. Use already archived July–November hours, same prior-month fixed 60 pairs. After a UTC hour with price falling at least 8% from open to low, recovering into the upper half of the range, and taker-buy quote volume no more than 40%, buy the highest volume qualifying symbol at the next hour open and sell two hours later at that hour's close (two bars of exposure). Take only the first qualifying hour per UTC day, ending before midnight. No substitute on missing entry. Value a missing second held bar at zero. Random control selects a pair with a valid signal-hour bar and positive volume at the same decision hour; identical fill and costs. Record all full months, incomplete November, one continuous wallet, baseline and stress. The hourly data were used for other rules, so this is retrospective.
