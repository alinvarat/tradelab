# Weekly rotation in established Binance spot pairs — protocol before computing strategy outcomes

Question: can selection among high-variance liquid USDT pairs compound a 5,000 THB paper account to 30,000 THB within a month, while preserving capital across months? This differs from buying on first listing day and from Hunter's large-coin leveraged trend. No real orders.

- Universe from Binance's public S3 monthly spot-kline symbol prefixes; use archived rather than current tradable pairs, so obsolete/delisted symbols can be included. Use actual daily OHLC, trade count and quote volume from Binance spot REST, with alternative official host and published monthly archive fallback. Exclude stable/fiat and exchange-issued leveraged tokens defined in script. Retain trading pairs after renames as distinct symbols; use only the observed history since pair start. Data begin 2020-01-01; continue through 2026-10-31 UTC. Any remaining coverage gaps are reported. Symbol series enter the universe only with 30 consecutive prior complete bars as of signal, at least 1,000 prints on the signal day and median 30-day quote volume at least 1 million USDT. No information about future returns or availability is used to rank.
- At Sunday UTC close, select one eligible coin. **M (continuation):** close is at least the preceding 29-day closing maximum, prior seven-day price gain ≥20%, and quote volume ≥2× its preceding 29-day median. Rank by prior seven-day gain (descending, alphabetic tie). **R (reversal):** close is at least 20% below seven days before, latest day close is above prior close, and latest day volume ≥2× preceding 29-day median. Rank by most negative seven-day gain, then symbol. Buy at Monday UTC open, whole account, unlevered. Only one primary implementation per hypothesis, plus fixed cost/latency tests. If no candidate or opening trade missing, hold cash for that slot. No automatic replacement.
- Sell at next scheduled weekly open or UTC month boundary (the latter uses last daily close and the appropriate exit friction). Target is net realizable equity 6× starting equity, checked inside each valid bar using high. Both purchase and sale incur 0.35% of notional per side (fees, spread, slippage), stress 1.00%. If high only just reaches the target price, require a further 0.25% of price as fill buffer. Fill at target, not at high. A missing bar once holding is assigned zero proceeds unless an exit was already observed. A delayed robustness version enters Tuesday, keeps that week's original exit boundary, and uses the fixed Sunday signal. No short, stop loss, funding, or leverage. Capital is a USD/USDT proxy, before local tax or costs of funding and withdrawing.
- Every month also simulated independently starting 1× for a fair comparison of months. Once 6× is reached and sold, stay in cash for that month. Report hit frequency, month-end loss ≥50% or ≥95%, median, annual splits, buy count, best/worst month, daily close equity and independently chained (no deposits) monthly mult. A second diagnostic of feasibility counts whether any eligible symbol in each weekly slot had a hypothetical single-position 6× during that slot, with foresight. This is only an opportunity count, separate from strategy results.
- Compare with 2,000 fixed-seed random strategies for held-out months using each rule's same signal dates and uniformly sampled point-in-time eligible pairs, with same cost and exit logic. Dates still have the timing of the original signals, assets are randomized. Include buy-and-hold BTC starting at the first UTC open through month's close with same target/fees.
- Time windows: reference 2021–2023, holdout 2024–2025 split by year, forward-in-calendar stability check Jan–Oct 2026 (also retrospective). Do not use outcomes to change M/R thresholds. Predeclared gate for a repeatable paper candidate: reach 6× in at least half of the 24 held-out months, downside ≥50% in fewer than one in ten months, exceed random-asset selection at one-sided p ≤ .025 (two primary rules), upside more than downside in each held-out year, and retain at least half-month hit rate at high cost and after a one-day delay. This demanding screen is a research filter, not a promise of monthly success. Occasional verified historical hits will be reported even if the screen fails.

Post-result execution audit stays separate from selection. Re-check reported target hits using official archived one-minute bars, then query the first minute that clears the fill buffer for its recorded Binance aggregate trades. Buyer taker volume at or above target is evidence of market activity, while actual fill sequence and resting order queue remain unobserved. Binance short REST queries can return empty for older trades and may truncate at 1,000 aggregate rows.
