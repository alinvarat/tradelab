# Early exit on observed Deribit option sales

The settled-at-expiry results are known. Freeze this new exit rule before downloading the post-entry tape. Reuse the market, strikes, direction, and entry rules from options_transfer. The 2024–2025 period is a comparison, and 2026 Jan–Oct is a separate historical transfer whose early-exit prints were not inspected when specifying this rule. This is not a forward paper trial.

- Preserve 3× (minimum) and 6× (stretch) as separate policies. From 15 minutes after the entry print and strictly before expiry, check chronological public option prints with sell aggressor, excluding flagged block, combo, and liquidation trades. Require a single print with volume at least the entire held position. Use its reported index price, discount the per-unit coin price by 5%, apply the same API instrument taker fee and premium cap, then reduce USD proceeds for the same conversion cost. Under stress use +35% to buy and a 15% sale discount. Exit on first sale whose proceeds plus spare cash reach the target relative to the account balance at the start of that calendar month. Otherwise settle at expiry. The recorded trade is a proxy for executable price and size, with no order-book or queue information. Credit at most the target on early exit to avoid granting a larger jump above the limit.
- Use complete time-range acquisition. Recursively split a response with has_more until the result fits, and fail rather than assume missing prints. Preserve raw qualifying sale observations and archive endpoints.
- Recompute both monthly resets and a continuous account with exchange order minimums and printed size. Compare to the same-expiry-only result and 3,000 random-direction draws with identical entry and exit mechanics. No deployment if fresh months lack repeated target hits or severe loss is more frequent than target hits. Treat threefold as minimum and still assess the sixfold goal.
