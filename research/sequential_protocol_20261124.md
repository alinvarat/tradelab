# Daily sequential evidence (daily-v1, locked 2026-11-24)

Each study reports separate e-processes for the model's net return and the model minus its matched random control. Bound each leg's net return to [-200%, +300%] before the subtraction and before averaging by UTC day. Missing controls are excluded from the paired series. The model series uses every otherwise eligible model outcome. Only completed UTC days count. Each of the two directions uses alpha 0.05 per reported series and threshold 20, with the predictable bet and first crossing recorded by core/sequential.py. The series test bounded daily means under conditional-mean nulls. Days can share market exposure, particularly with overlapping holding periods. We make no independence claim or pooled significance claim across protocols.

A positive interim signal requires a positive crossing in both series. A paired negative crossing is an interim futility signal. These signals are recorded alongside each locked final verdict and do not change entries, exits, position sizes, or the final criteria. Continue to report uncapped returns, matched-control results and high-cost checks under the original protocols.
