# Social-buzz event study on a broad universe (locked 2026-11-23, before the first event)
Owner direction: drop the 3x-per-month target and the narrow large-cap list; follow social/news attention across the whole market. This is analysis with simulated money only; nothing is bought or sold here. Earlier project research found no edge from buying after attention or news, so "no difference shown" is the expected result.
Source: ApeWisdom public tracker of Reddit/social mentions (all pages, ~750 US tickers, with each ticker's mention count now and 24 h ago). Universe and market caps: Nasdaq stock screener list (names, market cap; its prices are NOT used, they lagged badly in a spot check). Prices: Yahoo quotes.
Scan: every 10 minutes. Buzz event: mentions >= 20 and >= 3x the 24-hour-ago count (floor 5), the ticker is a US-listed stock with market cap >= $50M, quote price >= $1 and day dollar volume >= $1M; one event per ticker per UTC day, max 30 new events per scan.
Ticker hygiene (added before the first event after a live dry run showed word collisions such as SO, YOU, AM, HR counted as company buzz): tickers that are ordinary English words are excluded by a fixed list in core/buzz.py (WORD_TICKERS). The list is only extended, never trimmed to fit results.
Per event: record mentions/rank/upvotes, Yahoo price/move/volume, market-open flag, up to 3 headlines, SPY price, and a random liquidity-matched control stock (market cap within 0.5x to 2x, liquid by the same filter).
Outcomes: price at +1h, +1d, +3d after the event (only when the market is open and the quote is within 6 hours of the horizon; otherwise left empty, never filled with a stale price), as return minus SPY's return over the same interval, for the event stock and its control.
Evaluation (locked): primary horizon +1d. Nothing may be concluded before 100 events with a +1d result. Then the paired difference (event minus control) is bootstrapped (2000 resamples, seed 20261123, 95% interval): "events beat controls" only if the interval is above 0 (and it still needs a later non-overlapping window), "events lag controls" if below 0, otherwise "no difference shown". +1h and +3d are secondary.
Why this before any trading rule: events are far more numerous than trades, so the question is answered sooner, with a control that fixes market-wide moves and size.
Not done here, by design: trading on these events, sentiment scoring of posts (the source gives counts, not text), pump-and-dump filtering beyond the liquidity floor. Illiquid micro caps are excluded because paper fills there would be fiction (spreads of several percent).
Limits: ApeWisdom is a third-party summary of Reddit and may change or vanish; counts include bots and repeated posts; Yahoo quotes can lag ~15 minutes; the first weeks will fall in one market regime.

Addendum 2026-11-24 (before any +1d result): a control may never be another share class of the same company (the first live event GOOGL was matched with GOOG). Fixed in code by comparing the first word of the company names; events already recorded before the fix keep their original control. Everything else is unchanged.
