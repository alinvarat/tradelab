# Addendum Q1-0a to protocol_wallet_follow_probe_20261127.md, locked 2026-11-27 (before the second probe run)

## What the first probe run (Actions run 45860797473, 22 windows of the bare global feed) showed
- The bare trade feed `/v2/trades?start=&end=` **ignores start and end**: all 22 windows returned the same 3,000 newest rows (0 new wallets after the first window). The frame was 27 wallets, all recent. The OpenAPI text says the `condition` and `event_id` shapes serve a fixed three-year window, the bare feed only the current and previous month, and `start`/`end` are honored on the `user` shape only. The first-run frame is **void** (a fetcher assumption was wrong, not a data result); no wallet-level count from it is used. The routes, rate and Hyperliquid parts of that run are kept.
- Routes: `/v2/positions/snapshot` serves only closed UTC days from 2025-12-31 (400 for 2025-06-01), so it cannot give a ranking date earlier than 2025-12-31. `/v2/user-pnl` and `/v2/user-stats` answer but are as-of-today (excluded by section 1 of the protocol). `/v2/trades?user=` defaults to `taker_only=true` (maker rows excluded).
- Rate: 438 calls, 5 HTTP 429 (all retried), 1 error, 0.43 s per call.
- Hyperliquid (Kill rule of protocol section 3, read as written): the leaderboard has 46,756 rows but is as-of-today; `userFillsByTime` from 2025-01-01 returned exactly 2,000 rows for 19 of 20 sampled wallets and the earliest fill was at most 16 months back (0 of 20 before 2025-06-01); `recentTrades` carries wallet addresses but only the last 10 trades. **Result: no point-in-time wallet frame and no depth to the first split: the Hyperliquid arm is not runnable point-in-time.** It is closed by that locked rule.

## Change (one dated fix, one re-run)
The frame is rebuilt from the **condition feed**: for each of the 22 months (2025-01 .. 2026-10) one gamma keyset page of resolved markets ending in the month with volume >= 50,000 USD (up to 100), keeping binary markets with a condition id, ordered by sha256("q1-wallet-probe-markets-20261127" + conditionId), first 10 per month (at most 220 markets). The market sample reads the final volume of a market, which is not known on its start date; it reads no outcome and no wallet, and it is used only to size the frame in this probe. Trades: `/v2/trades?condition=` up to 5 pages of 1,000 rows (newest first); a wallet joins with any row of notional >= 500 USD. Everything else (sampling of 200 wallets by sha256, 25-page user histories, qualification, kill 0a and 0b rules, saturation rule) is unchanged. Added counts: per month the listed and used markets, rows, pages and the min and max trade timestamps (to check the feed is the right period); for 20 wallets the row count with `taker_only=false` against the default.
