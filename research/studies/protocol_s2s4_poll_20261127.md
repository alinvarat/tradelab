# S2 and S4 long polls: protocol (locked 2026-11-27, before the first poll)
Parent: `research/studies/RESULTS.md` "S1-S5 stage-0 probes: pre-registration" (rules unchanged) and its results section. This file only fixes how the plan's 3-day and 48-snapshot windows are run on GitHub Actions and how the pre-registered kills are applied to them. Counts only; no price after a signal is read; not a family (k stays 60). Code: `research/studies/s15_long.py`, verdict arithmetic `core/s15_eval.py` (offline tests `tests/test_s15.py`), workflow `.github/workflows/s2s4_poll.yml`. Data branches `data/s2-poll`, `data/s4-poll`.

## S2 (GeckoTerminal trending list, 72 hours)
- Poll `trending_pools?duration=1h` pages 1 and 2 every 30 s (one retry per page after 5 s). A poll counts as answered only if both pages answered; every poll, answered or void, is a line in `polls.jsonl`. Covered time = the sum of gaps between consecutive answered polls that are <= 120 s.
- Entrant: a pool never seen in any earlier answered poll of the whole collection (the very first poll is the baseline). A pool that leaves the list and returns is counted as a re-entry in `state.json` and used for nothing.
- Delay of an entrant = its first-sight time minus the previous answered poll, so void polls lengthen it honestly.
- Qualifying = computed round trip <= 3% (constant-product, 49 USD, 2 fees + 2 impacts, as pre-registered).
- Sells check: for each qualifying entrant, at most 2 `trades` calls per 30 s cycle in first-seen order; an entrant not checked within 10 minutes stays "not measured". An entrant whose 300-trade page does not reach back 60 minutes is "truncated" and also not measured. No exit = fewer than 2 distinct selling wallets in the 60 minutes before first sight.
- Window: ends 72 hours after the first poll. Verdict is read only when covered time >= 64.8 hours (or the window has ended) and answered polls >= 80%; otherwise the status is RUNNING or VOID (coverage), never a kill.
- Kills, applied at the end of the window with the plan's numbers: median delay > 60 s; more than 20% of the measured qualifying entrants with no exit (at least 10 measured); **qualifying entrants a month = qualifying / covered hours x 730 below 100** (the three-day window replaces the single-window upper-bound rule used in the 18-minute probe). Otherwise "survives stage 0": one forward protocol (plan S2 "Then", bar 240) is next.

## S4 (Kalshi x Polymarket, 48 hourly snapshots)
- One snapshot at minute :10 of each hour (at least 3,500 s after the previous one) using the pre-registered matcher unchanged (`core/s15.py` `features`, `pair_match` at 60 minutes), Kalshi `mve_filter=exclude`, 48 hour window, Polymarket gamma top 2,000 by 24 h volume (gamma refuses deeper offsets). Fees: Kalshi series `fee_type` and `fee_multiplier` (quadratic only; others excluded and counted), Polymarket markets with fees enabled excluded and counted. A failed snapshot is a logged void; 48 answered snapshots complete the window; verdict is read at >= 44.
- Locked return of a pair: 20 USD at the top of both books, best of the two opposite-leg combinations after fees; the one-instant allocation yield is the greedy best 147 USD allocation (at most 20 USD a pair, net lock per capital per day to resolution, floor 0.25 day). A snapshot with no lockable pair has yield 0.
- Daily yield for the kill = the mean of the snapshot yields over the window. Pairs a day = distinct (Kalshi ticker, Polymarket id) pairs ever matched / (snapshots / 24). Mismatch share = distinct pairs with both sources named and different / distinct matched pairs.
- Kills: complete if mean yield < 0.5% a day, pairs a day < 10, or (>= 10 pairs) more than 20% mismatched sources; as a standalone if mean yield < 3.73% a day (then it can only be a component). Near-miss counts (same entity, level and direction within 24 h) are reported for context and change nothing.
