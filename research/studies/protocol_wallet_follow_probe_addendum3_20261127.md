# Addendum Q1-0c to protocol_wallet_follow_probe_20261127.md, locked 2026-11-27 (before the probe2 run)

New counts-only mode `probe2` (no P&L, no returns): the follow rule needs a market price **60 s after the wallet's trade**, for trades up to 22 months old. The OpenAPI text says `/v2/prices-history` keeps 1-minute data for at least 7 days and 5-minute for at least 60 (floors), only the 3-hour series is permanent, and explicit windows cap at 15 days; the condition tape serves three years but pages newest-first and ignores `start`/`end`. The probe therefore tests (1) whether a condition-feed cursor with the `ts` anchor changed (an opaque cursor, decoded and re-encoded, same public endpoint, no authentication involved) seeks the tape to a past time: 12 sample markets (2 per sampled month from the outcome-blind market sample, every 3rd month of the 22), 4 cursor variants each (`same` sq, `sq` 2^63-1, `sq` dropped, `sq` 999999999999999999), target = a trade found 4 pages deep plus 120 s; reported per variant: HTTP 200 share, share whose page contains the reference trade, share whose first row is within 300 s of the target; and (2) what `/v2/prices-history` serves for 24-hour windows of closed markets (60 s and 3 h buckets), by age.

Decision fixed now (used by the Q1 test protocol): if a cursor variant returns HTTP 200 and its page contains the reference trade in at least 10 of 12 tests, the delayed fill is the **first tape trade at least 60 s after the wallet's trade, in the same token, plus the 1-cent rule**. If no variant does, the delayed price cannot be observed for past trades and the standalone follow rule is **not testable point-in-time with a 60 s delay**: the study is then closed as "not testable", and any test that uses the wallet's own price plus a flat 2 cents is written as a separate post-hoc screen, never as a pass.
