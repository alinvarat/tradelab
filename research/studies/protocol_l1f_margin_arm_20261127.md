# Protocol: L1f margin arm, forward paper test, locked 2026-11-27 (before its first trade)

Origin: `RESULTS.md` section "L1f short venues" (2026-11-27). The perp-reachable L1f notices fail stage 0; the edge sits in the coins with no Binance perp, which can only be
shorted through Binance cross margin, and borrowability at the notice cannot be verified backwards. This arm records it going forward. Not a result until the e-process below decides.
Supersedes the proposal in `protocol_l1f_venues_addendum_20261127.md` (same idea, now with the recorder). The locked L1f / event-book / venues protocols are not edited.

## Recorder (pod, `core/margin_arm.py`, `margin_step` in `core/market_collect.py`; no API key, no signed endpoint, no real order)
- Events: the same fresh Binance "Will Delist" notices as the L1f forward ledger (catalog 161, `core.screens.binance_delist_symbols`, polled every 30 s, a notice older than 15 minutes at first sight is not an event). Every notice is recorded as an event row with its snapshot whether or not it is traded.
- Snapshot at first sight, from the public unauthenticated `https://www.binance.com/bapi/margin/v1/friendly/margin/vip/spec/list-all`: is the coin in the list (borrowable now), its VIP 0 daily interest rate and VIP 0 borrow limit (coins); also whether a Binance USDT-M perp exists now (`fapi` book ticker, symbol or 1000/1000000 prefix; the flag, not a filter: both groups are paper-traded and reported apart).
- Trade, if the coin is in the list AND limit x entry bid >= the stake: short at the Binance spot bid (`/api/v3/ticker/bookTicker`, COINUSDT) 60 s after first sight; buy back at the ask 4 h later, or earlier at the first poll where the ask is 25% or more above the entry bid (stop). Not in the list, no spot book, or limit below the stake: skip, recorded with the reason (the skip rate is a result).
- Sizing: 20% of a separate 147 USD ledger per event, no leverage (as L1f). Result = raw short return + BTC mid return (BTC-adjusted as L1f), minus fees 0.2% (0.1% a side) minus the MEASURED borrow cost: mean of the entry and exit snapshots' daily rate x (ceil(hold hours) + 1) / 24 (interest accrues per started hour; the exit snapshot falls back to the entry rate if its read fails). No other assumed cost: the entry and exit use the real bid and ask.
- Heartbeat: one row every 30 minutes (pending, closed, traded, equity). Rows are appended to `margin_arm/<UTC day>.jsonl` on the pod volume and copied by `core/margin_publish.py` every 30 minutes to the append-only branch `data/l1f-margin` (`margin_arm/<day>/<start>-<end>.jsonl`); it does nothing without `TRADELAB_DATA_PUSH_TOKEN`.

## Decision (fixed now)
`core.sequential.EProcess(lo=1.0, hi=5.0)` on one value per UTC day of exit (as the L1f ledger). Edge at e-value 20; no edge when the negative side reaches 20; no verdict before 30 traded notices. Two reports beside it, no extra tests: the traded notices whose coin has no Binance perp now (the 42-coin group of the venues study) and the rest, and the skip share. The margin upper bound of the venues study (mean +24.1%, x 2.23 on 39 coins, spot-priced with an assumed 0.1% borrow) is the expectation to beat; the realised value after measured borrow and the real bid/ask replaces it. At 1.15 such notices a month, 30 traded notices take about two years: the arm is a slow check, not a 3x-a-month engine; it is kept because it costs nothing to run.
