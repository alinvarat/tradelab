# News latency forward test: can fast Haiku, many in parallel, trade news? (locked 2026-11-26, before the first collection)

Owner question (2026-11-26): "Can't we just use fast Haiku, many in parallel, to trade news?" This test measures the answer forward instead of arguing it. Simulated money and public data only; no orders. It is **not** the 2026-11-24 `research/news_latency/protocol.md` (that one prices a random control at +1/+5 min after the archive's first sight of a large-cap headline, with no LLM and no feed timestamps; it stays as it is). This test adds the feed's own publish time, the Haiku classification, a live price at the decision, and the whole 60-minute path.

Code: `core/news_latency_fwd.py` (pure logic, `tests/test_news_latency_fwd.py`), `research/studies/news_latency_fwd.py` (IO), workflow `news_latency_fwd.yml`, keeper wiring. Data branch `data/news-latency-fwd` (titles only, no article bodies). Collection starts only when this file and its hash are on `main`; `run` refuses to start otherwise and also checks the two files below.

## Reachability probe (Actions runs 45754281422 and 45754840324, before this protocol, no outcome data read)
Reachable and parsed from an Actions runner: SEC getcurrent 8-K atom (100 items, second resolution), Binance announcements catalogs 48/49/161 (`www.binance.com/bapi`, ms), OKX `support/announcements` (ms), RSS of CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt, The Block, Bitcoin Magazine, CryptoSlate (second resolution), PR Newswire (two feeds; second field but always `:00`, so treated as minute resolution), GlobeNewswire (minute resolution), SEC ticker map (5,999 CIKs), OKX swap list (539 live perps), OKX 1-minute candles and ticker (bid/ask), Yahoo 1-minute chart (65 of 65 bars for AAPL in the last session; snapshot `regularMarketPrice` with its own time). Not usable: Business Wire feed (0 items, dropped); GlobeNewswire's earnings-only feed duplicates the main feed (dropped). Local clock minus OKX server time at the probe: +59 ms.
The binance and OKX announcement feeds are slow (the newest items were 1 to 33 days old): they will contribute few items; that is a finding, not a defect.

## What is recorded for every new item
`t_pub` (source time; resolution s or m recorded), `t_seen` (local clock when the poll response was received), `t_text_ready` (SEC filings only, after the exhibit fetch), `t_llm_start`, `t_llm` (the `claude` CLI call returned), Haiku `direction` (up/down/none), `confidence` (0-100), `instrument`, served model string, any error, a live price snapshot at `t_llm + 5 s` (crypto: OKX ticker last/bid/ask and its own time; stock: Yahoo `regularMarketPrice` and its own time), and the 1-minute bars of the instrument from `t_pub - 5 min` to `t_pub + 61 min` (OKX `-USDT-SWAP`; Yahoo with pre/post), fetched at `t_pub + 62 min`; missing bars stay empty, never filled.
Polling: one long job (about 5 h 45 min) loops every 10 s (SEC), 15 s (Binance, OKX), 30 s (RSS), restarts itself and is checked by `keeper.yml` every 30 minutes. Items first seen on the first poll of a source in a run are `cold_start`: stored, never scored, never in any statistic. State is kept on the data branch between runs.
Limits stated now: `t_seen - t_pub` has a floor of half the poll interval; for minute-resolution sources `t_pub` is truncated so the lag is overstated by 0 to 59 s (reported by resolution); `t_llm` includes `claude` CLI process start and queueing for one of 6 parallel slots (`TRADELAB_CLAUDE_PARALLEL=6`, locked), which is real cost of this route, not model speed; the runner clock is NTP-synced (probe offset +59 ms); Yahoo quotes of thin stocks can be stale (handled by the snapshot age rule below).

## Haiku input and selection
Model `claude-haiku-5-5` through `core.ai_news.chat` only (AGENTS rule 7); one call per item, no batching, no prices in the prompt. An empty or unparseable answer is a logged failure (`llm_err`), never "neutral". The Max-plan token is shared with the production bot, so a hard cap applies per UTC day of `t_pub`: 500 stock items and 400 crypto items scored; later items are stored as `skipped_cap` (latency only). Selection:
- **SEC 8-K:** instrument = ticker of the filer's CIK from the SEC ticker map (Nasdaq, NYSE, NYSE American, Cboe; plain 1-5 letter tickers). Scored only if the filing lists at least one of items 1.01, 1.02, 2.01, 2.02, 2.05, 3.01, 4.02, 5.02, 7.01, 8.01 (or lists none) and `t_pub` is inside 09:35-15:00 America/New_York on a weekday. Haiku reads the company, the items list and the first 2,500 characters of the EX-99 exhibit (else the first document); the fetch time counts inside `t_llm - t_seen`.
- **PR Newswire, GlobeNewswire:** instrument = the ticker in "(NASDAQ: X)", "(NYSE: X)", "(NYSE American: X)" in the title or summary; no ticker, no scoring. Same trading-hours rule. Haiku reads title and summary.
- **Crypto sources (Binance, OKX, 6 RSS feeds):** Haiku reads title and summary and names the coin; it is tradable only if `<COIN>-USDT-SWAP` is a live OKX perp (list refreshed every 30 min), otherwise `no_instrument`. Market-wide news maps to BTC by the prompt.
- Stocks are further required to have at least 3 of the 5 one-minute bars before `t_pub` with volume > 0 (a causal liquidity screen).

## Definitions (all computed from stored rows; code in `core/news_latency_fwd.py`)
- Latency: `t_seen - t_pub`, `t_llm - t_seen`, `t_llm - t_pub`: n, median, p90, per source and overall, excluding `cold_start` items; negative values are kept and counted.
- `p0` = close of the last 1-minute bar that ended by `t_pub`; `p60` = close of the last bar that ended by `t_pub + 60 min`; both must be less than 5 minutes stale. Move `M = p60 / p0 - 1`. **Large move: `|M| >= 1.0%`.**
- **Fraction already happened by `t_llm`:** `f = (price_at_llm - p0) / (p60 - p0)` for large-move items, where `price_at_llm` is the snapshot mid (crypto (bid+ask)/2, stock last) at `t_llm + 5 s` when its own timestamp is within 60 s of the request (primary, `f_snap`); sensitivity `f_bar` uses the last bar close before `t_llm`; `f_seen` uses it before `t_seen`. Reported: n, median, quartiles, share with f >= 0.5 and f >= 1. f is signed (below 0 means the price first went the other way).
- **Trade:** only items with Haiku direction up or down and confidence >= 60 (primary), instrument ok, snapshot ok. Entry at `t_llm + 5 s` at the snapshot's side price (buy at ask, sell at bid; stock: last), exit at the `p60` close, i.e. at `t_pub + 60 min`. No entry later than `t_pub + 55 min` (`too_late`). Gross = direction x (p60 / entry - 1). **One open trade per instrument**: later items on the same instrument before the exit are dropped (this is also the dedup of one event seen on several feeds; the earliest `t_llm` wins). Latency statistics are not deduplicated.
- **Costs:** round trip crypto 0.14% (0.05% fee + 0.02% slippage per side, the engine's assumption), stock 0.20% (assumption, thin names cost more); the **2x cost** case is reported. The snapshot side price already pays the spread; the cost is charged on top, which is conservative.
- **Controls under the same entry, exit and cost:** random direction (parity of sha256 of the item key) and flipped direction. Reported: mean net per trade, hit rate, gross, Haiku minus random control with a 95% interval.
- Intervals: bootstrap over UTC days (2,000 resamples, seed 20261126). No compounding account is simulated; this test does not claim a 3x-a-month rate.
- Sensitivities reported, none changes the verdict: confidence >= 80, any confidence, large-move trades only, entry at the first bar open >= `t_llm + 5 s` instead of the snapshot.

## Verdict (per market, crypto and stock separately; never pooled)
- Fewer than **100 trades**: "unread", nothing is concluded; numbers are descriptive.
- **"edge candidate (forward)"** needs all of: >= 100 trades on >= 10 UTC days; mean net per trade > 0 at 1x cost with the lower 95% bound > 0; mean net > 0 at 2x cost; Haiku minus random control lower bound > 0. Anything else with >= 100 trades is "no edge shown".
- Even an edge candidate is only a forward observation: it needs a later non-overlapping window before any rule trades it, and it would still have to survive execution (the 5 s snapshot is a quote, not a fill, and no size or queue is modelled).

## Reporting
Every report states: counts (items, cold, scored, failed, skipped by reason, excluded by reason), the three latency pairs (median and p90) per source, the move-fraction table for large moves, the P&L tables with controls and 2x cost, the verdict per market, the Haiku calls and tokens (plan usage; USD paid is 0, the CLI's list-price figure is shown as notional) in `reports/COSTS.md`. If a source or price route stops answering, it is reported as unreachable, not replaced.
Any change of a threshold, source list, cap or prompt needs a new dated protocol or addendum before the data it affects.
