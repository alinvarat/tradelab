# Protocol P3: Polymarket liquidity-reward quoting (paid to quote), locked 2026-11-26

Plan: `research/plans/PLAN_20261126b_fable.md`, direction P3. Simulated money and public endpoints only; no keys, no orders, no account. Runs on GitHub Actions only (`poly_rewards.yml`). Code: `poly_rewards_core.py` (pure), `poly_rewards.py` (fetch). No data was read before this file was locked. A rule below changes only by a dated addendum written before the first affected row.

## 1. Facts taken from Polymarket's rewards documentation (docs.polymarket.com, liquidity-rewards page, read 2026-11-26)
- Score of an order: S(v, s) = ((v - s) / v)^2 with v = maximum spread from the midpoint in cents, s = the order's distance from the midpoint in cents; the in-game multiplier is 1 here. Sizes are shares.
- Q_one = YES bids (= NO asks), Q_two = YES asks (= NO bids). Midpoint in [0.10, 0.90]: Q_min = max(min(Q_one, Q_two), max(Q_one, Q_two) / 3). Outside it: Q_min = min(Q_one, Q_two) (two-sided needed).
- The book is sampled once a minute at a random offset; one UTC day is up to 1,440 samples; rewards are paid per maker address at 00:00 UTC. **The minimum payout is 1 USD: smaller amounts are not paid.** Whether that is per market or per maker is not stated; this protocol applies it to the **total daily reward of the whole 147 USD book** (the rule the docs' wording supports) and also reports the figure before it.
- Per market: `rewards.rates[].rewards_daily_rate` (pool, USD a day), `rewards.min_size` (shares), `rewards.max_spread` (cents). Public, no auth: CLOB `/sampling-markets`, `/rewards/markets/multi`, `/book`, `/books`; data API `/v2/trades?condition=`.
- Orders below min_size do not score. The docs mention a "size-cutoff-adjusted midpoint"; this protocol uses the plain midpoint (best bid + best ask) / 2.

## 2. Probe (step 1, no P&L)
`poly_rewards.py probe`: one request per candidate endpoint; records HTTP status and key names only. An endpoint that needs auth is "unavailable", never worked around. If the field mapping in `parse_reward_market` does not match, write an addendum with the real field names before `measure`.

## 3. Measure (steps 2-4)
- **Universe:** all `/sampling-markets` rows with pool > 0, two tokens, accepting orders, not closed. Ranked by pool / (min_size x (1 - 2 x 0.5 v / 100)); the top 300 get a book (YES token; the NO book is its mirror).
- **Competition (conservative):** every resting share in the band 0 <= s < v counts as fully scoring (no min-size filter, no per-maker split); competitors' Q_min is taken from the aggregated Q_one / Q_two, which overstates it. Our share = Q_ours / (Q_ours + Q_comp). Reward per day = pool x share. Today's pool and book are held constant.
- **Quote shapes (all reported, one primary):** distance d = f x v with f in {0.25, **0.5 (primary)**, 0.75}; mode `two` = YES bid at mid - d plus NO bid at 1 - mid - d, i.e. YES ask at mid + d (cost per min-size unit = min_size x (1 - 2d)); mode `single` = the cheaper token's bid only, only for mid in [0.10, 0.90] (scores at 1/3). Quote prices must lie in [0.01, 0.99].
- **Qualifies:** the shape's one-unit cost <= 147 USD and the quote prices are valid. **Best 147 USD allocation:** greedy, one min-size unit at a time, by marginal reward per USD, until 147 USD is spent (sizes are whole multiples of min_size; our own size reduces our share).
- **Decision (step 5):** gross daily % = total reward / 147 for the best shape. `kill_complete` if the best gross < 0.5% a day or fewer than 10 markets qualify (no collector is built); `kill_standalone` if < 3.7299% a day (3^(1/30) - 1); otherwise `standalone_pass`. Anything but `kill_complete` builds the forward collector.
- **Replay (backtest, can kill but cannot confirm):** only if not `kill_complete`. Markets of the primary and best allocations; last <= 10,000 trades per market (data API cursor pages), so coverage is days, not months. There is no historical book, so the mid proxy is the last trade <= 30 min old and today's pool and competition are held constant. Every 3,600 s a two-sided quote at mid +- d of the allocated units; a bid fills **in full** on the first trade in the hour whose yes-equivalent price (`poly_fv.yes_equiv`, side-agnostic) is <= our bid (ask: >=); a filled side stays out to the next refresh; a fill is marked at the last yes-equivalent trade price within 24 h after it (primary; 1 h reported), at the bid: against us by 0.005 per share (`poly_fv.HAIRCUT`; 0.010 reported). Reward per hour = pool x share / 24 with both sides live. Maker fee 0.
- **Reported (each):** days, mean daily net return on the capital deployed, day-clustered (UTC) se and one-sided p (`poly_fv.cluster_stats`), the compound monthly (1 + mean)^30 - 1 and the linear 30 x mean, ending equity from 1.0 and worst drawdown of the daily series, fills; for scenarios base, mark 1 h, haircut x2, zero reward (isolates adverse selection), and a no-reward control (20 highest-24h-volume gamma markets not in the reward set, quoted with the median v and min_size of the reward set, one unit each, fill-only per USD).
- **Monthly return on 147 USD** = the base compound monthly, net of fills, reported next to the gross figure and the linear one. A replay of a few days is an estimate of the sign and size, not a verdict.

## 4. Forward paper tape (step 5; only if the probe is not `kill_complete`)
- **Tracked set**, refreshed at the start of every run: the 50 reward markets with the highest pool per USD of a primary-shape min-size quote (the same rank as section 3), plus 20 no-reward control markets as above. Group is stored per market.
- **Cadence:** 58-minute runs dispatched hourly by `keeper.yml`; a book snapshot of every tracked YES token every 5 minutes (stored: time, best bid, best ask, Q_one and Q_two of the band) and the new trades of each market from the data API at the same moments (stored: time, outcome index, side, price, size; deduplicated). The plan says one-minute snapshots; 5 minutes is chosen to keep the data branch small, and fills come from the trade feed, so snapshot gaps lose no fills. Branch `data/poly-rewards`.
- **Rule (fixed before the first row):** per tracked market one min-size unit, primary shape (two-sided, d = 0.5 v), refreshed every 3,600 s at the snapshot midpoint (a snapshot > 20 min old = no quote); **filled in full whenever a trade prints at or through our price**; inventory marked at the bid (0.005 against us) at the last trade price within 24 h; reward per hour = pool x our share (from the stored Q of the snapshot) / 24 while both sides are live.
- **Unit:** a market-day, return per USD of resting capital (unit cost), clustered by UTC day (the daily series is the sum over markets divided by group capital). Controls: zero-reward replay of the same fills, and the control group's fill-only return.
- **14 days can only kill:** kill if the pooled net mean is <= 0, or if the reward income is smaller than the loss of the zero-reward run. **Found** needs at least **60 tape days**, a day-clustered one-sided p < 0.05 on the daily difference (reward group net minus control group net), and a positive mean at double the haircut. At 60 days without that: "not found". The lengths 14 and 60 are fixed here.
- **FB1:** best bid and ask are stored per market, so an FB1 basket can be formed only where every leg is in the tape. The tape does not deliver FB1's full event discovery; FB1 stays registered and unwritten.

## 5. Honest limits
No historical books exist; the replay holds today's competition constant and fills any touch in full. Reward amounts are computed from the documented formula, not observed payouts. Single-wallet size effects on the real queue are not modeled. "Not found" is a valid result and goes to `RESULTS.md` and `LESSONS.md` the same day.
