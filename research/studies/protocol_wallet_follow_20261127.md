# Protocol Q1: follow point-in-time ranked public Polymarket wallets, locked 2026-11-27

Plan: `research/plans/PLAN_20261127_fable.md`, direction Q1, kill tests (1) and (2). Simulated money and public endpoints only; no keys, no account, no orders. Runs on GitHub Actions only (`wallet_follow.yml`, mode `run`). Code: `wallet_follow_core.py` (pure, offline tests in `tests/test_wallet_follow.py`), `wallet_follow.py` (fetch). Locked after the counts-only probe (protocol_wallet_follow_probe_20261127.md and addenda Q1-0a, Q1-0b, Q1-0c) and **before any return, rank or P&L was computed**. **One run, one result.** A rule below changes only by a dated addendum written before the first affected row; a failed fetch is repaired without changing a rule and is reported.

## 0. Probe results that fix the design (counts only, no P&L)
- Kill 0a passed: frame 10,420 wallets (220 outcome-blind markets, 22 months), 32 of 200 sampled wallets qualify (>= 100 resolved conditions, history back to 2025-05-26), estimated about 1,667 qualifying wallets (>= 300 needed).
- Kill 0b: median hold of positions >= 500 USD with a known exit 3.8 days, median entry price 0.93; all-win ceiling (1 / (0.93 + 0.01)) ^ (30 / 3.8) = 1.63x: **not a standalone path**; Q1 can enter the book only as a component, and this protocol cannot reach a 3x claim by itself (the stage-0 gate below still applies the standard ceiling test).
- Hyperliquid arm: closed by the probe rule (no point-in-time frame, 2,000-fill cap; addendum Q1-0a). Result: arm not runnable point-in-time.
- `/v2/trades?user=` serves a wallet's taker fills for up to three years; the condition feed serves a market's tape (three years) newest first; the bare feed and `/v2/prices-history` cannot give a 60 s-delayed price for past trades (1-minute data is kept 7 days); a cursor cannot be forged (HTTP 400). The delayed fill is therefore read **from the market tape**, page by page.

## 1. Universe and data (point-in-time)
- **Frame:** the probe's rule, unchanged: for each of the 22 months 2025-01 .. 2026-10, resolved binary markets ending in the month with volume >= 50,000 USD (one gamma keyset page of up to 100), the first 10 by sha256("q1-wallet-probe-markets-20261127" + conditionId), their taker fills (up to 5 pages of 1,000, newest first); a wallet joins with any fill of notional >= 500 USD. The market sample reads final volume (not known at the market's start); it reads no outcome and no wallet, and wallets are eligible at a date only through their own history (section 2), never through the frame.
- **Histories:** every frame wallet's `/v2/trades?user=` (taker fills, default) up to 25 pages. A wallet truncated at the cap is **excluded and counted**; a wallet with fewer than 50 positions is dropped and counted; rows are de-duplicated on (tx, token, side, price, size, time) and the duplicate share is reported (kill rule below).
- **Position** = all of a wallet's fills in one (condition, token). Opened at its first BUY fill (time t0, price px0, notional usd0). Closed when the wallet has sold >= 95% of the bought shares (exit time = its last SELL, price = that fill's price) or, if not, when the market resolved (gamma `closedTime`, `closed=true`, `outcomePrices` exactly 0 or 1 for a two-token market; anything else is treated as open). Realised P&L = sell proceeds + unsold shares x (exit price, or payout 0/1) - buy cost. Fee markets (`feesEnabled`) can be ranked on but are never followed.

## 2. Ranking at a month start D (only positions closed before D)
- Window [D - 180 days, D). Eligible: >= 50 positions closed in the window, >= 25,000 USD bought in them, and not market-maker-like (bought both tokens in more than 50% of the window's conditions; computed on the ranking window only).
- Score = total realised P&L (USD) of those positions. Pools: **top 50** by score; **bottom 50**; **100 random** of the rest (by sha256("q1-wallet-follow-20261127" + month + wallet)). Fewer than 150 eligible wallets: the month is skipped and reported.
- Follow months: 19 monthly ranking dates D = 2025-04-01 .. 2026-10-01 (the follow window is [D, next month start)).

## 3. Test 1: persistence (no rule, no costs), train ranking dates only
For every D whose forward window ends inside train (D + 90 days <= 2026-01-01; 2025-04 .. 2025-10): rank as in section 2 and read each eligible wallet's realised ROI (sum of P&L / sum of cost) on positions first bought in [D, D + 90 days) and closed by the run date (>= 10 positions). Per date: Spearman correlation between score and forward ROI, and the median forward ROI of the top 50 minus the median forward ROI of all eligible wallets. **Kill** if the mean correlation over the dates is <= 0 or the mean of the top-50-minus-median gap is <= 0.

## 4. Test 2: one follow rule
- **Signal** (per wallet of the three pools, in its follow month): a position opened by a BUY fill of notional >= 500 USD at a price in [0.05, 0.95], on a market that is not a fee market and whose outcome is known (the wallet's own exit or a resolution before the run). Positions still open at the run date are dropped and counted (`open`), as are those below 500 USD (`small`), outside the band (`band`) and in fee markets (`fee`).
- **Entry (delay and cost):** the first tape BUY of the same token at least 60 s after the wallet's fill and within 30 minutes of that time (taker fills of the condition feed); fill price = the **worse (higher)** of that trade and the wallet's price + 0.01; no fill if there is no such trade or the price is above 0.97. A tape that did not reach the signal time within 60 pages is `tape_truncated` (dropped, counted).
- **Exit:** (a) if the wallet exited (>= 95% sold): our exit is the first tape SELL of the token at least 60 s after its last SELL and within 30 minutes, at the **worse (lower)** of that trade and their price - 0.01; no such trade: their price - 0.02; if the wallet left within 60 s of its entry the entry is `unfollowable`. (b) Otherwise hold to resolution (payout 0 or 1).
- **Costs:** no fee (fee markets excluded), no maker fee; the cost is the worse-of rules above (>= 1 cent each way). Net return per unit stake = (exit - entry) / entry; gross = the same at the wallet's own prices; cost = max(gross - net, 0); the stress column subtracts cost once more (2x cost). Equal stake per cluster; account weight w = 1 / 10 (ten concurrent stakes).
- **Cluster = market:** entries of the same condition (any wallet, any token) are averaged into one trade; t = first signal time, exit = last exit. All counts are per cluster.
- **Arms:** `top` (the rule), `bottom` and `rand` (the same rule on the other pools: controls). Splits by signal time: train [2025-04-01, 2026-01-01) = 9 months, validation [2026-01-01, 2026-06-01) = 5, test [2026-06-01, 2026-11-01) = 5.
- **Reported (per arm and split):** n, mean, sd, s = mean / sd, N per month = n / months, x = N x s x |s| at cluster level; for the top arm also the capital-turn-capped x (N capped at 20 stakes x 30 / median hold days), the ending 30-day multiple distribution, entries before clustering, and every drop count. Validation and test moments are printed for every arm; **no decision other than the pass rule below reads them**.
- **Stage-0 gate** (`core.sweep_stats.gate`, train clusters only, w = 1/10, 275 train days): >= 30 train clusters; train net mean above 0 and above the reversed-side placebo (the same trades with the side reversed); volatility / cost >= 10; x = N x s^2 >= 0.2; all-win ceiling (90th-percentile favourable excursion x w - cost, compounded over N a month) >= 3x. A failed gate is **not found** and validation and test are not evaluated.
- **Kill (any, train data only):** persistence (section 3); duplicate rows > 5%; stage-0 gate; more than 50% of train profit from the best 1% of clusters (or a non-positive train total); the random-wallet control not beaten: the top wallets' mean entry return minus the random wallets' mean, pooled over train months, must be > 0 with a one-sided label-permutation p < 0.10 (2,000 permutations of the top / random labels within each month, seed 11).
- **Pass** (all): no kill, and the sweep criteria of `core.sweep_stats.criteria` on the top arm's clusters: validation and test net mean > 0, validation and test mean at 2x cost > 0, >= 30 validation and >= 20 test clusters, Holm-adjusted validation p < 0.05 (**family size 1**: one rule, no grid; block bootstrap of the daily series, 20-day blocks), >= 70% of the 6-month folds positive, last-12-months mean at 2x cost > 0; and the top arm's mean beats the random arm's mean in validation and in test.
- **Read of a pass:** a pass here means "candidate for a forward ledger", never a trading claim: the ceiling, the post-hoc frame and the 22-month window are stated limits. Forward confirmation needs a separate dated protocol.

## 5. Stated limits
- The frame comes from a market sample chosen by final volume; wallets that traded only in markets outside the sample are invisible. Taker fills only (maker rows are excluded by the endpoint default; the probe saw many wallets with maker volume above taker volume). Truncated, very active wallets are excluded. Open positions at the run date are dropped, which removes long-dated markets from the last months only. The tape records fills, not quotes: a fill model, not an order book.
