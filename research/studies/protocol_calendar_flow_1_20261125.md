# Protocol calendar_flow_1: crypto calendar, flow and structural effects (locked 2026-11-25, before any data was pulled)

Owner instruction 2026-11-25: try every route that could make 3x a month possible. Seven rules, one Bonferroni family
(K = 22 variants), one sharded study `calendar_flow_1` (run `research_sharded.yml`, shards 20). Code: `core/calendar_flow.py`
(pure calculations, offline tests in `tests/test_calendar_flow.py`) and `research/studies/calendar_flow.py` (fetch and merge).
Do not edit this file after the first data pull; write a new dated protocol or an addendum instead (AGENTS.md rule 5).

## S0 shared specification (applies to every rule)
- Splits (UTC): train 2021-01-01..2022-12-31, validation 2023-01-01..2024-12-31, test 2025-01-01..2026-10-31. Train picks among the
  pre-listed variants (trimmed pick, drop best 5 train events), validation decides, test must not be negative. Older data is descriptive only
  (rows before 2021 fall in "out"). R2 splits by listing time T0 with train 2020-2022 (its own text), validation 2023-2024, test 2025-2026-10.
- Judge: `core.screens.judge` (event rules) for the table, pick, integrity and "without best 1 / best 5"; the pass decision is computed by
  `core.calendar_flow.rule_report` from the same rows: moving-block bootstrap with the UTC day as block (`core.news_backtest.block_bootstrap_p`,
  n = 20,000 draws, seed 7, fixed now because 2,000 draws cannot resolve a 0.0023 bar). Daily panel rules (R4): `core.factory.block_p`, 20-day blocks, 20,000 draws, seed 11.
- Bonferroni: K = R1 1 + R2 3 + R3 1 + R4 14 + R5 1 + R6 2 = 22, bar = 0.05 / 22 = 0.00227 for every rule (one constant, not per study). A test asserts the variant count.
- Pass = validation net mean > 0 with p < bar AND validation mean > 0 at double cost AND validation mean > 0 after dropping the best 1 trade and after
  dropping the best 5 trades (the repo's `without_best_1` / `without_best_5`; "best 1/5" is read as these two) AND test mean > 0 AND >= 3 events a week
  (all splits together; a rule below that is diagnostic and can never be primary: R2 and R6 are flagged). Reported for every rule: events/week,
  mean net, mean at 2x cost, worst trade, 3x-month check on validation (`core.factory.month_multiples`, size 20% of equity per trade, no leverage,
  trades of a month compounded in time order, overlap ignored), the random control, and the stop-bounded loss per trade.
- Costs for perps: 0.05% taker + 0.02% slippage per side = 0.14% round trip per leg. Hedged rules (R2, R3 non-BTC) pay two legs (0.28%). Real funding from
  `futures/um fundingRate` files, charged to whoever pays it: pnl = -side x sum of settled rates. A settlement counts when entry < settlement <= exit
  (stamps are rounded to the whole second, files carry a few ms). Missing funding is a gap and the trade is skipped, never zero. Double cost doubles the 0.14% legs (and for R1 the 1 bp spread and fee); real funding is not doubled.
- Short gain = 1 - exit/entry (`core.screens.short_gain`). Before any verdict the study asserts: no row has entry or exit <= 0, no short leg gains more than +100%,
  net equals gross - cost + funding. A violation voids the verdict of that rule (verdict "invalid"), it does not raise.
- Stops are evaluated on the bar high/low (1h or 1m). A bar that touches both stop and target takes the STOP. A bar that opens beyond the stop fills at its open
  (worse of stop and open, as in L12b). A hedge leg exits at the close of the bar in which the coin leg exited.
- Random control: same events and times, direction chosen at random (seeded) per event, same costs, same funding rule, the mirrored trade runs through the
  same stop and time exit (no target: a target exists only for the real direction).
- Data: data.binance.vision only (plus Deribit history API for R5, Yahoo for R3b). A failed download is a gap (`gaps` in the summary), never "no data".
- Known limits (all rules): costs are assumed not measured; fills are idealised to the bar; no market impact; correlated events inside a day are one cluster in the bootstrap.

## R1 stablecoin depeg reversion (1 variant)
- Pairs (Binance spot 1m, ever listed): USDCUSDT, FDUSDUSDT (2023-07..), TUSDUSDT, USDPUSDT (2021-09..), BUSDUSDT (..2023-12, delisted, kept). DAIUSDT excluded.
- d = close - 1.0000 of a 1m bar. Signal: first bar (cooldown 30 min after any exit on that pair, one open position per pair) with |d| >= 0.0010.
- Entry at the next bar's open with 1 bp adverse (d > 0: sell the stablecoin for USDT, inventory 50/50, no borrowing; d < 0: buy). Spread applied at entry AND exit
  (the spec text names the entry only; applying it on both is the pessimistic reading), 1 bp each. Fee per side f. Target: any bar close (from the entry bar on) within 2 bp of 1.0000, exit at that close.
  Time exit: close of the 60th bar. Hard stop: price 30 bp beyond the entry price (bar high/low, stop first, gap rule above).
- Fee scenarios, all reported: f = 0 bp (promo pairs, DECIDES), 2 bp, 10 bp (VIP0, shown to prove the live fee must be zero). Double cost: 2 x (2 x 1 bp spread + 2 f) in total.
- Reports: per-pair breakdown; with and without depeg windows (USDCUSDT 2023-03-10..15, TUSDUSDT 2023-06, FDUSDUSDT 2025-04); trades/week; pnl per week for a 147 USD book (20% per trade) at 1x, 3x, 5x, 10x leverage. Expected verdict on its own: no (edge of a few bp); role: uncorrelated high-frequency diversifier.
- Max loss per trade: (30 bp + 2 x 1 bp + 2 f) x 20% of equity.

## R2 new Binance USDT-perp first-week short (3 variants; frequency flagged, expected 1-3 a week)
- Events: every futures/um symbol matching ^[A-Z0-9]+USDT$ (USDC-quoted and dated delivery contracts drop out), T0 = first 1h bar of its earliest file (S3 listing, monthly, else daily).
  A symbol whose first bar is the archive start (2020-01-01 00:00) is dropped (listing before 2020).
- V1: short at the open of the bar T0+24h, exit at the close of the bar ending T0+7d. V2: short at T0+1h open, exit at the close of the bar ending T0+24h. V3: short at T0+24h open, exit at the close of the bar ending T0+3d.
- Each short hedged with a long BTCUSDT perp over the same bars. Hard stop: 1h high >= 1.35 x entry (coin leg), loss about 35% of notional = 7% of equity at 20% sizing, plus a gap if the bar opens higher.
- Split by T0. Descriptive only: rows split by later-delisted (last monthly file older than 2 months) and by 1000-prefix.

## R3 weekend-move fade to Friday's CME close (1 variant, basket)
- Basket: the 10 USDT perps with the highest trailing-30d quote volume (720 hourly bars strictly before F), frozen each Friday. Ever listed. A floor of 1e8 USDT per 30 days keeps shards small; the summary prints the lowest member volume to show the floor never binds.
- F = Friday 16:00 America/Chicago, S = Sunday 17:00 America/Chicago (zoneinfo, DST handled), time exit = Tuesday 17:00 America/Chicago. Reference prices: open of the 1h bar at that time (Chicago offsets are whole hours).
- gap = open(S) / open(F) - 1 (Binance prices). sigma = sample stdev of the last 30 UTC daily returns strictly before F's UTC day (at least 25 needed). Signal |gap| >= 1.5 sigma.
- Entry at the open of S, fade (gap up = short). Target: any 1h bar whose range touches the F price (from the entry bar on). Stop: a further 1.0 x |gap| against entry. Time exit at the Tuesday bar close. Non-BTC symbols hedged with BTCUSDT perp (not the BTC row itself).
- Decision p-value: all events of one week share the Sunday S, so the UTC-day block of the bootstrap is the week block. Report distinct weeks and per-week mean.
- R3b (descriptive, no pass authority): Yahoo BTC=F (period1=1513000000, period2 = now, daily): true CME gap = Monday open / Friday close - 1, weeks containing the last Friday of the month skipped (roll), compared with the Binance gap of the same week (sign agreement, correlation) and the share of gaps whose Friday close is touched by the Monday or Tuesday bar. ETH=F tried once; a miss is logged as a gap.

## R4 day-of-week drift (14 variants, daily panel)
- Panel: Binance spot USDT coins ever listed, daily UTC bars 2020-11..2026-10, tradable when 31+ days of history and 20-day average quote volume (previous 20 days) >= 2M USDT (exactly factory_crypto_1; stablecoins and leveraged tokens excluded by the same lists).
- Legs for each weekday d (Mon..Sun, UTC day d = close of the day before to close of day d): (a) long BTCUSDT unhedged; (b) equal-weight long of the top 30 tradable coins by 20-day average quote volume (decided at the previous close) minus short BTC.
- Costs: 0.2% per unit of turnover, entry and exit each week (leg a 0.4% a round trip; leg b 0.8%: two units each way). Real BTC perp funding for the short leg of (b) (short gain = + settled rate); spot legs pay none. Double cost doubles the 0.2%.
- Pass per leg: same sign and mean > 0 in train, validation and test, block p (20-day blocks) < bar in validation, double cost > 0 in validation. The daily series carries 0 on days the leg is flat (so blocks are 20 calendar days). Descriptive: weekend (Sat+Sun) vs weekday gross BTC mean per split. Many legs failing is the expected result.

## R5 Deribit expiry strike pin fade (1 variant, BTC and ETH)
- Expiries: every Deribit option expiry at 08:00 UTC from history.deribit.com get_instruments (expired=true, BTC and ETH) with their listed strikes. If the call fails or returns nothing, every day 2021-01-01..2026-10-31 is an expiry day with the grid of multiples of 1000 (BTC) / 50 (ETH); counted as a gap. If the call works, days it does not list are not expiry days (counted).
- At E-8h (00:00 UTC): P = open of the Binance spot 1m bar, K = the listed strike (multiple of 1000 / 50) nearest to P. Signal: 0.3% <= |P-K|/P <= 1.0%. Entry at the open of the 00:00 bar of the matching USDT perp in the direction of K (below K = long), exit at the close of the 07:59 bar (the price at 08:00:00; "the expiry minute" read as the last minute before 08:00). Hard stop 0.8% adverse. No target. Cost 0.14% plus funding (the 08:00 settlement counts).
- Breakdown by asset and by expiry type (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly), descriptive only.

## R6 post-expiry unpin drift (2 variants, gate-exempt diagnostic)
- Events: last Friday of each month 08:00 UTC, BTC and ETH perps, data from 2020 (older rows are descriptive). V1 long, V2 short, entry at the close of the 07:59 bar, exit 24h later (close of the 07:59 bar the next day), stop 3% adverse. Train picks the direction, validation decides, Bonferroni counts both variants. About 0.25 events a week: fails the >= 3/week gate by construction; it cannot be primary and only informs whether R5 timing carries information.

## Expected outcome and what a pass would mean
Most rules fail. None alone reaches 3x a month at 20% size without leverage. R2 and R3 are the most plausible portfolio candidates, R1 a bounded-loss diversifier, R6 can never pass the frequency gate. A pass is a hypothesis: it needs a forward ledger under a new dated protocol before it counts.
