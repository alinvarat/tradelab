# Addendum 1 to protocol P3 (`protocol_poly_rewards_20261126.md`, which stays locked and unedited), written 2026-11-27

Reason: a review of the P3 measurement found five defects that would make the 60-day forward verdict dishonest. This addendum changes the **measurement** only, before any forward verdict exists (the 14-day and 60-day lengths, the kill rule, the `found` criterion and alpha stay as locked). Only one tape run (12 five-minute snapshots, 2026-11-26, no fills) exists; it is kept on `data/poly-rewards` but **not used**: the forward clock starts at the `epoch.json` the first minute-cadence run writes. Code: `poly_rewards_core.py`, `poly_rewards.py`, `poly_rewards_push.sh`, `poly_rewards.yml`, `keeper.yml`.

1. **Fill rule (replaces "filled in full whenever a trade prints at or through our price", section 3 and 4).** P1 (`protocol_p1p4_20261126.md`) rejected touch fills because they hide adverse selection. A resting quote fills only on a print **0.1% through** it: a YES bid at B fills on prints with yes-equivalent price <= B x 0.999, the YES ask at A (= NO bid at 1 - A) on prints with 1 - y <= (1 - A) x 0.999. The fill is **partial, by the printed size**, summed over the prints of the refresh window up to our order size. A touch (print at the price or inside it) fills nothing. Reward stops at the first full fill of a side until the next refresh (before: the full hour was credited).
2. **Cadence.** The book of every tracked YES token is sampled **every 60 seconds** (the docs' own sampling), continuously: a run lasts 330 minutes, queues its successor 10 minutes before it ends (the concurrency group holds it pending, so there is no hole), pushes to `data/poly-rewards` every 30 minutes, and `keeper.yml` only restarts the chain if nothing is running or queued. Trades are pulled every 5th tick (the feed is history; if more than 5 pages of new trades appear the run counts a `trades_truncated`). **Gaps are logged**: `runs.jsonl` (ticks ok, ticks missed, failed book fetches per chunk) and `summary.json` `gaps` (every hole longer than 150 s, count, total, coverage). A snapshot older than 180 s is stale: no quote is placed from it and no reward accrues on it. Reward is accrued per minute sample (pool x share / 1,440), with our distance from the **current** midpoint (a drifting mid moves us out of range) and the docs' Q_min rule, instead of one share per hour.
3. **Tail / resolution.** CLOB `/markets/{condition}` (`closed`, `tokens[].winner`) is polled for every tracked market at the start of a run and every 30 minutes (`resolved.json`). Filled inventory of a resolved market is valued at its payoff (held token 1 or 0, no haircut), not at the 24 h mark; unresolved inventory keeps the 24 h mark (against us by 0.005) and its notional is reported as `open_usd`. Scenarios reported: `base` (resolution-marked), `mtm24_no_resolution` (the old mark, for comparison), `haircut_x2`, `zero_reward`, and **`tail_stress`** (unresolved inventory bought at >= 0.80 goes to 0). The verdict rule is unchanged but now reads `base`; `tail_stress` and `open_usd` are reported next to it and must be quoted with any verdict. A fill at 0.95 in a market that resolves against it now costs about 0.95 a share.
4. **Single-sided eligibility** (docs.polymarket.com liquidity-rewards, read 2026-11-27): single-sided liquidity scores at 1/c (c = 3) only while the midpoint is in [0.10, 0.90]; outside it a quote must be double-sided (Q_min = min(Q_one, Q_two)); orders count only within the market's max spread. The pure functions already had this; the defects were that the replay quoted both sides for a shape it had allocated single-sided, and never re-checked the band as the mid moved. Now a single-sided quote is placed only with mid in the band, fills only on its own leg, and earns only at samples where the current mid is in the band; all other allocations are excluded.
5. **Reconciliation.** The "13.3% of 147 USD a day" upper bound and the "145%/day on 56.5 USD" replay do not conflict: **`gross_pct` is a fraction of the 147 USD budget**, and RESULTS.md / LESSONS.md printed it as a percent. 13.3 means 1,330% a day (1,962 USD a day on 147 USD; two-sided primary 6.66 = 978 USD a day), and the replay's 1.45 means 145% a day. The replay pays each market exactly the allocation's `pool x share` (checked per market in `replay.reconcile`, ratio 1.000; the offline test asserts the invariant). The size of the number comes from the data: the top-ranked markets have pools of 100-450 USD a day and **zero in-band competition** in the snapshot (Q_comp 0, share 1.0), so one 20-share quote on 19 USD collects the whole pool. That is the assumption the forward tape must test (do rivals show up), not a finding.

Effect on earlier statements: the P3 `standalone_pass` is an **upper bound under the touch-fill, constant-competition, hold-to-mark model**, not a result; the replay fill figures (+23% / +1.98% a day, "no adverse-selection loss") were computed with whole touch fills and are superseded. The forward verdict is read only from rows written after the epoch, under this addendum.
