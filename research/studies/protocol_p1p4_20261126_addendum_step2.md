- Time zero T0 = the first 1-hour bar of the first venue at or after the venue's listing time minus 1 h. Entry = open of the bar at T0 + 24 h, exit = close of the bar ending T0 + 7 d (`core.calendar_flow.contiguous`/`walk`, stop 1.35 x entry, a short week reports reason `gap`), BTCUSDT long hedge from Binance 1-hour klines (open of the entry bar to the close of the bar in which the coin leg closed), cost 0.28% (`LEG_COST` x 2), venue funding not fetched (0), reported at double cost (`net - cost`). A cluster with no bar at T0 + 24 h, or with a missing BTC bar, is dropped and counted; a failed download is a gap and counted separately.
- Kill 2 is evaluated on **train** (the stage-0 convention): train mean <= 0, or train mean not above the mirrored placebo or the random-age control, or train x < 0.2 with N capped at 8.6 a month (x = min(N, 8.6) x s^2). x and N are also reported by split uncapped and capped.
- `core.sweep_stats.gate` is applied unchanged on the train clusters with account size lev 1, k 2 (two wallet units, w 0.5): its all-win ceiling and volatility/cost lines are reported; the train n >= 30 line applies as written. Post-gate criteria and the single-rule Holm are P1's (validation block-bootstrap p < 0.05, validation n >= 30, test n >= 20, folds, last-12-month at double cost, validation and test above both controls).
- Placebo = the mirrored trade (coin long, BTC short, stop at entry x (1 - 0.35)), same bars, same cost. Random-age control = for each cluster up to 3 seeded (`20261126:<base>`) other contracts of the same venue whose listing time is >= 180 days before the cluster's T0; the first one with bars at the same calendar entry time gives the control trade (the same short, hedged, stop, cost); a cluster without one is left out of the control mean.
- Hyperliquid 1-hour candles reach only the newest 5,000 per coin (a reach limit, reported); a coin older than that is a gap. Contracts no longer listed by a venue are absent from the lists (survivorship; it biases against a short).
- Reported per split (listing time: train 2023-01-01..2024-09-01, validation ..2025-09-01, test ..2026-10-29): n, mean, sd, s, N a month, x, capped x, hit rate, placebo, random-age control, mean at double cost, by first venue, and the daily correlation with L1f (by exit day).
