# fxcom_1: 8 pre-registered rules on FX, gold and commodities. Locked 2026-11-25, before any Yahoo or FRED data was pulled for this study

Simulated money only. No LLM, no cost. Code `core/fxcom.py`, study `fxcom_1` in `research/studies/run.py`, single job via `research.yml` (not sharded: about 14 Yahoo symbols x 2 requests plus 7 FRED CSVs). Goal context: a repeatable method that grows 147 USD by 3x in a month after costs and beats a random control. Realistic expectation stated before the run: published edges here are Sharpe 0.5-1 (R1, R7) and nothing is expected to give 3x/month; a clean kill is the likeliest result. Any result other than a pass is reported as "not found".

## 1. Universe and data

- Daily: GC=F SI=F HG=F CL=F BZ=F NG=F ZC=F and EURUSD=X USDJPY=X GBPUSD=X AUDUSD=X USDCAD=X (R1, 12 assets); EURUSD=X USDJPY=X AUDUSD=X USDCAD=X USDCHF=X NZDUSD=X (R7, 6 currencies). Yahoo `core.data.chart(sym, "10y", "1d")`. R7 needs USDCHF=X and NZDUSD=X as daily pairs too.
- Hourly: GC=F SI=F HG=F CL=F BZ=F NG=F and EURUSD=X USDJPY=X GBPUSD=X AUDUSD=X USDCAD=X USDCHF=X NZDUSD=X (13 candidates; the "12 assets" of the brief is a count of whatever passes the gate). `core.data.chart(sym, "2y", "1h")`. Closes and opens only (Yahoo FX highs/lows and volume are unreliable).
- Hourly data gate, per symbol: drop the last (possibly unfinished) bar; drop bars whose timestamp is not on the hour; fail the symbol when more than 1% of bars were off the hour or fewer than 9,000 bars remain. A failed symbol is dropped from every hourly rule and reported (`gates`); the rules still count in the Bonferroni family.
- Bar convention: Yahoo timestamps are bar STARTS. "Close at HH:00" means the close of the bar that starts at HH-1:00. A decision at the close of bar t fills at the open of bar t+1. All local times use `zoneinfo` (America/New_York, Europe/London) from raw UTC timestamps, never a +12h shift. Known-day tests: 2025-03-07 NFP 08:30 ET = 13:30 UTC (bar 13:00 UTC), 2025-08-01 (DST) = 12:30 UTC (bar 12:00 UTC).
- Daily date key: the local date in the symbol's `meta.exchangeTimezoneName`. The last daily bar is dropped when its date is not before the run date. Each asset keeps its own bar calendar; a decision at an asset's bar k earns its bar k+1 return.
- Hourly bars flagged: any bar whose start is 17:00-17:59 ET, and any bar whose previous bar in the same asset is more than 3,600 s earlier (first bar after a weekend or break). Flagged bars cannot be entered (neither signal bar nor entry bar). Positions are forced flat at the close of the last bar before any missing hour (counted `forced_flat`), so no position crosses a gap the test never saw.
- FRED CSV: DFF (USD, daily, lag 1 day), ECBDFR (EUR, daily, lag 1 day), IRSTCI01JPM156N (JPY), IR3TIB01AUM156N (AUD), IR3TIB01CAM156N (CAD), IR3TIB01CHM156N (CHF), IR3TIB01NZM156N (NZD), monthly, lag 45 days (point in time). GBP has no usable series (stale since 2026-01): its rate differential is set to 0 (only the markup applies) and counted `gaps.gbp_rate_zero`. The fetcher uses the default urllib User-Agent first, retries 3 times, and a failure is counted as a gap (missing currency rate = zero differential, R7 ranks the currencies that have rates and needs at least 4).
- Roll guard (data guard, not an executable result): daily returns are clipped symmetrically to +-4 trailing-60-day sigma (trailing excludes the day itself, needs 20 returns); hourly close-to-close returns with |r| > 8 trailing-480-hour sigma are set to 0 (adjusted price series, counted `gaps.roll`). Every rule is also run WITHOUT the guard and reported (not judged).

## 2. Splits (fixed before any data was read)

- Daily rules (R1, R7): warm-up 2016-11-25 to 2017-12-31, train 2018-01-01 to 2021-12-31, validation 2022-01-01 to 2024-12-31, test 2025-01-01 to 2026-11-24.
- Hourly rules (R2-R6, R8): warm-up 2024-11-25 to 2025-01-31, train 2025-02-01 to 2025-07-31, validation 2025-08-01 to 2026-01-31, test 2026-02-01 to 2026-11-24. Hourly validation is only about six months: low power, said in the report. A trade belongs to the split of its EXIT time.
- No parameter is chosen on train; every threshold is fixed below, so train is descriptive.

## 3. Costs (fixed now; per side, fraction of notional)

- Trading: EURUSD USDJPY GBPUSD USDCAD USDCHF 0.015%; AUDUSD NZDUSD 0.025%; GC 0.06%; SI HG CL BZ 0.10%; NG 0.15%; ZC 0.12%. The 2x run doubles trading costs, the commodity holding cost and the FX markup (not the interest-rate differential).
- Holding, commodities (perp-style funding assumption, conservative): long pays 0.04% per calendar day of notional, short pays 0.01%, pro rata by elapsed hours (ZC=F uses the same).
- Holding, FX: for each 17:00 ET crossing while held (triple on Wednesday): side x (r_base - r_quote)/365 x days, minus 0.5%/yr/365 x days markup, rates as of that time with the lags above. USD-base pairs (USDJPY, USDCAD, USDCHF): base = USD. Daily rules use the actual calendar days between bars instead.
- Booked gain per trade: side x (exit/entry - 1); a short gain is 1 - exit/entry. Equity return of a trade = size x gain - size x (entry cost + exit cost) - holding cost x size. Entry is the next bar open. A one-bar DELAYED entry variant (open of t+2; exits unchanged) is reported for every rule (robustness only, not an extra judged rule).

## 4. Rules (8 judged, interpretations fixed here)

Common to the hourly rules R2-R6, R8: size (notional / equity) = min(leverage cap, 1% / stop-distance fraction), leverage cap 10x commodities and 20x FX; stop distance fraction = |entry - stop level| / entry; a trade whose stop is already breached at entry is skipped. At most one open position per asset. Per-rule total open risk (sum of size x stop fraction of open trades) is capped at 4% of equity: trades are accepted in entry-time order and a trade that would breach the cap is skipped (it is not replaced by a later signal inside its hold window). Stops are checked on bar closes and filled at the next bar open. "Exit after n bars" = exit at the open of bar entry+n. A signal-based exit or stop at the close of bar s fills at the open of bar s+1 (the last bar of the data fills at its close).

**R1_tsmom_voltarget** (daily, 12 assets). At each close d: signal_i = mean of sign(close_d / close_{d-L} - 1) over L in {21, 63, 126, 252} bars (one combined signal in [-1, 1]). unit_i = 0.10 / sigma60_i / N, N = 12, sigma60 = annualised (sqrt 252) stdev of the last 60 daily returns including day d. Target weight = signal_i x unit_i. Gross |w| capped at 5x (all weights scaled down together). Rebalance an asset only when |target - current| >= 0.10 x unit_i (otherwise keep the weight). Weights earn the asset's next bar return. Low-count baseline.

**R2_hourly_breakout**. Close_t > max(close_{t-48..t-1}) -> long, < min -> short, enter at open t+1. ATR24 = mean |close-to-close change| over the last 24 bars (price units). Stop at entry -+ 2.0 x ATR24. Exit when the close crosses the opposite 24-bar closing extreme of the prior 24 closes (long: close < min of the previous 24 closes), or after 48 bars.

**R3_hourly_overextension_fade**. R6h = close_t / close_{t-6} - 1; sig = stdev of 1-bar returns over the last 480 bars x sqrt(6). |R6h| >= 3.0 x sig -> enter the OPPOSITE side at open t+1. Exit after 6 bars or a stop at 1.5 x |R6h| beyond entry (stop fraction = 1.5 x |R6h|). Signal bars t, t+1, t+2, t+3 counted from any R6 release bar of that asset (calendar below, whether or not R6 traded it) cannot signal.

**R4_ny_session_continuation**. America/New_York. M = close at 08:00 ET (close of the bar starting 07:00) / close at 19:00 ET of the previous calendar day (close of the bar starting 18:00) - 1 (13 h); skip when either bar is missing. sigma = stdev of the same 13 h return over the previous 60 trading days (needs >= 40); sigma_hold = stdev of the 7 h return (open of the 08:00 bar to the close of the bar starting 14:00) over the previous 60 days (needs >= 40). |M| >= 0.5 x sigma -> enter at the open of the 08:00 ET bar in direction sign(M). Exit at 15:00 ET (close of the bar starting 14:00). Hard stop 1.5 x sigma_hold from entry (bar-close check, next-open fill). Skip US federal holidays; the "Friday after 12:00 ET" clause cannot bind (entry 08:00, exit 15:00) and is recorded as vacuous.

**R5_london_breakout_asia_range** (FX 7 + GC SI). Asia range = max and min of the hourly closes of bars starting 00:00-05:00 UTC of the same UTC date (closes 01:00-06:00 UTC, so the range is fully known before any London-window close in either DST regime; this is the fixed interpretation of "00:00 to 07:00"). Require 0.15% <= (max-min)/last Asia close <= 1.2%. Window: bars whose close time is 08:00-11:00 Europe/London local (starting 07:00-10:00). First bar in the window with close > max (long) or < min (short) -> enter next open; one trade per asset per day. Stop: stop distance from entry = min(distance to the opposite side of the range, 1.5% of entry), bar-close check. Exit at 16:00 London (close of the bar starting 15:00), no target.

**R6_macro_release_fade** (America/New_York, mechanical calendar, one rule): (a) Thursday 08:30 jobless claims and (b) first Friday of the month 08:30 payrolls on GC SI EURUSD USDJPY GBPUSD AUDUSD USDCAD USDCHF NZDUSD; (c) Wednesday 10:30 EIA crude on CL BZ; (d) Thursday 10:30 EIA gas on NG. Skip any week (Mon-Sun) that contains a US federal holiday Monday-Thursday. Release bar k = the hourly bar containing the release time; move = close_k - close_{k-1}; z = (close_k/close_{k-1} - 1) / stdev of 1-bar returns over the last 480 bars (needs 240). |z| >= 1.0 -> at the open of bar k+1 enter AGAINST the move, exit at the open of bar k+4 (3 bars), stop level = close_k + (sign of move) x |move| (stop distance measured from the entry price; bar-close check). FOMC excluded.

**R7_fx_carry_volscaled** (daily). Currencies EUR JPY AUD CAD CHF NZD vs USD. At the last FX bar of each ISO week (the Friday close): rank by rate differential (r_ccy - r_USD, point-in-time as above); long the top 2 currencies, short the bottom 2. Weight per currency = side x 0.10 / sigma60 / 4 (sigma60 of the pair as in R1), gross capped at 5x. Pair weight = currency weight x (+1 for XXXUSD, -1 for USDXXX). Same 0.10 turnover filter as R1. Carry/swap accrues as in the FX holding cost. Low-count baseline; labelled a proxy (rates partly stale and revised).

**R8_metal_energy_spread_reversion** (hourly pairs GC/SI and CL/BZ). spread = ln A - ln B on the hours where both bars exist; z = (spread - mean480)/std480 over the last 480 pair-hours (current bar included, needs 240). |z| >= 2.5 at close t: enter at next open, short the spread if z > 0 (short A, long B). Exit at the open after |z| <= 0.5, after 72 bars, or after |z| >= 4.0 at a bar close. Dollar-neutral 1:1; gross leverage of the pair capped at 6x (3x per leg); also 1% risk sizing with stop fraction = (4.0 - |z_entry|) x std480 per leg-notional; entries at |z| >= 4.0 are skipped. Costs are charged on both legs; both legs pay their holding cost.

## 5. Evaluation (what is computed and what passes)

- Daily net returns per rule as a fraction of equity (one value per trading day, 0 when flat; hourly-rule trades are booked on the UTC date of their exit). Series: base cost, `name@2x` at double cost. ONLY these 8 + 8 go into `core.factory.judge_all`. Delayed-entry, unclipped and control series stay out of it.
- Bonferroni across the batch of 8: bar 0.05/8 = 0.00625 on the validation moving-block bootstrap p (20-day blocks). Daily and hourly rules have different splits, so `judge_all` is called twice with alpha scaled so the bar is identical (0.05 x 2/8 and 0.05 x 6/8).
- Pass (judge_all): validation mean > 0, p < 0.00625, validation > 0 at 2x cost, test mean > 0 (rule unchanged). Added by this protocol: (a) beats the random control at the same bar on validation (control p < 0.00625); (b) validation still > 0 after removing the best 5% of trades and after removing the best single trade (days for R1 and R7); (c) trades per week >= 3 (below = "low-count", cannot be the main route to 3x but may pass the screen as a sleeve). `screen_pass` = judge pass and (a) and (b); `main_route_pass` = screen_pass and (c).
- Sharpe is reported with sqrt(252) on trading-day series (judge_all annualises with sqrt(365); its figure is ignored).
- 3x month check: judge_all `validation_3x_check` (leverage 1-12x on the daily series) AND the actual capped-leverage CONTINUOUS account run: start 147 USD at the start of validation, compound trade by trade (or day by day for R1, R7) to the end of test, 5 USD minimum order (a trade whose notional < 5 USD is skipped; for R1/R7 the position-days below 5 USD at 147 USD are counted), at base and at 2x cost: ending equity, worst drawdown (on realised equity), calendar months with equity multiple >= 3, ruin flag.
- Random control: 2,000 draws per rule and split keeping entry times, assets, holding times, stop distances, sizes and costs; direction flipped at random per trade (the opposite side's holding cost is recomputed). R1 and R7: the sign of each position run (a contiguous same-sign stretch of an asset) is flipped at random, weights, turnover and trading cost unchanged. Report the rule's validation and test mean against that distribution (p = share of draws >= the rule). Seed fixed (20261125).
- Ceiling check FIRST: perfect foresight, one trade a day, the best asset and direction after 1x costs at the leverage caps; daily bars (open to close of the day) and hourly bars (best window inside the day, close to the best earlier open) separately; months with a >= 3x multiple in validation and test; if zero in the universe, that is the first line of the result.
- Reported also: trades per week per rule, results by asset and by year, buy-and-hold drift per asset per split (so a long gold run is not counted as skill), results with and without the roll guard, delayed-entry variant, without best 5% and best single trade, data gaps (`gates`, `gaps.roll`, forced flat, skipped for the 4% cap, minimum notional).
- Known limits: Yahoo `=F` is unadjusted front-month (the guard is a data fix, perps have funding instead of roll); FX has no perp, so FX uses a modelled retail-CFD spread; asynchronous daily closes across asset classes; USD-base pairs booked as a CFD on the quoted price (gain = side x (exit/entry - 1)); hourly validation about 6 months; Binance TradFi perp history (XAU from 2025-12, CL from 2026-04) overlaps the test window and is not an independent confirmation; no forward test is started by this study.
- Kill: any rule that fails the judge is closed; "not found" is a valid outcome of the whole batch.
