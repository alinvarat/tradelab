# Protocol D3 + D4: delist-hazard short before the notice, and tag-removal / survivor-relief long, pre-registered 2026-11-26

Locked before any price of this study was pulled. Spec source: `research/plans/PLAN_20261126_fable.md`, directions D3 and D4. Code: `research/studies/d3d4_core.py`
(pure functions, under `research/` so a merge does not deploy), `research/studies/d3d4.py`, workflow `d3d4.yml`, tests `tests/test_d3d4.py`. Frozen inputs and rows are kept on
branch `data/d3d4`. Goal: independent forced-flow components for the event book (not a result until a rule passes every gate below AND is confirmed forward through a dated addendum).

## D3: short before a delist notice (Binance USDT-M perp, point-in-time features)
- **Universe.** Every Binance USDT-M perp `<BASE>USDT` in `data.binance.vision` (`list_symbols`, delisted contracts included), base with a 1000 / 1000000 prefix stripped to match notice names. Stablecoin
  bases and coins that already have an L1f or `fut_delist` notice published before d (the notice has happened; that is the post-notice L1f leg) are excluded in every set and in the control pool. Signal day d = each UTC day 2024-01-01 .. 2026-10-14 (last entry leaves a 15-day hold inside the window).
- **Features at day d, from daily perp klines up to the close of d-1 only (no look-ahead; all computed in stage A, frozen):**
  - `lowvol`: median daily quote volume of d-30..d-1 (at least 25 days present) in the lowest third of all perps with that history on day d. Market cap is NOT used (no point-in-time source).
  - `fall`: close(d-1)/close(d-31) - 1 <= -20% AND close(d-1) < close(d-8). A perp needs bars on d-1, d-8, d-31 (so not a new listing).
  - `tag`: the coin is tagged at 00:00 UTC of d: last tag-add notice < d, no monitoring-tag removal notice and no L1f / `fut_delist` notice of the coin after that add and before d. Tag adds and the L1f / `fut_delist`
    lists come from the frozen `data/forced-flow` events (`mon_tag`, `l1f` and `fut_delist` families); removals from the fresh catalog parse below. Tags that existed before 2024-01-01 are unknown (cold start; reported).
- **Rules (3 sets x 3 holds = 9; Holm only over the 6 judged ones).** s1 `tag` (descriptive baseline only: a standing tag short is the basket the plan lists as killed on paper); s2 `tag & lowvol & fall` (judged);
  s3 `lowvol & fall` (judged; the tag is not required, to show whether it adds anything). Holds 3, 7, 14 days. Rule id `d3|s2|h7d`.
- **Trade (short, 1 x).** Entry at the open of the 01:00 UTC 1-hour bar of d (at most 3 bars late). Exit at the first of: (a) the open of the 1-minute bar at or after `notice + 60 s` of the coin's first Binance L1f or `fut_delist` notice
  after entry (the D3 leg stops where the L1f leg starts; 1-hour bar open at or after that time when 1-minute data is missing, counted); (b) the open of the hourly bar `hold` after entry (at most 3 bars late);
  (c) a 25% stop above entry on an hourly high, fill at the stop plus 0.5%. If the perp stops trading before the exit, exit at the close of its last bar (settlement proxy, counted) - dropping such trades would remove the winners.
  Trades with no usable exit bar are dropped and counted. One open position per coin and rule; the next entry needs a later exit (non-overlapping holds).
- **Costs (judged).** Round trip 0.6% (taker fees 0.11% + slippage 0.2% per side, as forced_flow) plus REAL funding: the sum of Binance settled `fundingRate` rows with entry < time <= exit; a short receives positive funding
  (cost = -sum). Funding file missing: 0 and counted. Stress: cost doubled. Sensitivity at the 0.14% perp taker round trip is descriptive only.
- **Judged unit = cluster = entry day.** The trades of one rule entered on the same day are one observation: the mean of gross, cost, funding, net, mfe, mae. N per month = clusters per month; s = mean/sd over clusters; x = N * s^2
  (stage 0 and everything after use clusters; coin-level numbers are reported beside). Splits by entry day: train 2024-01-01..2025-07-01, validation ..2026-03-01, test ..2026-10-29 (as forced_flow).
- **Matched control.** For each (day, hold) one draw (seed `20261126:d3:<day>`) of 5 perps from that day's pool = `lowvol & not tag & not fall` (same volume bucket, not signalled), the same short with the same entry, hold,
  exit rules, stop, funding and cost; control = mean of the drawn coins (needs >= 3 usable). Train net mean must exceed the control mean; so must validation and test. The 24 liquid alts of forced_flow are not used (small-cap beta would pass as edge).
- **Stage A (kill 1, timestamps and flags only, NO returns).** Computed in an Actions run (`mode=events`) before any price of a trade is read: per set the number of train (before 2025-07-01) qualifying coin-days and the
  hazard per coin-day = share of those coin-days on which a Binance L1f or `fut_delist` notice for the coin is published during that UTC day (also reported: within 3, 7, 14 days; weekday/hour histogram of the notices;
  lead from tag add to delist notice). **Kill 1: neither judged set (s2, s3) reaches a train hazard >= 10% per coin-day on >= 20 qualifying train coin-days.** The plan's fixed-hour / vote-to-delist conditions are not
  tested here (the user brief for this run names the three features); this is the plan's coin-day threshold applied to those features. The frozen conditions cannot change after the hazard table is read.
- **Stage 0 (train only, `core.sweep_stats.gate`, count relaxed as forced_flow: n_train >= 20 clusters).** train mean > 0 and above the reversed-side placebo, vol/cost >= 10, x >= 0.2, all-win ceiling >= 3x a month,
  train mean above the matched-control mean. Kill 2: no judged rule passes stage 0, or train mean <= 0 for every judged rule.
- **After stage 0:** `core.sweep_stats.evaluate` + `judge_family`, Holm over the stage-0 survivors (alpha 0.05, block bootstrap on the validation daily series); val and test net mean > 0, also at double cost; validation n >= 12 and test n >= 10
  clusters; both 6-month folds positive; last-12-month mean at double cost > 0; validation and test means above the matched control. A rule PASSES when it meets every criterion. Strict counts (30 / 30 / 20) reported beside.

## D4: long spot after a tag removal, or after a survivor round
- **Events (Binance catalogs 161, 49, 93, full titles, no prices).** `tagrm`: coins named after "Remove the Monitoring Tag for" in a Binance notice title (seed-tag removals are not events). `survive`: at each Binance
  "Will Delist ... on <date>" (L1f) notice time T, every coin that was tagged for at least 1 day at T (rule above) and is NOT named in any L1f / `fut_delist` notice within +-7 days of T; one event per coin per 30 days.
  One event per (family, coin) per 30 days. Window 2024-01-01 .. 2026-10-29 by notice time; splits as D3.
- **Judged unit = cluster = notice** (coins of one notice averaged; a notice is one risk unit in the book).
- **Kill 1 (counts, stage A, no prices): fewer than 20 train clusters in total (tagrm notices with a coin + survive notices with a survivor) kills D4.** Priced once anyway, descriptive only, can not pass.
- **Trade (long, spot).** Binance spot `<COIN>USDT` 1-minute klines (data.binance.vision; symbol then `1000` prefix); the coin needs a bar before the notice. Entry at the open of the first 1-minute bar at or after notice + delay (delay 1 or 5
  minutes); exit at the open of the bar `hold` later (1 h, 4 h or 24 h); stop 25% below on a bar low, fill at the stop minus 0.5%. Cost 0.6% round trip; no funding or borrow on spot (0). Stress: cost doubled.
  Rules: 2 families x 3 holds x 2 delays = 12; Holm over the stage-0 survivors.
- **Control / placebo.** Reversed side (gate placebo). Random control: per event one draw (seed `20261126:d4:<coin>:<ts>`) of 5 coins from the 24 liquid alts of forced_flow (never the event coin), the same long on spot 5-minute bars with the same delay,
  hold, stop and cost (needs >= 3); train, validation and test means must beat it. Stage 0 and the post-stage criteria as D3.

## Parser: the one allowed fix
Stage A dumps every Binance title containing "Monitoring Tag" in full. One parser fix for `monitoring_removed` is allowed after reading those titles, documented in the PR, before the freeze. The tag-add events are the frozen ones;
the fresh parse only adds removals (and a count cross-check of adds and L1f against the frozen lists).

## After a kill
A kill is reported as "not found / data insufficient". The priced run is still done ONCE so that n, mean, sd, s, N and x by split and the L1f correlation can be reported, but a killed direction is descriptive only and cannot pass. No rule,
grid, threshold, cost or feature change after seeing any result; one run and one fix (a data or parser bug, documented) is the only allowance.

## Reporting
Per rule: n, mean, sd, s = mean/sd, N per month, x = N s^2, hit rate, for train / validation / test (clusters, and coins for D3); gross mean, placebo mean, control mean by split; hazard table; count of trades through a notice
(share of entries followed by an L1f / `fut_delist` notice inside the hold, and the mean of those against the rest). L1f correlation: daily net series of each rule bucketed by EXIT day (primary) and by 7-day week of exit (secondary),
against the daily series of the book component `l1f` (rule `l1f_ref|h4h|d1m` rows of the frozen `data/forced-flow` run, bucketed by exit day the same way), over 2024-01-01 .. 2026-10-29. Rules tested / passed and the closest to passing are given as `reports/LEADERBOARD.md` does.
A pass is proposed as a dated addendum `research/event_book/components_addendum_<date>.json` with a forward collector plan (separate PR; `components.json` and locked files are never edited).
