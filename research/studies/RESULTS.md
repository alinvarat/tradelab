## factory_crypto_2 screen: 7 perp rules with measured funding (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any data was pulled)
Code `core/factory_perp.py` (pure functions, new registry `PERP_RULES`; `core/factory.py` and batch 1's registry and 0.05/11 bar are untouched), study `factory_crypto_2`. No LLM, no cost. Builds on `core/factory.py` from PR #161. None of the closed lines (L1-L13, batch 1) is repeated: every rule below is a cross-sectional or pairs book on perps with measured funding.
- **Universe:** every Binance USDT-M perpetual ever listed, delisted included: the S3 prefixes under `data/futures/um/monthly/klines/`, kept when the name ends in `USDT` and has no `_` (quarterlies out), and the base (after stripping a leading `1000000` or `1000`) is not on `L8_SKIP`. Perp prices only, no spot join. Daily panel from the 1d monthly zips (open, high, low, close, quote volume col 7, taker-buy quote volume col 10); hourly panel from the 1h zips, downloaded only for perps whose 20-day average quote volume ever reaches 5M USDT. BTCUSDT daily bars are also loaded from 2019-09 for the A3 regime only.
- **Funding:** settled rates from the monthly `fundingRate` zips. Timestamps are rounded to the nearest minute at ingestion (ms jitter must not move a 00:00 settlement into the next window). Windows are half-open `(a, b]`: a settlement exactly at the decision time T belongs to the position held before T. A coin-month whose kline file exists but whose funding file failed to download or is missing is a gap: the coin is not tradable on any day whose funding windows (the A1 lookback `(T-7d, T]` and the hold `(T, T+24h]`) touch that month, and not in that month's hours on the hourly panel. A1 also drops a coin with no settlement in `(T-7d, T]`.
- **Daily accounting (`run_perp`, A1-A5):** weights set at the close of day i (T = 00:00 UTC of day i+1) earn close(i) -> close(i+1). Per position: `w*(c(i+1)/c(i)-1) - w*sum(funding in (T, T+24h])` (a long pays positive funding, a short receives it; no assumed `FUNDING_DAY`). A1-A4: the net dollar exposure is hedged with a BTCUSDT perp leg; A5 hedges only its beta residual (see A5). The hedge leg is a real position: it pays turnover cost and measured funding. Cost 0.2% per unit of turnover (`ONE_WAY_COST`); the 2x check doubles the cost only, funding stays measured. Vanished coin: a held coin with no close on day i+1 earns 0 that day and pays no funding (closed at its last close); its exit turnover is charged the next rebalance. Vanished positions are counted per rule and side.
- **Eligibility (daily):** batch 1's `features` (>= 31 days of bars, 20-day average quote volume over days i-20..i-1 >= 2M USDT, BTC excluded from ranks), plus the funding gate above.
- **A1 carry_funding_7d:** fund7 = sum of settled rates in `(T-7d, T]` / 7. Short the top 10% by fund7, long the bottom 10% (at least 5 per side, else flat), 0.5 gross per side, BTC-hedged, daily rebalance.
- **A2 taker_absorption:** imb = 2*taker_buy_quote/quote_volume - 1 on day i; z = (imb - mean of imb on the coin's previous 30 bars) / their population sd (needs 30 prior bars; sd 0 drops the coin). Cross-sectional OLS of z on r1 with intercept over the eligible coins; absorption = residual / pstdev(residuals). Book_i: short the top 10% by absorption, long the bottom 10% (k_min 5, 0.5 per side). Held weights = 0.5*Book_i + 0.5*Book_{i-1}, where Book_{i-1} keeps only coins that still have a close on day i. BTC-hedged.
- **A3 vol_regime_switch_rv:** regime = BTC 30-day realised vol (pstdev of the 30 daily simple returns ending day i) / median of that series over days i-365..i-1 (needs 365 prior values; BTC from 2019-09 so the regime exists from about 2020-11). < 0.9: hold batch 1's `xs_momentum_r7` book; > 1.3: hold `xs_reversal_r3`; otherwise cash. Thresholds locked.
- **A4 vol_regime_switch_dvol:** as A3 with regime = Deribit BTC DVOL / median of the previous 365 DVOL values. The DVOL value used at T is the close of the latest 1D candle whose period has ended by T (no assumption that candles are stamped 00:00 UTC). DVOL starts 2021-03-24, so A4 is cash until about 2022-03: train is partial; validation and test are complete.
- **A5 residual_reversal_ensemble:** coins with age >= 60 bars (and the factory volume bar); beta60 = OLS slope of the coin's 60 daily returns ending day i on BTC's same-day returns (all 60 day pairs must exist). e1 = r1 - beta60*rBTC1, e3 = r3 - beta60*rBTC3. score = mean of the cross-sectional percentile ranks (0..1, ties averaged) of -e1, -e3, -qv_ratio, -vol30. New longs from the top 10% by score, new shorts from the bottom 10% (k_min 5, else flat); a held long stays while it is in the top 30%, a held short while in the bottom 30%. Equal weight within a side; side grosses gL = Bs/(Bl+Bs), gS = Bl/(Bl+Bs) (Bl, Bs = mean beta of longs, shorts; gross 1.0, sum w*beta = 0; 0.5/0.5 if either mean beta <= 0). No dollar hedge (it would undo the beta neutrality); a BTC leg of -sum(w*beta) covers any residual.
- **A6 hour_of_day_xs (hourly):** at the start of UTC hour H on day D, eligible coins have a daily 20-day average quote volume over days D-20..D-1 >= 5M USDT, the funding gate, and all 20 same-clock-hour returns on days D-20..D-1. score = mean of those 20 returns (day D never used). Top / bottom 10% (k_min 5). Trade hour H only if mean score(top) - mean score(bottom) >= 0.8% (the gate stays 0.8% in the 2x run). Book: long top, short bottom, 0.5 per side, entered at the hour's open (previous close) and marked at its close; measured funding for settlements in `(open, close]`. Turnover is netted between consecutive traded hours; closing a book at the start of an untraded hour is charged to that hour. Daily return = compounded hourly returns of the UTC day (0 with no traded hour).
- **A7 coint_pairs_hourly (hourly):** formation at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each month from the previous 1440 hourly closes. Universe: the 40 perps with the highest 30-day average daily quote volume (>= 5M) with all 1440 closes, plus ETHUSDT and BTCUSDT. For each pair, B = the coin with the higher 30-day quote volume, A = the other; b = OLS slope of log A on log B with intercept; residual DF without lag, `de_t = g*e_{t-1} + u`, t_g = g/se(g). Keep t_g <= -3.90 and half-life -ln2/ln(1+g) in [4, 72] hours. ETH/BTC is always included and bypasses the test; it counts inside the 20 and toward the cap of 3 pairs per coin; the rest are filled by most negative t_g. b is frozen for the month. z = (s - mean)/pstdev over the last 168 closed hours, s = logA - b*logB. Entry at the next open when 2.5 <= |z| < 4.5 (no entry into an immediate stop): short the rich leg, long the cheap leg, notionals 1 : |b| scaled so the pair gross is 1/6. At most 6 open; simultaneous signals are taken in pair order (most negative t_g first, ETH/BTC first). Exit at the next open after z crosses or touches 0, after 72 hours, at |z| >= 4.5, at the month's last hour, or when a leg has no bar (closed at its last bar). Legs are buy-and-hold within a trade; no BTC hedge; cost 0.2% per unit of turnover; measured funding on both legs. No re-entry in the hour of an exit; z is undefined (no signal) when the spread sd over the window is below 1e-6 (clarifications added in the code commit, still before any data).

"Small" is therefore a random draw from the non-majors, not a volume cut; the study reports P&L by terciles of each day's traded notional (descriptive). A perp must exist to produce a file, so a short is only ever simulated while the perp traded.

- Rule, mechanical, per symbol-day and per hour (every hour starts flat): tick = smallest positive gap between distinct traded prices of that day (a structural constant; the day's trades are used to read it, a small look-ahead on a fixed number). Every 60 s the reference is the last trade price printed before the step (no quote if it is older than 300 s). Bid = reference - k ticks, ask = reference + k ticks, one clip each, live from 1 s after the decision until the next step (1 s latency). A bid fills at its price when a LATER trade prints strictly below it (touching is not a fill; the same for the ask above), at most once per side per step. Inventory is capped at +/-3 clips (a side switches off at the cap). At the end of each hour any inventory is closed at the hour's last trade price, one tick against us, paying the taker fee. Because the book is flat at every hour boundary, nothing is held across a funding time (00, 08, 16 UTC): measured funding is zero by construction. No queue position, no partial fills, no market impact (clips are tiny), no rebates.

P&L unit: a fraction of one clip's notional per symbol-day (0.001 = 10 bps of a clip; times the clip in USDT for dollars). Per day we report net P&L, fill counts, inventory risk (mean and max inventory, share of steps at the cap, worst hour, worst day) and the split by year, by symbol and by volume tercile.

A pass would still need a forward test: it is simulation on trade prints, not on a book.
- Known limits: no order-book data, so the touch is approximated by the last trade; a trade through a price in reality does not always fill us (the through rule is conservative against that) but our own order is not in the queue and the 1 s latency is assumed; only 30 symbols and 1 in 10 days; the tick is read from the same day; one exchange; the volume terciles are measured on the same day (descriptive only); a regime of cheap fees (VIP or rebates) is not modelled.

## H1 screen: perp-below-index snapback, and the mirror (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any premium-index, perp, spot or funding row was pulled)
Code `core.screens.basis_snap_rows` / `basis_snap_summary`, study `h1_basis_snapback` (sharded: `rows_h1_basis_snapback`, `summarize_h1_basis_snapback` in `run.py`; run with `research_sharded.yml`, default 20 shards). No LLM. Hypothesis (not proven): when a USDT perp trades far below its index (premium index <= -0.5%) while spot has not fallen as fast, the gap is a perp-only dislocation (forced liquidations, thin book) that closes within hours; the mirror (perp far above index, spot not rising as fast) closes the same way from above. Differs from L11/L12: the trigger is the perp-vs-index gap measured on 5-minute bars, not a price move or a funding level, and positions are held at most 4 hours.
- **Universe (no survivorship):** every USDT perpetual ever listed on `data.binance.vision` (`list_symbols("futures/um", "USDT")`, delisted perps included), minus the L8 skip list (stablecoins, WBTC...); futures/um has no leveraged tokens, so no name-suffix filter is applied (a suffix filter would drop JUP, SYRUP). Shorts and longs exist only where the perp existed at the time: an event needs a settled funding rate in the 24 h before entry. A perp is used only if the spot pair with the SAME name has 5-minute bars (needed for the spot condition); 1000-prefixed perps and index perps with no same-name spot pair are therefore out (counted, known limit).
- **Data:** monthly files 2022-01 to 2026-10 (the current month has no monthly file and is not used): `premiumIndexKlines` 5m (futures/um) for every symbol-month; for months that contain at least one candidate bar (premium close beyond +-0.5%) also futures `klines` 5m, spot `klines` 5m and `fundingRate` (the candidate month, the month before and the month after when the candidate is within 24 h of a month edge). **Sampling:** none by symbol or time; only the loading of perp, spot and funding files is limited to candidate months, which cannot change a row. A fetch that fails after retries blocks the events that touch that month and is counted in `shard_gaps` (never read as "no data").
- **Event (decision at the close of a 5-minute bar i, using only data up to that close):** L (long): premium-index close <= -0.5%. S (short): premium-index close >= +0.5%. Spot condition over the last 30 minutes (6 bars): perp close return minus spot close return <= -0.2 percentage points for L, >= +0.2 pp for S (the perp moved against the index more than spot did). Liquidity: sum of perp quote volume over bars i-11..i (1 hour) >= 100,000 USDT. Needs the 12 perp bars i-11..i and the spot closes at i and i-6 to exist. A symbol cannot open a new event on the same side until the previous trade on that side has exited. Two separate lines, L (`long_discount`) and S (`short_premium`), judged separately.
- **Entry:** the perp's open of bar i+1 (the bar after the decision). **Exit:** the first bar j in i+1..i+48 whose premium-index close has come back to >= -0.1% (L) or <= +0.1% (S) is a signal at its close; fill at the perp open of bar j+1. If no bar signals, exit at the perp open of bar i+49 (4 hours after entry). Premium bars i+1..i+48 and perp bars i+1..i+49 must exist, otherwise the event is dropped and counted (`<kind>_incomplete` in `shard_gaps`); this conditions on future data existing, so a delisting or outage inside the hold removes exactly the worst losers (known limit).
- **Costs:** futures taker fee 0.05% per side plus slippage 0.05% per side = 0.20% round trip, deducted from every trade. Funding: the funding rates actually settled with entry <= settlement time < exit (`fundingRate` files): a long pays the rate, a short receives it. Short gain is `core.screens.short_gain` (1 - exit/entry); long return is exit/entry - 1. No BTC adjustment (hold <= 4 h, a perp-vs-index trade; stated as a limit). **Double-cost check:** the same rows with another 0.20% deducted.
- **Splits:** train 2022, validation 2023-2024, test 2025 to 2026-10 by decision time. Single exit rule, so no horizon is picked on train: `core.screens.judge(rows, ["snap"], bounds)` with the data gate. Control, not part of the verdict: the horizon `timed` (same entry, always exit at 4 h) shows whether the premium-return exit adds anything.
- **Pass (every line on its own, Bonferroni across the 2 rules: alpha = 0.05 / 2 = 0.025):** `judge` verdict passed (validation mean > 0, without the best 1 and best 5 events still > 0, test mean not negative) AND validation block p (cluster = UTC day) < 0.025 AND double-cost validation mean > 0 AND >= 3 events a week over 2022-01 to 2026-10 AND data gate ok. **Kill:** `judge` killed, or double-cost validation mean <= 0, or < 3 events a week. Anything else is inconclusive. A pass needs a forward test before any use.
- **Known limits:** the open of the next 5-minute bar is optimistic in a dislocation (book thin, spread wide), and 0.05% slippage may be too low exactly there; premium index is Binance's own index gap, so a persistent gap (delisting, index source change) can look like a dislocation and never close (the 4 h exit then bounds the loss but not a gap through it); no leverage, margin or liquidation modelling; one exchange; events cluster in market-wide crashes (day-cluster bootstrap, but a few days may dominate: the summary reports the share of events on the busiest day and the top-5 symbol share); same-name spot requirement drops 1000-prefixed perps.

Splits: the first 40% / next 30% / last 30% of those markets by `closedTime`. Small, so expected to be inconclusive.

Kill: validation <= 0, p > 0.2 or double-cost or trimmed <= 0. Otherwise inconclusive. Per-split counts, losing events per split, and the 8 most extreme rows are printed and read before any verdict is believed.
- Known limits: trade prices are executed prices, not quotes (a basket needs every leg to trade within 30 minutes, which biases toward active events); Polymarket has thin books where our 10 USD may move the price; Kalshi candle quotes are end-of-hour snapshots without size; the sampled markets are those that resolved (open or voided markets are absent); Manifold is play money and is not tested; B5 is a one-prompt test.

A coin exists in the data only while its perp traded, so a short is always possible at the time. Sharded over symbols (default 20 shards; if a shard nears 4 h, rerun with more shards, no rule change).
- Data: hourly perp klines (monthly zips), `metrics` daily files (5-minute rows; only the on-the-hour snapshot of `sum_open_interest` in coins and `sum_open_interest_value` in USDT is used) and settled `fundingRate` monthly files. Window 2021-12 to 2026-10. BTCUSDT perp hours are the benchmark. Open interest is measured in contracts, not USD, so the price move itself cannot fake a drop.
- Event, decision time D = end of hour i (all inputs known at D): open interest at D <= 85% of open interest at D - 24 h (drop >= 15%) AND the hour-i close is >= 10% below (long) or >= 10% above (short) the close 24 h earlier. Filters: open interest value at D - 24 h >= 1,000,000 USDT; 720 prior contiguous hours with average hourly quote volume >= 200,000 USDT (hour i excluded). One row per coin per 168 hours (both sides share the cooldown). Rows with entry before 2022-01-01 are dropped.
- Entry: the open of the hour starting D + 1 h (one hour of delay for data publication and fill, stated as conservative; the 5-minute entry variant is not used). Exit: the close of the last hour of day 1, day 3 and day 7 held (d1, d3, d7).
- Return: long `close/entry - 1`, short `short_gain` (`1 - exit/entry`); minus the BTC perp's gain (short: BTC short gain) over the identical window; minus 0.4% round trip on the coin leg and 0.1% round trip on the BTC hedge leg; plus funding actually settled in the window (a long pays the rate, a short receives it; measured, not assumed). A hold with fewer than one settlement per 8 h of the window (missing funding file) is dropped, not treated as zero funding. BTC hedge funding is ignored (small approximation). A perp whose data ends before the exit while the data window continues (delisted or settled) closes at its last close, so delisting crashes stay in the sample; a hold that only runs past the 2026-10 data end is dropped. A zero open-interest snapshot is treated as missing. The unhedged result is reported as descriptive only.
- Splits: train 2022, validation 2023-2024, test 2025 to 2026-10. `core.screens.judge` with the trimmed pick (hold chosen on train without its best 5 events) and the data gate. Bonferroni across the 2 rules: validation block p (cluster = UTC day) must be < 0.05 / 2 = 0.025.
- Pass, per rule, all of: `judge` verdict passed at base cost (validation mean > 0, p < 0.05, still > 0 without the best 1 and 5 events, test mean > 0); validation p < 0.025; validation mean > 0 at double cost (another 0.4%); at least 3 events a week over 2022 to 2026-10. Kill: validation mean <= 0 or p > 0.2 or a loss without the best 1 or 5. Anything else is inconclusive. A pass needs a forward test before any use.
- Known limits: perp klines and a 1-hour entry grid stand in for fills (a cascade has wide spreads and the 1-hour delay may miss the bounce); the 5-minute metrics rows may be published late or missing (missing days are counted in `shard_gaps.metrics_days_failed` and just yield no event there); events cluster in market-wide crashes (the summary reports the maximum events on one day, and the BTC hedge removes only the common move); one exchange; metrics files only exist from late 2021 for many perps; tails (a short in a squeeze that keeps squeezing) are not capped by a stop. Thresholds were chosen once, not fitted; no other thresholds are run.
- Data gaps: a symbol whose klines or funding months failed after retries is dropped and counted (`klines_failed_symbols`, `funding_failed_symbols`), so a partial universe is visible in the summary.
- **Run 1 (2026-11-25, Actions 45515954945): both rules killed.** Data gate ok on both (no problems or warnings; 7,359 rows over 916 symbols, 252.1 weeks, 20 shards). **oi_flush_long** (5,991 events, 23.8 a week, 690 coins, at most 211 events on one day; train 878 / validation 2,208 / test 2,905, all unique): the train pick is d7 (after trimming); net mean per event validation **-1.519%** (p 0.945), test +0.816% (p 0.196), without the best 1 / 5 events validation -1.584% / -1.786%, double cost validation -1.919%; d1 -0.962% / -0.109% and d3 -0.125% / +1.664% (p 0.068) in validation / test; mean funding in the net +0.275%. Gates: judge not passed, validation p below 0.025 no, double-cost validation positive no, test positive yes, 3 a week yes. **oi_flush_short** (1,368 events, 5.4 a week, 507 coins; 200 / 427 / 741): the pick is d1; validation -0.924% (p 0.971), test -1.820%, double cost validation -1.324%; d3 and d7 also negative in validation and test; mean funding in the net -0.723%; gates all fail except 3 a week. The hedged result is the pre-registered one; the unhedged descriptive numbers do not help (long d3 test +2.165%, p 0.031, but validation +0.826% p 0.23 and train -2.58%). Plain reading: buying after a 15% open-interest collapse plus a 10% drop lost 1.5% a week later in 2023-24, and the squeeze short lost in both later splits; the cascade is not mean-reverting on this grid.


## factory_us_pit_1 screen: 4 US large-cap rules on a point-in-time S&P 500 (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any data was pulled)
Code `core/factory_us_pit.py`, study `factory_us_pit_1`. No LLM, no cost. Why: LESSONS says the binding constraint toward 3x/month is the number of independent bets a day; these four rules make many decisions a day from free data, on a point-in-time universe so survivorship cannot inflate them. None repeats a closed line (the closed 8-K/2.02 studies bought every filing; here the filings are filters, predicted dates or a mechanical reaction screen).
- **Universe:** tickers with an S&P 500 membership spell (fja05680 `sp500_ticker_start_end.csv`, `st.parse_membership`/`st.members_on`) overlapping 2016-11-01..2026-10-31, delisted names included. A name is usable on session D only if it is a member on D, has a bar on D and has a CIK in SEC `company_tickers.json`. Names without a CIK (mostly delisted or renamed tickers) are excluded from all four rules, because each needs 8-K data. Coverage per split = usable member-days / member-days; a split below 70% is flagged, and a rule cannot pass when validation or test is flagged.
- **Bars:** Yahoo daily chart with `period1`=2016-01-01, `period2`=2026-11-01, `events=div,split` (split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted OHLC; dividends and split dates from the same call), for members, SPY, ^VIX and XLK, XLF, XLV, XLY, XLP, XLE, XLI, XLB, XLU, XLRE, XLC. Lesson 9 check: a ticker whose median gap between bars exceeds 4 calendar days (monthly bars) is rejected. The session calendar is SPY's bars; session dates are New York dates.
- **SEC:** `data.sec.gov/submissions` for each CIK, including the older `files` pages (skipped when `filingTo` < 2016-09-01), fetched one at a time with the `TRADELAB_SEC_UA` secret, about 0.15 s apart, uncached, with retries. A CIK whose fetch fails is excluded and counted, never treated as "no 8-K". 8-K counts by year are reported so a missed page is visible. "Any 8-K" = forms 8-K and 8-K/A; a "2.02" = an 8-K or 8-K/A listing item 2.02 (filters), and B3/B4 events use form 8-K only.
- **Acceptance time convention (locked):** `acceptanceDateTime` is read as **New York wall-clock time** (the `Z` suffix is ignored). If the stamp is really UTC, this places every filing 4-5 h *later* than it happened, never earlier, so no rule can trade on a filing before it existed; the cost if wrong is that the no-news filters miss some late filings and B4's D can slip a session. The summary reports the histogram of stamp hours for 2.02s so the run shows which convention the data has.
- **Accounting (all rules):** each position has a fixed notional w (fraction of the book) set at entry. Its P&L on each session is w x (price change since the previous mark) / entry price, so over the hold it sums to w x (exit/entry - 1) for a long and w x (1 - exit/entry) for a short (short_gain). The SPY hedge is the same with notional w x beta, in the opposite direction. Cost w x c is charged at the entry session and at the exit session. A missing bar during a hold carries the last price (0 P&L); a name that stops trading exits at its last price. The daily series is the sum over open positions, with a value (0 when flat) for every session. Descriptive shorts pay 0.5%/yr borrow.
- **B1 residual_reversal_no_news (primary h2).** Signal on session D with closes through D. Eligible: member on D, close(D) >= $5, 20-day average dollar volume (D-19..D) >= $20M, known CIK, no 8-K accepted in [16:00 ET of D-2, 16:00 ET of D). Sector ETF = the ETF among the 11 SPDRs (each only with 60 full sessions of bars) with the highest correlation of daily returns over D-60..D-1; beta60 = OLS slope (with intercept) on it over the same 60 sessions; resid = r_stock(D) - beta60 x r_ETF(D); z = resid / sd of the OLS residuals over D-60..D-1 (n-2). The stock needs 60 full sessions of returns. Long the up to 10 lowest z with z <= -2.5; w = min(G/N, 10%), G = 1.0 if ^VIX(D) > median of ^VIX closes D-252..D-1 (252 needed), else 0.5. Hedge: beta_SPY60 (OLS on SPY, D-60..D-1) x w short SPY. Entry D+1 open, exit D+2 close (h1: D+1 close, h5: D+5 close, descriptive). Cohorts overlap (a new cohort each day), so the book can hold two cohorts (gross up to 2x G). Cost 0.05%/side, 2x check 0.10%. Short side (z >= +2.5, highest first) is descriptive. Control: the same number of names drawn at random from that day's eligible names with |z| < 0.5, same entry, exit, weights, hedge and costs.
- **B2 overnight_tug_of_war.** At session D, with sessions D-20..D-1 only: ON20 = sum of ln(open_t/close_{t-1}), ID20 = sum of ln(close_t/open_t), t = D-20..D-1; an overnight whose open is an ex-dividend date is left out of ON20. A name is dropped on D if a split date falls in D-20..D+1, or any of its bars D-21..D+1 is missing. Eligible: member on D, close(D-1) >= $10, average dollar volume D-20..D-1 >= $50M, known CIK, no 2.02 accepted in [16:00 ET of D-2, 16:00 ET of D) (the only locked form: a filing after D's close cannot be known in D's auction). Among eligible names with ID20 below the cross-sectional median, buy the 25 with the highest ON20 at D's close at 4% each (fewer if fewer qualify), sell all at the D+1 open; the D+1 open gets that day's dividend added back. Return = basket close-to-open minus SPY close-to-open on the same notional. Cost 0.03%/side, 2x check 0.06%. Control: the same number of names drawn at random from that night's eligible names.
- **B3 earnings_premium_predicted_date.** For member m and session D, using only 2.02 8-Ks accepted before 16:00 ET of D: for each past 2.02 with acceptance date f in [D-379, D-351] calendar days, P = f + 364 days, moved to the first session on or after P. m is a candidate on D if some P equals session D+5 and no 2.02 was accepted in [D - 60 calendar days, 16:00 ET of D). Buy at D's close. Exit at the close of the first session whose 09:30 ET open is after the first 2.02 8-K accepted at or after 16:00 ET of D, or at the close of D+10 (P+5 sessions) if that comes first. w = 5%, at most 20 open (a position exiting at D's close frees its slot for D), same-day candidates taken in ticker order. Return minus SPY over the same hold. Cost 0.05%/side, 2x 0.10%. Descriptive: only names in the top half (that day's candidates) by mean SPY-adjusted close-to-close return over their last 4 completed windows of this rule. Control (operational): for each rule trade, the same name and hold length L entered at the close of a random session D' within 252 sessions of D whose last 2.02 is 30-60 sessions before D' and with no 2.02 in D'..D'+L+1, same weight and cost, no capacity limit.
- **B4 earnings_reaction_drift_long (primary D+5).** Event: an 8-K with item 2.02 of a member; D = the first session whose 09:30 ET open is after acceptance (the same day if accepted before 09:30 ET). EAR = close_D/close_{D-1} - 1 - (the same for SPY). Long if EAR >= +5%, volume_D >= 3x the average of D-20..D-1, and (close-low)/(high-low) >= 0.6 on D (high > low). Enter at the D+1 open, exit at the D+5 close, or at the close of any session from D+1 on whose close is >= 6% below the entry price. w = 10%, at most 10 open (a position stopped at a close frees its slot from the next open), same-day events taken by EAR descending. Return minus SPY over the same hold. Cost 0.05%/side, 2x 0.10%. Descriptive: exits at D+10 and D+20 (same stop), and the mirrored short (EAR <= -5%, range position <= 0.4, stop 6% above entry). Control: for each rule trade, a random member on the same D with |EAR| < 1% and a bar on D, same entry, exit rule, weight and cost.
- **Splits:** train 2017-01-01..2019-12-31, validation 2020-01-01..2022-12-31, test 2023-01-01..2026-10-31 (by session date).
- **Pass (per rule, the 4 primaries only, Bonferroni across the batch):** validation mean daily return > 0 with moving-block bootstrap p (20-session blocks) < 0.05/4 = 0.0125; validation mean > 0 at double cost; test mean > 0; at least 3 entries a week on average over validation (B2: nights with a basket count as one entry each); validation and test coverage >= 70%. Anything else is "not passed". (c) B4 fills its slots at D's close by EAR order; a chosen event with no D+1 open stays in cash, and the next event does not take its slot. Its control is drawn the same way. ## factory_crypto_3 screen: 10 time-series trend and event rules on the six most liquid Binance USDT-M perps (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any data was pulled)
Code `core/factory_ts.py`, study `factory_crypto_3` (single job, no LLM). Why: the ~50 earlier public rules were almost all cross-sectional alt-coin reversal or momentum on daily bars. This family is the untested one: single-asset trend and event rules on BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP and DOGE, where taker cost is lowest and leverage is realistic. Judged as ONE family with `core.factory.judge_all` (Bonferroni bar 0.05 / 10 = 0.005).
- **Data:** data.binance.vision `futures/um` monthly 1h klines 2019-09-01..2026-10-31 (the first months are indicator warm-up only; a symbol with no file yet is simply not traded) and monthly `fundingRate` (settled, measured). Missing hourly bars are filled flat from the previous close and counted. No other data.
- **Accounting (all rules):** the book is split in six equal slots (1/6 of equity per symbol, fixed even before a coin lists). A rule sets a position p in [-1.5, 1.5] of its slot at the close of a bar and earns the next hour's close-to-close return. Cost 0.05% taker + 0.02% slippage = 0.07% per unit of |change in p|, each side. The measured funding rate is charged at each settlement on the position held across it (long pays when the rate is positive, short receives). Double-cost check: trading cost x2 (measured funding unchanged). Hourly portfolio returns are summed over symbols x 1/6 and summed to UTC days.
- **Splits (UTC):** train 2020-01-01..2023-12-31, validation 2024-01-01..2024-12-31, test 2025-01-01..2026-10-31. Block bootstrap (`core.factory.block_p`, 20-day blocks, one-sided mean <= 0) on daily returns.
- **Rules, every parameter fixed here** (decide at the close of the stated bar, trade at that close; a stop is checked on closes only):
  1. `don_1h_55`: 1h bars. Long when close > highest high of the previous 55 bars; short when close < lowest low of the previous 55. Exit on close beyond the opposite 20-bar channel, or close 3 x ATR(14, simple mean of true range) against the entry close. Size 1.
  2. `don_4h_20`: same on 4h bars (UTC 00/04/08..), entry 20, exit 10, stop 3 x ATR(14).
  3. `don_4h_55`: 4h bars, entry 55, exit 20, stop 3 x ATR(14).
  4. `ma_4h_20_100`: 4h bars, long when SMA20 > SMA100 of closes, else short, always in the market. Size = min(1.5, 0.40 / annualised vol), vol = stdev of the last 180 4h log returns x sqrt(6 x 365). Resize only when the new size differs by more than 25% from the held size, or on a flip.
  5. `ma_1d_20_100`: the same on UTC daily bars (SMA20 / SMA100, vol from the last 30 daily returns x sqrt(365)).
  6. `wick_long_4h`: 1h bar with (open - low) / open >= 3 x sigma and close above its midpoint (high + low) / 2, sigma = stdev of the previous 168 hourly log returns. Long at that close, hold 4 bars, ignore new signals while in the trade. Size 1.
  7. `wick_long_24h`: the same signal, hold 24 bars.
  8. `wick_short_4h`: mirror: (high - open) / open >= 3 x sigma and close below the midpoint; short, hold 4 bars.
  9. `weekly_open_break`: 1h bars; W = open of the first bar of the UTC week (Monday 00:00), R = high - low of the previous full week. First close in the week with close >= W + 0.5 R goes long, close <= W - 0.5 R goes short (one entry per week and symbol); exit when the close crosses back through W, or flat at the last close of the week. Size 1.
  10. `trend_pullback_4h`: 4h bars, SMA200 and RSI(14, Wilder). Long when close > SMA200 and RSI < 35; exit when RSI > 55, close < SMA200, 42 bars held, or close 3 x ATR(14) below entry. Short is the mirror (close < SMA200, RSI > 65; exit RSI < 45, close > SMA200, 42 bars, or 3 x ATR above). Size 1.
- **Pass (per rule, the 10 only):** validation mean daily return > 0 with block p < 0.005; validation mean > 0 at double cost; test mean > 0; at least 3 entries a week on average over validation (an entry = flat to position, or a flip, summed over the six symbols). Anything else is "not passed". No rule is re-tuned after seeing a result; a variant needs a new dated entry and counts as a new family.

## indicators_1 screen: eight classic indicators on crypto perps, gold and FX (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any bar was read for this study)

- **Cell** = indicator x market class x timeframe, symbols pooled into one equal-slot portfolio, long and short combined, one parameter set per indicator, nothing tuned. Markets: `crypto` = BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, DOGE USDT-M perps (6 slots of 1/6, a slot is flat before its coin lists); `fx` = EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD (4 slots); `gold` = GC=F as XAUUSD proxy (1 slot); `etf` = SPY, QQQ, IWM. Timeframes 15m, 1h, 4h (built from 1h, UTC-aligned blocks; crypto needs all source bars, Yahoo at least half), 1d (crypto only, from 1h).
- **Rules, standard parameters, decided at the bar close and filled at the NEXT bar's open:** (1) `rsi`: RSI(14, Wilder); long when RSI crosses below 30, exit at RSI >= 50; short when it crosses above 70, exit at <= 50. (2) `macd`: MACD(12,26,9) histogram flipping positive = long, negative = short, always reversing. (3) `bollinger`: Bollinger(20, 2); long when the close crosses below the lower band, exit at the middle band; short when it crosses above the upper band. (4) `stochastic`: Stochastic(14,3) raw %K with %D = SMA3; long when %K crosses above %D while %K < 20, exit at %K >= 50; short when %K crosses below %D while > 80, exit at <= 50. (5) `supertrend`: Supertrend(ATR 10 Wilder, x3) flip = reverse. (6) `adx`: ADX(14) Wilder; long on the bar where ADX > 25 and +DI > -DI becomes true, exit when ADX < 20 or -DI > +DI; short mirrored. (7) `vwap`: session VWAP (UTC day, volume weighted typical price); close crossing above = long, below = short, reversing; no entry before 2 hours of the session have closed; flat at the last bar of each session. (8) `ichimoku`: (9,26,52); cloud at bar i = spans computed 26 bars earlier; long on the bar where close > cloud top and tenkan > kijun becomes true, exit when tenkan < kijun; short mirrored.
- **Common exits (all rules):** a close-based stop 2 x ATR(14) from the entry close, a time exit after 48 bars, the rule's own exit. There is no profit target, so there is no stop-versus-target ordering; a bar that stops out does not re-enter. Size is 1 slot, no pyramiding, no leverage in the judged series.
- **Costs:** crypto 0.05% taker + 0.02% slippage per side and the measured funding of the position held across each settlement. gold/FX: `core.broker_costs.xm_cfd_cost_fraction(symbol, "standard", price)` `trade_per_side` (spread only, no commission) on every change of position, plus the 3% a year swap estimate on the time held (weekend gaps included). 2x cost stress: per-side cost x2 and swap x2 (measured crypto funding unchanged). Control, reported not judged: every position sign-flipped at the same costs.

Constants: annual volatility crypto 0.60, fx 0.08, gold 0.16, etf 0.16; round trip crypto 0.14%, fx 0.0002/1.10, gold 0.55/3500 (XM Standard rows of `core/broker_costs.py`), etf 0.08% (assumption); leverage cap crypto 12 (the repo's LEVS ceiling), fx 30 and gold 20 (ESMA-type caps, `research/brokers/costs.md` section 2), etf 5 (assumption); bars a year in the module.
