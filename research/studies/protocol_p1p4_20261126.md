## P1. Universe, data, splits (as D1)
Every Binance USDT-M perp that ever had monthly 1-minute klines on data.binance.vision, delisted included, ticker `...USDT` without underscore, minus the 30 majors of `core.d1_liq.MAJORS` (888 symbols in D1).
Months 2023-12 to 2026-10 (2023-12 history only). Splits by the fill bar open: train 2024-01-01..2025-07-01, validation 2025-07-01..2026-03-01, test 2026-03-01..2026-10-29 (`core.d1_liq.BOUNDS`).
A bar is usable for an order only if the trailing 24 h quote volume up to the close of bar t-2 is >= 5,000,000 USDT (the D1 tradeability floor).

## P1. Order and fill model (conservative; fixed here)
- Reference price = close of 1-minute bar t-2. A resting order is live for exactly bar t. Bid at ref x (1 - d), long; ask at ref x (1 + d), short. d in {3%, 5%, 8%}. Both sides are independent orders (a bar may fill both).
- **A fill counts only if bar t trades THROUGH the limit by at least 0.1%**: bid needs low <= limit x (1 - 0.001); ask needs high >= limit x (1 + 0.001); touching or reaching the limit is not a fill. And the bar's quote volume >= 5,000 USDT.
  A bar that opens already beyond the limit is still filled at the limit (never at the better price). Fill price = limit, always.
- Entry fee maker 0.02%. No entry slippage (passive). Exit is an adverse-selection exit: a taker order at the open of bar t+1+hold (<= 3 bars late if a bar is missing; else the fill is dropped and counted), taker fee 0.05% plus slippage 0.15%.
  **Cost (all judged numbers) = 0.02% + 0.05% + 0.15% = 0.22% round trip, plus funding 0.01%.** The D1 taker cost (0.40% + 0.01%) is reported as a stress column. The doubled-cost check of the criteria is `net - cost` (as `core.sweep_stats`).
- Stop: 5% adverse from the fill price, checked on every 1-minute bar from the fill bar through the bar before the exit (the fill bar's own low/high counts as happening after the fill: pessimistic); stop fill = stop + 0.5% slippage (gross = -5.5%).
  MFE/MAE: on the fill bar only the close counts for MFE (pessimistic) and the low/high for MAE; later bars use high/low.
- Holds 1, 5, 15 minutes after the end of the fill bar. One position per symbol and cell at a time (a later fill before the exit is skipped).
- **Cells: d {3,5,8}% x hold {1,5,15} min x side {bid, ask} = 18 judged rules** (`d3|h1m|bid` ...). One family, Holm over the stage-0 survivors.
- **Cluster = all fills of one UTC hour** in the cell: the unit is the equal-weight mean of those fills' gross / net / MFE / MAE. Account weight lev 3, k 1 in the gate arithmetic (as the D1 sweep). N = clusters per 30.4 days, s = mean / sd of cluster nets, x = N s^2.

## P1. Controls (same stop, hold, side)
- **Reversed-side placebo** (the sweep gate's own): `-gross - cost + fund` of the same fills.
- **Late taker (D1 rule on the same bars)**: for every fill, a market entry at the open of bar t+1 (60 s after the move is known), same side, same exit time and stop, cost 0.40% + 0.01%. The resting fill must beat it by >= 0.20% a fill (train mean of net - taker net).
- **Random control**: per fill, 3 random bar opens of the same symbol in the same calendar month (seed `20261126:<symbol>:<fill ts>`), same side, hold and stop, no wick condition, entered at the bar open, costed like the rule (0.22% + funding); needs >= 2 usable. The rule's train mean must exceed the control mean.

## P1. Kill list and stage 0 (train only; `core.sweep_stats.gate` unchanged, cluster level)
Kill a cell if any holds: train cluster n < 30; train net mean <= 0 or <= the placebo; volatility/cost < 10; cluster-level x < 0.2; all-win ceiling < 3x a month; train mean not above the random-control mean;
mean(net - late-taker net) on train fills < 0.20%; more than 50% of train profit (sum of fill nets) comes from the best 1% of train fills.
**Audit kill (whole direction):** an aggTrades audit of a hash-chosen sample of fills (every fill of the hold-5 cells with sha1(`sym:bar:d:side`) mod 20 = 0, the lowest 40 hashes per shard, 16 shards) counts a fill as supported when prints in the fill minute at a price through the limit by 0.1% sum to >= 150 USD (5 clips of 30 USD).
If more than 20% of sampled fills are unsupported, every cell is killed. A failed download is a gap, reported, not "unsupported".
Post-gate criteria (Holm over the survivors, alpha 0.05, validation block-bootstrap p): validation and test net > 0; both > 0 at doubled cost; validation n >= 30 and test n >= 20 clusters; >= 70% of 6-month folds positive; last-12-month double-cost mean > 0;
validation and test mean above the random-control mean and above the late-taker net. A cell passes only if every criterion holds. Not found is a valid result.

## P1. Reporting
Per cell and split (train / validation / test): clusters n, mean, sd, s, N a month, x, hit rate; fills n and mean; gross; placebo; random control; late taker; mean at the D1 taker cost; stop share; top-1% share; late-taker gap; audit result (supported share, by d);
daily correlation (by exit day, cluster net) with the L1f component of the event book (frozen forced-flow rows `l1f_ref|h4h|d1m`); counts: fills, fills skipped (busy, no exit bar), symbols with fills; failed months. Rules tested, rules passed, closest cell. One run and at most one dated fix (a data or parser bug); no cell, threshold or cost change after any outcome.
A pass is proposed to the event book by a dated addendum file `research/event_book/components_addendum_<date>.json` in a separate PR; the locked components file is not edited. A forward OKX book/liquidation tape starts only if a cell clears this backtest.
