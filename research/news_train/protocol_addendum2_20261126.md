**v3** states the learned rules (crypto delist, removal and monitoring-tag short for 1 h and 1 d, none for 5 m or when the move before entry is below -8%; funding-interval and other non-listing short for 1 d; listings none; stock none unless an unmistakable large surprise). **v4** is v3 with analogs. **Selection and lock rule (fixed now).** The version that goes to the sealed run is the one with the highest clean-validation score per offered item on the primary cell (stock, 1 d); ties go to the lower version number. If every version scores at or below 0 there, the lowest-numbered of those with the highest score is still locked, because the owner asked for one sealed opening, and the verdict is read exactly as the protocol states it (unread below 100 traded items, otherwise the three conditions). Crypto cells are reported and are not part of the selection.
