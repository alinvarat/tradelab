## Defect found while planning (disclosed, round 1 not edited)
`core.factory_us_pit.accept_ts` reads the SEC `acceptanceDateTime` ("...22:26:14.000Z") as New York wall-clock, which moved every filing 4-5 hours later (locked convention of the key-person study). The stamp is UTC: accession 0001193125-26-478697 reads 22:26:14Z in the submissions API and 22:26:14 UTC in the Atom feed of the forward collector. Round 1's "38% of filings are in-session" used the shifted time, so its in-session/after-hours split was wrong; it never looked ahead (filings were treated as later than they were). Round 2 recovers the true UTC time (`logic_r2.fix_ts`, tested).

## Items
- **Stock:** SEC 8-K (not 8-K/A) of a point-in-time S&P 500 member (same list as round 1), items in `params.stock_items` or none listed; text is the Ex-99 press release when the filing has one, else the filing body (round 1's `filing_text`, with its redaction; text of a filing already fetched in round 1 is reused from `data/news-train`). Stand-alone wire press releases have no free historical archive; they exist only in the forward collector (branch `data/news-latency-fwd`), whose rows are not part of any scored set here.
- **Crypto:** GDELT GKG 2.1 files (`data.gdeltproject.org/gdeltv2/<stamp>.gkg.csv.zip`). Only every 4th 15-minute file (the hourly file) is read (`gdelt.stride`), fixed before any download; the file stamp is `t_seen` (when the row was available), never the article time. A row's `PAGE_TITLE` is the text. A headline is kept when exactly one listed coin is named and the headline has crypto context (word list or theme in params); spam domains and 13F-style titles are dropped; syndicated copies are removed (same normalised title, or word-set Jaccard >= 0.8 for the same coin within 6 h, keep the earliest); at most 6 rows per coin and UTC day by sha order. Coin names, symbols and dates are replaced in the analyst text; the source domain is shown.

## Labels (identical in validation and sealed)
- **Stock, 1-hour Yahoo bars (about 730 days back):** entry = open of the first bar that starts at or after `t_pub + 93 s + 5 s` (the next bar); a filing after the last bar start or before the first enters at the next session open. Holds: `1h` (open to close of the entry bar; none for the half-hour last bar) and `eod` (to the close of the entry session). Recorded and shown to the analyst: `in_session`, minutes of delay to entry, `pre_move` (entry open over the open of the hour that contained the filing, or the opening gap), `day_move` (over the previous close), 5-session run-up. 93 s is the proxy of round 1; the 5-minute bars (about 60 days) are archived for the forward set and are secondary, descriptive, never in a gate.
- **Crypto:** Binance Vision 1-minute bars (USDT-M perpetual, else spot; monthly files, daily files for the current month); entry = last 1-minute close at or before `t_seen + 5 s`; holds 5 m, 1 h, 1 d; recorded `pre_lag` and `pre_1h`.
- **Costs:** as round 1 (stock 0.09% a side plus 0.02% borrow on shorts, crypto 0.40% round trip; 2x case reported). Metric, baselines (abstain, random expectation, keyword rule), day-clustered bootstrap: as round 1.
- **Primary cell:** one per market, `1h` (stock and crypto), the same in validation and sealed. Everything else is reported, not gated.

## Calibrated abstention (w1-w3)
The analyst gives a signed expected move in percent per hold. A hold trades only when its direction agrees with the sign of that estimate and `|estimate| >= theta`. theta per (market, hold) is fitted on **train only** as the grid value (`select.theta_grid_pct`) with the highest mean net P&L per offered item subject to at least `theta_min_trades` trades (stock 25, crypto 60); if none qualifies the hold never trades. theta is stored in `calib.json` and applied unchanged to validation and to every sealed set. w0 has no calibration.

## Selection and lock (fixed now)
On clean validation, primary cell, per market: a version is eligible if it has at least `min_traded_val` traded items (stock 30, crypto 100); the lock is the eligible version with the highest score per offered item (ties: lower number). If none is eligible the market gets no lock and its sealed set stays unopened (reported as "no candidate"). Locks are written to `LOCK_R2.json` (version, prompt sha256) before a sealed run.

## Sealed sets
1. **Crypto GDELT 2026-08-01..2026-11-26: opened once** by `sealed_crypto` with the locked crypto version; labels are computed only inside that run; the run refuses if `sealed_r2/crypto_result.json` exists. Opening it does not touch the stock forward set.
2. **Stock forward, S&P 500 8-Ks accepted at or after 2026-11-27 00:00 UTC**, built by the scheduled job from EDGAR (the pod's `/api/news_latency` ingest log is behind Cloudflare and not reachable from Actions; the `data/news-latency-fwd` rows are collector-start `cold` rows or press releases without tickers and are not used). Same universe, text, redaction, 93 s proxy entry and costs as validation. Schedule: `news_train_r2.yml` `fwd` mode on cron 40 22 * * 1-5 and dispatched by the keeper. Each run adds new items, snapshots the Yahoo 1-hour and 5-minute bars of their symbols, and, once `LOCK_R2.json` names a stock version, caches that version's decision on each item (a decision is made once per item and never changed). **Outcomes are not computed** until the trigger: the number of items that have settled (entry bar exists, session closed) and carry a non-abstain decision on the primary hold, `traded`, reaches **100** (`done.min_traded`). `progress.json` publishes counts only. At the trigger the labels and the result are computed once and written to `fwd/result.json`; later runs refuse to rewrite it. A trigger that depends only on decisions cannot be steered by outcomes.
3. Verdict for either set = round 1's four conditions on the primary cell: at least 100 traded; mean net per traded > 0; paired difference against the random and the keyword baselines positive with Holm-adjusted one-sided p < 0.05 (m above); still > 0 at 2x cost. Below 100 traded the verdict is "unread".

## What this cannot show
GDELT hourly files sample the stream and stamp rows later than publication; costs are assumed; the stock 93 s entry is a proxy and 1-hour bars place the entry up to an hour after an in-session filing (the pre-entry move records how much of the reaction that includes); wire press releases are not in the historical sets; the same period and the same S&P 500 filers are reused from round 1, so stock gains would be weaker evidence than the forward set.
