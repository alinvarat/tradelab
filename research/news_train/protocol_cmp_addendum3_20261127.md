# CMP addendum 3 2026-11-27 (written before any wide-arm item was selected, decided on or scored; no forward stock outcome of any arm has been read)

`protocol_cmp_20261126.md`, `params_cmp.json`, addenda 1 and 2, round 2 (`protocol_r2_20261126.md`, `LOCK_R2.json`, `params_r2.json`), round 3 and the ingest addendum stand unchanged. The S&P 500 forward stock arm of the comparison keeps its rule, its decisions and its gate (stock outcomes closed until round 2's `fwd/result.json` exists). This addendum adds a **separate** arm; nothing is pooled with the S&P 500 arm.

**Evidence for the bottleneck (read from the repo and the data branches, 2026-11-27).**
1. The forward stock set of the comparison counted 0 items because (a) the logged round-2 forward run (45940440565) ran at 04:31 UTC on the first forward day, before any US filing, after Thanksgiving closed EDGAR on 2026-11-26, so "fwd candidates 0" says nothing about the rate; (b) `data/news-ingest` holds 0 `sec_8k` item rows although the pod's `sec_8k` source is enabled and polling (health row: 361 polls, 358 ok, 98 cold items, 0 new items), because the logged window (2026-11-26 00:00 to 2026-11-27 10:04 UTC) contained no EDGAR filing day. The pod-row join (`sec:<accession>` keys on both sides, same dashed format) is therefore **unverified**, not shown broken.
2. Rate of the registered universe: the round-2 stock build found 858 + 752 + 929 + 785 = 3,324 S&P 500 8-K candidates with a `stock_items` code over 2025-12-01..2026-06-30, about 23 per trading day (my count: 145 trading days). At 100% pod coverage that is about 13 trading days to the registered 300 items.
3. The gate is slower than the count: the comparison's stock scoring waits for round 2's forward result, which fires at 100 traded items (`done.min_traded`). Validation `w3` traded 37 of 788 offered at 1 h (4.7%, `evals/w3_validation.json`), so about 2,100 items, about 93 trading days at 23 a day. Widening the S&P 500 arm cannot change this and is not attempted.

**Change.** A forward stock-wide arm (`arm = wide`) built from the pod's own EDGAR rows, so the pod join is automatic. Parameters in `params_cmp_a3.json` (sha256 `7cf8d1e175605d41c726304709e591effd6c013a5e536edaccc155ee6eff9387`). Candidates: `sec_8k` rows with key `sec:<accession>`, title form exactly `8-K` (not 8-K/A), published at or after 2026-11-27 00:00 UTC. A UTC day is selected once, 3 hours after it closed: candidates are ranked by sha256 of `seed:key` and tried in that order until 60 are admitted or 400 were tried. A candidate is admitted when its CIK has an SEC ticker, no ticker of the issuer is a point-in-time S&P 500 member on the filing's New York date (the round-2 universe stays out, so the arms are disjoint), the filing has a `params_r2.stock_items` code or lists none, and its text has at least 80 characters (round-1 `filing_text`, redaction as in round 2). Selection reads no decision and no outcome. Prompts, heads (`w3` direction, `m1` magnitude), both models, identical prompt bytes, decide-once, 93 s lag plus the pod's real seen-minus-published time plus each model's call time, hourly Yahoo bars, costs and the 1 h hold are the comparison's own. Only `descr` changes, to "Issuer: a US-listed company (sector: ...)" (the registered text says "large"). Items without an entry bar after 5 days are skipped and counted, never imputed.

**Scoring gate of this arm.** Its own: counted settled items (both models answered `w3` and `m1`, entry bar at least 2 days old) reach 300 (`min_n`), or the hard stop 2027-03-31 (then marked underpowered). One shot, `fwd/result_stock_wide.json`: `m1` partial rank correlation difference (headline) and `w3` paired direction (descriptive), as for the S&P 500 arm. The round-2 gate is not applied because no wide item is a round-2 item, round 2 reads nothing from the comparison's branch, and the S&P 500 arm's outcomes stay closed. The wide result is never read into round 2's forward result, its Holm family or its verdict.

**Limits.** The wide universe includes small and thin issuers: the registered cost of 0.09% a side is optimistic for them, `w3` analogs and `m1` were fitted on S&P 500 items, and there is no size filter, so a result describes Haiku versus Sonnet on this universe only and says nothing about round 2. The cap of 60 a day bounds Claude calls on the shared plan (4 per item). Selection happens once per day, so the first wide items appear after the first closed forward day. The pod row for an item requires the pod to have been up when the filing appeared (cold-start rows are not logged); items the pod missed are absent from the arm, not imputed. Rate in practice is unmeasured until the first closed day with filings has been read.
