# Round 3 addendum B, 2026-11-26: Track B source and design (written from probe counts only, before any Track B item was built, labelled or sent to Haiku)

Probe runs 45809153701 and 45810303051 (`probe_b.py`; counts, statuses and title samples only, no return, no reply). Findings:
- **B1 fails its gate.** GlobeNewswire answers 403 to every URL from Actions (search by date, RSS, both sitemaps); the Wayback CDX was unavailable (503, 504, connection refused) for all six day-prefix queries; PR Newswire serves only its current list, RSS and a rolling sitemap, with no dated archive of 6 months.
- **B1b not verified.** The EDGAR daily index is reachable (169 to 324 8-Ks per day on four sample days, above the 100 gate), but Yahoo answered 429 to all 30 bar-coverage calls (5-minute and 1-hour, 15 small-cap tickers), so small-cap bar coverage and the 2,000-item estimate cannot be shown. B1b stays a later candidate, not run here.
- **B2 passes its gate.** The GDELT translingual GKG (`<stamp>.translation.gkg.csv.zip`) is reachable for 2025-12-15, 2026-03-01..04 and 2026-07-15 (4-15 MB per file), carries the original-script title and a language code (`srclc:kor`, `zho`; 8 to 100 Korean and 16 to 360 Chinese titles per 15-minute file). Coin-mapped Korean titles appeared in 4 of 9 sampled files (about 40 a day extrapolated, above the 200-a-week gate). The probe's first run missed CJK because titles are HTML-entity encoded (fixed with unescape, probe bug disclosed). RSS feeds (blockmedia.co.kr, panewslab.com) answer with timestamps but keep about 10-100 items, so they are forward-only and not used.

**Chosen: B2.** Source: translation GKG rows with `srclc` in {kor, zho}, every 15-minute file; the file stamp is `t_seen`. Coin map: params `coins` (names in Korean and Chinese plus latin names; exactly one coin per headline; longer names first so "bitcoin cash" is not read as bitcoin); dedupe by character-bigram Jaccard >= 0.8 within 6 h per coin; at most 6 rows per coin and UTC day by sha order; coin names, symbols and dates redacted. Splits by `t_pub`: train 2025-12-01..2026-03-31, validation 2026-04-01..2026-06-30, **sealed 2026-07-01..2026-11-26** (the most recent period, untouched by any round for this source); samples 1,500 / 1,500 / 2,500 by sha. Kuber pod forward items: not reachable from Actions (round-2 protocol, unchanged), reported as a blocker. Labels, entry (t_seen + 5 s), holds, costs (crypto 0.40% round trip, 2x case; the 20-coin universe of round 2 is a subset of the map) and Binance Vision bars as round 2. Prompt: the registered round-2 version w1 (surprise vs expected, already-priced, calibrated abstention; theta fitted on train, floors as round 2), read-only from `versions_r2.json`; K = 1, so the Holm family is m = 3 x 1 = 3. Baselines: abstain, random direction, and a keyword rule with a Korean, Chinese and English list fixed in params. Selection: w1 is locked for the sealed set only if validation 1 h has at least 100 traded items; otherwise "no candidate" and the sealed set stays unopened (`LOCK_R3B.json`, written from `selection.json`). Done condition: round 2's four conditions on the 1 h cell (at least 100 traded, mean net per trade > 0, Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 against random and keyword, positive at 2x cost). The size estimate (Track A m1) is not run on this track. Opened once; the run refuses a second.
