## Track A: magnitude, not direction
**Task.** For each item the analyst estimates the absolute 1-hour move after entry (`abs1h`, percent) and the probability that it exceeds `big_mult` x the round-trip cost (`p_big`; stock 0.36%, crypto 0.80%). No direction is asked. Target: `y = |gross 1h return|` with the round-2 labels (stock: entry at the first hourly bar at or after publication + 98 s, 1h = entry bar open to close; crypto: Binance Vision 1-minute entry at `t_seen` + 5 s, 1 h hold). Primary hold: 1 h in both markets. Stock items whose entry bar is the half-hour last bar have no 1 h target and are excluded (counted).

**Data.** Development = the labelled round-2 items (round-2 train 2025-12-01..2026-03-31, round-2 validation 2026-04-01..2026-06-30; stock 1,200 + 796, crypto 1,500 + 1,500), used read-only from `data/news-train-r2/dev.jsonl.gz`. Sealed = items no round (1, 2, or the round-2 forward set) has scored, all later than development: stock S&P 500 8-Ks accepted 2026-07-01..2026-07-31 (the "buffer" month every earlier round skipped; sha-ordered cap 700), crypto GDELT headlines of 2026-07 (cap 1,000) plus the unpicked remainder of the round-2 sealed-period pool (2026-08-01..2026-11-26 rows not in `crypto_sealed_items.jsonl.gz`, by key and by normalised title). Construction, redaction, dedupe, per-coin-day cap and stock text are round 2's (`run_r2.py`, unchanged). Sealed items are stored without bars, labels or features; those are computed only inside the sealed job. Dev and sealed keys must be disjoint (asserted).

**Baselines, all known at entry.** (1) keyword rule: count of round-2 keyword-list hits (direction ignored); (2) recent realized volatility `rv`: stock = root-mean-square open-to-close return of the 35 hourly bars before the entry bar (at least 14); crypto = sqrt(sum of squared 1-minute log returns over the 24 h before entry / 24) (at least 1,000 minutes); (3) pre-entry move `pre`: stock |opening gap, or move from the start of the filing hour to entry|, crypto |move over the hour before entry|. Rows lacking `rv` or `pre` are excluded and counted.

**Versions (K = 3, registered; m3 would need a dated addendum written after the train-only error analysis and before its first call).** m0: plain size estimate. m1: m0 + pre-entry move + the typical hourly move (`rv` shown). m2: m1 + 5 analogs closed strictly before `t_pub` with their absolute moves (pool = development items). Each version is called once per development item; no repeated draws.

**Statistics (day-clustered bootstrap over UTC days, one-sided; 500 resamples on development, 2,000 on sealed).** Score = the analyst's `abs1h`. T1 (primary, "adds information beyond volatility"): partial Spearman of the score with y given `rv` and `pre` (rank-transformed, linear residuals) > 0. T2: Spearman(score, y) minus Spearman(keyword, y) > 0. T3: Spearman(score, y) minus Spearman(pre, y) > 0. Descriptive, never gated: raw Spearman of every score and of `rv`, AUC for `y > threshold` (from `p_big` and `abs1h`), the top-third-by-score versus the rest (mean y, share above threshold), and the partial rho of `rv` given the score. **Holm family:** m = 3 x K x 2 markets = 18; untested hypotheses count as p = 1.

**Selection (fixed now, validation only, per market).** Eligible: at least 300 scorable validation rows, at least 95% valid replies, partial rho (T1 point estimate) at least 0.03. Lock = the eligible version with the highest partial rho (tie: lower number). No eligible version gives "no candidate": that market's sealed set stays unopened. Locks are written to `LOCK_R3.json` (version, prompt sha256) before the sealed run, by the selection output only. Error analysis (`run_r3.py analyze`) is on TRAIN only, by item class, rv tercile, pre-entry class, in/out of session and coin.

**Sealed set: opened once** by `sealed` with `LOCK_R3.json`; the run refuses if `sealed_r3/result.json` exists. **Done condition per market:** at least 300 scorable sealed rows, and T1, T2, T3 each with a Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 and a positive point estimate. Below 300 rows the verdict is "unread". Meeting it would only make the score a candidate filter or sizing input: any use as a filter must separately pass the event-book gates (not claimed here). Disclosure: the sealed crypto remainder shares its period with round 2's sealed pool, whose direction results are public; rv and pre are computed from bars before entry only.

## Track B: low-attention sources (candidate order and feasibility gates fixed now; the source and universe are fixed by a dated addendum written from probe counts, before any item is labelled)
Candidates in order: **B1** US micro and small-cap press releases on GlobeNewswire and PR Newswire; **B1b** (only if B1 fails its gate) small-cap 8-K Ex-99 press releases from EDGAR (same wire text, SEC UTC acceptance time) on companies outside the S&P 500; **B2** non-English crypto news: GDELT translingual GKG titles (Korean, Chinese) and timestamped RSS of Korean/Chinese outlets, mapped to coins with Binance Vision 1-minute bars. Probe (`probe_b.py`, run `probe_b`) records reachability, status, coverage counts, title/timestamp samples and 5-minute/1-hour bar counts on a fixed ticker sample; no return and no Haiku reply is read. Gates (all must hold): B1 archived release pages with publication timestamp and a ticker for at least 6 months at 20 or more releases a day in the CDX sample; B1b the EDGAR daily index reachable with 100 or more 8-Ks a day and an estimated 2,000 or more eligible small-cap items in 2025-12..2026-06; B2 at least 200 coin-mapped CJK or translated titles per week with a source timestamp, and a 6-month history (RSS alone, which has no archive, gives forward items only and cannot pass). If no candidate passes, Track B is reported "infeasible with free archives" and the blocker named.

**Fixed for whichever candidate runs.** Task: the same direction-plus-calibrated-abstention reading as round 2 (versions fixed in the addendum from the registered round-2 w1 prompt, no new prompt tuning) and the Track A size estimate (m1) as a secondary. Bars: Yahoo 1-hour for stocks (entry at the first bar at or after publication + 98 s; the 5-minute archive covers only about 60 days and is used for descriptive checks only), Binance Vision 1-minute for coins. Splits by `t_pub`: train and validation inside 2025-12-01..2026-06-30 (about 2:1 by date), sealed = the most recent untouched period 2026-07-01..2026-11-26 for the chosen source (Kuber pod forward items only if reachable; the round-2 protocol already records that the pod API is not reachable from Actions, so they are reported as unreachable). Costs (haircut, labelled as such because no free source gives historical quotes): small-cap stocks, long-only (borrow assumed unavailable), price at least 1 USD, prior 20-session average dollar volume at least 250,000 USD; round trip by that dollar volume: at least 5 M 0.6%, 1 M to 5 M 1.2%, 0.25 M to 1 M 2.4%, plus the 2x case. Coins: 0.40% round trip as round 2; coins outside the 20 listed in round 2 are excluded unless the addendum adds them with a higher haircut. Metric: net P&L per offered item with abstain = 0 against random-direction and keyword baselines, day-clustered bootstrap, Holm family fixed in the addendum (m = 3 x the versions registered for Track B), at least 100 traded items in the sealed set, positive at 2x cost. The sealed set is opened once.

## What this cannot show
Both tracks use public text that other participants also read; the size estimate may only recover what realized volatility and the pre-entry move already contain. GDELT stamps are later than publication, the stock 98 s entry is a proxy, and 1-hour bars put in-session entries up to an hour after the release. Track B costs are assumed. A positive Track A result is a filter candidate, not a trading edge.
