## 1. Data and what is already known (stated, not hidden)
Opened data: the HH set (3,167 items, labels in artifact `hh-labels` of run 46002848826, Haiku w3 decisions cached under `hh/decisions/` of `data/news-train-hh`). The aggregate Haiku w3 result on the whole set and per calendar month (Aug +0.097%, Sep -0.066%, Oct +0.025%, Nov +0.014% per offered item; +0.0297% overall, vs random +0.0448%, p 0.104) was published in RESULTS.md **before** this split exists. So the validation split is blind to every candidate's own results and to every fitted rule, but **not** blind to the baseline w3 aggregate. This is the same kind of confession round 1 had to make; it is made here first.

## 2. Family and budget
Family number **k = 62** (HH was 61; `research/studies/FAMILIES.md` does not exist yet). alpha_62 = 0.10 / (62 x 63) = 2.56e-5. This family does **not** nominate anything under rule H: with a validation third the power arithmetic (about 0.06% standard error per offered item) cannot reach alpha_62. Its only output is a **routing** decision (section 5), which is not evidence of an edge. Every version tried is counted in the result: the greedy rule search counts each scored option (`evaluations`, train side), the candidates scored on validation (at most 3) and the prompts called (at most 1) count as versions tried; Holm over the validation candidates uses m = 3.

## 3. Split (mechanical)
Temporal by UTC day of `t_pub`: the last ceil(D / 3) distinct days (D = 80, so 27 days) are **validation**, the rest **train** (`logic_w4.split_rows`). `stage` writes both on `data/news-train-w4`. The `analyze` and `fit` jobs restore only `w4/train` and refuse (`assert_train_only`) a validation row. Validation labels are read once, by the `validate` job, after the candidates are frozen and their hash is committed in a dated addendum.

## 4. Candidates (at most three, all frozen before validation)
- **Rule family (zero model calls):** deterministic restrictions on the cached decision (`exp`, `conf`, `flow`, side) and on pre-entry fields (gap, in-session, price, 8-K item code) from the registered menu in `params_w4.json`. The fit is greedy on **train only**: from the frozen w3 rule (theta 0.5%), add the one restriction with the largest paired gain per offered item at 1x cost that has day-clustered paired t >= 2.0 and at least 60 train trades, up to 3 steps; no qualifying option ends the search (the candidate then equals w3 and is dropped, not counted as a win).
- **w4 prompt (Haiku calls):** one new prompt text written after reading the train-only error analysis, registered with its sha256 in dated addendum 1 **before any w4 call**. Its analogs may be HH **train** items closed strictly before the item's `t_pub` (non-S&P analogs instead of the S&P 500 development pool). Its abstention threshold is fitted on train by `logic_r2.fit_theta`. A candidate may also combine w4 with the train-fitted rule. Calls cover all HH items (train and validation) and never see a label.
- w3 itself (rule {} at theta 0.5%) is the baseline, not a candidate.

## 5. Validation, routing, power
One shot on the validation rows with every needed reply: for each candidate the net P&L per offered item at 1x and 2x cost, its random control, and the **paired difference to w3** on the same items (analytic day-clustered t, exact sign test). **Routing rule:** a candidate is routed only if its paired difference to w3 is > 0 at 1x **and** 2x, its own net per trade at 2x is > 0, and it has at least 40 validation trades; of those the largest paired 2x difference is chosen. None qualifies: **nothing is locked** and that is the reported result. Selection of the best of at most 3 noisy differences is a stated optimism; Holm-adjusted p-values are reported, not gating. Power: the full HH set had a standard error of about 0.035% per offered item for the paired-to-random difference; the validation third has about 85 w3 trades and a standard error near 0.06%, so "beats w3 on validation" can neither confirm nor refute an edge of the size seen (+0.03% to +0.05% per offered).

## 6. Forward arm (separate, by a later dated addendum)
If a candidate is routed, its version is locked (prompt sha256 in a registry, fitted rule and theta in a lock file, a dated addendum) in its own commit; the forward arm scores it only on items whose `t_pub` is after that commit's UTC day. It is a **new** arm (its own selection from the pod's `sec_8k` rows with its own seed, the same pre-entry liquidity filter as HH: price >= 5 USD and mean dollar volume >= 2M USD, its own data branch); it reads nothing of the CMP wide arm's items or outcomes and writes nothing there. Headline: version minus random control; secondary: paired difference to Haiku w3 on the same items. Its ledger weight follows PLAN_20261128 section 2 rule F (standalone ledger number m, bar 40 m (m + 1)), fixed in the addendum before the first event. The existing locked arms keep their versions.
