## 2. The holdout
- **Window:** SEC `acceptanceDateTime` (true UTC, read as in round 2 through `logic_r2.fix_ts`) in **[2026-08-01, 2026-11-20)**. It ends a few sessions before the forward era so every 1 h and end-of-day label settles, and shares no item with the forward arms.
- **Universe (outcome-free):** companies listed on Nasdaq or NYSE in the SEC `company_tickers_exchange.json` that were **never** an S&P 500 member in any spell (ticker or CIK) and whose symbol is not in `core/universe.py` `STOCKS` (the 148-stock list). One ticker per CIK (the alphabetically first). Therefore items and issuers are disjoint from every earlier round, from N1 and from the forward arms. Item keys (`sec:<accession>`) found in any item file of `data/news-train`, `-r2`, `-r3`, `-r3b` and `-cmp` are also excluded and counted (expected 0). Price-only small-cap studies (`l3_smallcap_news`, `smallcap_intraday`) may have read returns of some of these issuers on some of these days; no language model read any of these filings in them, and they are not text-reading studies. **Stated overlap:** the calendar days coincide with the round-1 sealed and N1 windows, so a market-wide move on a day was seen through other issuers; Haiku, Sonnet and the random control are all scored on the same items, so the comparison between them is paired and shares that common factor.
- **Items:** 8-K (not 8-K/A) with one of the registered round-2 item codes (1.01, 1.02, 2.01, 2.02, 2.05, 2.06, 3.01, 4.02, 5.02, 7.01, 8.01) or an empty item list; text, redaction, 3,000-character cut and sector descriptor as round 2 with one change: the issuer line reads "a US-listed company outside the S&P 500 (sector: ...)". At most **3 items per issuer** and **3,000 drawn** by sha256 order of `seed:sec:<accession>` (seed in the params), before any bar is read.
- **Entry and labels:** exactly round 2: entry at the open of the first 1-hour Yahoo bar at or after `t_pub + 93 s + 5 s`; holds 1 h (primary) and end of day; cost 0.09% per side plus 0.02% a session for shorts (0.20% round trip), 2x cost reported. Pre-entry fields as round 2/3.
- **Liquidity floor (from bars before entry and the entry price only, no outcome):** entry price at least 5 USD and the mean dollar volume per session of the up to 5 sessions before the entry session at least 2,000,000 USD (at least 3 such sessions). Items failing it are dropped and counted. **Callable** = a label, the 1 h gross, rv and the pre-entry move exist, liquidity passes, text of at least 80 characters. The registered set is the first **2,000** callable items by sha order (a usage-limit stop therefore leaves an unbiased prefix); the build gate needs at least **1,000**.
- Selection bias, stated: items whose symbol has no Yahoo 1-hour history (delisted names, failed calls) drop out, which selects toward survivors and liquid names; the count is reported.

Both models read the same bytes (`user_sha` stored): head **w3** (direction with calibrated abstention, 5 analogs from the round-2 development pool closed strictly before the item) and head **m1** (size). The abstention threshold theta is the **frozen** round-2 value of w3 stock (`calib.json` on `data/news-train-r2`: 1 h 0.5%, eod 0.75%), applied unchanged to both models; nothing is refitted on this set. Fitted on large caps, theta may trade more often on small caps; the trade rate is reported.

## 5. Registered family (this is family k = 61; alpha_61 = 0.10 / (61 x 62) = 2.644e-5)
**Family k:** one outcome-reading protocol counts once (PLAN_20261128 section 2). k = 61 is the next free number (K0 = 60, no other family has been numbered); if another outcome-reading family reads an outcome before this one's merge step, k and alpha_k are recomputed from the same formula and the new value is written into the result, never chosen after seeing it.
**Primary metric (all heads w3, stock, 1 h):** mean net P&L per offered item, abstain = 0.
- **H1** Haiku minus the random control (one-sided, > 0). **H2** Sonnet minus the random control (one-sided, > 0). **H3** Sonnet minus Haiku on the items both answered (two-sided).
- **Random control:** for every item the model trades, the exact expectation of a random long/short coin flip under the same costs and the same hold (`logic.random_expectation`); 0 where the model abstains. The always-abstain (0), keyword-direction and long-everything baselines are reported, not tested.
- **Test statistic:** the per-item difference, **analytic day-clustered one-sided t** (ratio estimator over UTC days, Student t with days - 1 degrees of freedom, `logic_hh.cluster_t`) for H1 and H2; the **exact two-sided sign test** on the non-zero paired differences (`logic_cmp.sign_test`) for H3. The 2,000-draw bootstraps of the older rounds bottom out at 5e-4 and return 0 when no draw is <= 0, so a bootstrap p is **never** used against alpha_k (a unit test checks that a missing analytic p and a bootstrap p of 0 cannot meet the bar); bootstrap intervals are descriptive only.
- **Holm** over the family, m = 3 (`logic.holm_adjust`).
**Verdicts (fixed now):**
- **Nomination (rule H, per model):** "nominated for a forward ledger" only if the Holm-adjusted p of H1 (Haiku) or H2 (Sonnet) is at most alpha_61 **and** the mean difference to random is positive **and** the net mean per trade is positive at 1x **and** at 2x cost **and** at least 100 trades. Otherwise "not nominated". The words "found" and "confirmed" are never used for a historical result.
- **Model comparison:** needs, from decisions alone, at least 1,000 items both answered, at least 100 trades per model, and at least 100 discordant pairs (the two models' 1 h trade sides differ, abstentions included). Then "Sonnet better" / "Haiku better" if the Holm-adjusted sign-test p of H3 is below 0.05 and the mean paired difference has that sign, else "no difference detected". Below the discordant count: "underpowered". Below the first two: "unread". This verdict makes no claim of an edge and is not a nomination.
- **H2 arithmetic on paper (rule H2 of the plan):** the plan needs x_holdout x holdout months at least z^2 = 16.3 at k = 61; this holdout has 3.6 months, so x of about 4.5 would be needed. A text edge of that size is not expected from rounds 1 to 3 (every earlier cell was within 0.1% of zero per offered item), so **"not nominated" is the expected outcome**. The run is done because the owner asked for the model comparison and for the fastest clean read of Haiku against random, knowing that it spends family number 61.
- **Descriptive, never gated:** eod hold, 2x cost, the keyword and long baselines, trade rate and share long, per-month results (Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov), the m1 magnitude head for both models (partial Spearman given realized volatility and the pre-entry move, raw rank correlation, paired difference of the two partials), call latency, tokens, notional list price.

## 6. Sample-size gate and the one shot
`build` prints counts only (issuers, filings, drawn, bar coverage, drops by reason, callable per month) and writes the prompts without labels; the workflow's `run` mode proceeds only if callable >= 1,000. The call jobs never see a label. `merge` computes the counts gate from decisions alone (`logic_hh.count_gate`) **before** it opens any label; below the floor it writes `unread.json` with counts and reads no outcome, so a reach or usage failure costs no read of outcomes (the decisions are cached on `data/news-train-hh` and a later run resumes only the missing ones, deciding on counts, never outcomes). When the gate passes `merge` writes `result.json` once and refuses to run again if it exists. A usage limit stops a shard after 12 consecutive empty replies (`capped_by_usage_limit` is recorded); the analysis uses items both models answered and the cap is stated.

## 7. Integrity checks and what is reported
Rows, unique keys, unique issuers, per-month counts (the splits), prior-key overlap, drops by reason, round row counts, trade counts and winning and losing trades per model (RUNBOOK 4.1: a result with no losing trades, p at a floor or a single split is a data suspect), served-model mismatches (must be 0), notional spend. LLM spend goes to `reports/COSTS.md`.

## 8. Limits stated before the run
Costs of 0.20% are optimistic for small caps even after the liquidity floor (2x is reported); the 93 s lag is a proxy; 4-5% of filings arrive in session; survivorship through Yahoo coverage; theta fitted on large caps; the sector descriptor says "outside the S&P 500", which tells the model the issuer is smaller; the issuer set is one list of one date (the SEC exchange file as of the run); the calendar overlap in section 2; one window, one run, one comparison.
