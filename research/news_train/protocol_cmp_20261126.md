# Sonnet 5.5 versus Haiku 5.5 on news analysis: paired head-to-head, dated protocol 2026-11-26 (written before any Sonnet call)

**Owner exception (2026-11-26):** for this research comparison only, `claude-sonnet-5-5` may be called next to `claude-haiku-5-5`, through the same path as all news_train work (`core.ai_news.chat`, Claude CLI, Max plan, `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN`). AGENTS rule 7 otherwise stands: the production bot, `core/` and every locked file are unchanged and default to Haiku; results are never pooled across models. The model is switched in-process by `research/news_train/run_cmp.py` only. Locked files (`protocol*`, `params*.json` other than `params_cmp.json`, `LOCK*.json`, `versions*.json`, prompts, `logic*.py`) are imported, not edited. Code: `run_cmp.py`, `logic_cmp.py`; workflow `news_train_cmp.yml`; data branch `data/news-train-cmp`. `params_cmp.json` sha256: 2d6527eeaeac3c418e5986df687b2810906eb23b7f4f1db2e59db42e8e7395e4.

## Design
Paired: same items, same locked prompts (round-2 w1 crypto direction, round-2 w3 stock direction, round-3 m1 stock magnitude), same bars, labels, costs (0.20% stocks / 0.40% crypto round trip, abstain = 0) and the same calibrated-abstention thresholds. Thresholds are **frozen at the locked Haiku-fitted values** (`calib.json` on `data/news-train-r2`) for both models: Sonnet cannot be fitted on a clean train window (its memory may cover it). Only the model differs. Every prompt is rendered once and both models read the same bytes (`user_sha` stored per call).

## 1. Leakage first (gate)
Round-1 memory probe (`run.py leak`, unchanged) is run on Sonnet 5.5. Registered rule (checked against the round-1 Haiku table, which gives 2025-11): a month is *known* when at least 4 of 12 questions were answered with accuracy >= 0.60 on either asset; the model's cutoff is the last known month; **the combined cutoff is the later of the two models'**; an item is eligible only if `t_pub` is on or after the first day of the month after it. Ineligible items are dropped from every set (the stock July set is dropped entirely when the combined cutoff is 2026-06 or later). The probe also checks the `served` string of each reply (must be the requested model) and the notional USD per token.

## 2. Historical secondary set: DESCRIPTIVE
Round-2 sealed GDELT crypto (2026-08-01..11-26, w1, registered n 1,971) and round-3 sealed July 2026 8-Ks (callable items, registered n 518; m1 magnitude and w3 direction on the same items). These were opened once for Haiku, so **no gate, no verdict, no Holm**. Haiku is re-run fresh on the same prompts (the stored calls carry no timing); agreement with the registered Haiku run is reported as a reproduction check. Reported per model: direction net P&L per trade and per offered item at each hold, 2x cost, partial Spearman of the size estimate given realized volatility and the pre-entry move (stock m1), call latency (median, p90), tokens and notional list price. Paired: mean difference of net P&L per offered item with a day-clustered bootstrap, exact two-sided sign test on non-zero differences (ties dropped, counted), discordant count; for m1 the difference of the two partial rhos with a day bootstrap. Round-3 stock m1 was already weak (partial 0.145 sealed, 0.047 with an item-2.02 flag); w3 on the July items is first opened here, for both models, and is never read back into the round-2 forward result.
Usage limits: calls run at parallel 3; a shard stops a model after 12 consecutive empty replies (limit or outage) and never loops. Items go in a fixed sha order so a stopped shard leaves an unbiased prefix; the analysis uses only items both models answered, and the cap is stated in the report.

## 3. Primary set: forward (pod ingest log)
Source: `data/news-ingest` (pod rows `item`/`llm`, 10-minute publishing). Scored by the scheduled job (cron 17 */3 * * *) in Actions, both models on the identical prompt.
- **Crypto (primary):** rows of `binance_listings`, `binance_news`, `binance_delist`, `okx_announcements`, `eqn_rest` with `t_seen` >= 2026-11-27 00:00 UTC, mapped to the round-2 20-coin universe by the locked `coin_of`, de-duplicated as round 2, at most 6 per coin and UTC day by sha order, text redacted as round 2, head w1. Prompt pre-entry fields use 1-minute spot bars known at `t_seen + 5 s` (Binance Vision REST; spot for both models). Decision once per item, never changed, up to 3 attempts.
- **Entry:** each model enters at its own t_llm = `t_seen` + that model's measured wall-clock call time (retries included) + 5 s. The call is made later in Actions; the measured duration stands in for the live t_llm and is the only latency used. Sonnet is slower, so its entry is later; that cost is part of the comparison.
- **Stock:** round-2 forward 8-Ks that the pod also saw (real `t_seen`, join as the ingest addendum), heads w3 (direction) and m1 (magnitude), lag = seen minus published, then each model's call time; hourly bars rarely move the entry, so latency will rarely matter here. **Outcomes are not read until round 2's own forward result (`fwd/result.json` on `data/news-train-r2`) exists**, so these labels cannot steer that one-shot; stock decisions are cached meanwhile. Stock direction is likely to take months (a few percent of about 13 items a day trade); stock magnitude headline needs n >= 300 and is descriptive only.
- **Statistic:** per-item d = Sonnet net P&L minus Haiku net P&L at 1 h (each at its own entry), paired; mean d with a day-clustered bootstrap (2,000 draws, seed in params), exact two-sided sign test on non-zero d.
- **Pre-registered minimum n (counts only, no outcome):** the crypto result is computed once when the paired settled items (both models answered, both entries + 1 h of bars exist, after the daily cap) reach **300** and the **discordant** pairs (the two models' 1 h trade side differs, including one abstaining) reach **100**; or on 2027-03-31 whichever is first, in which case it is labelled underpowered. `progress.json` publishes counts and a linear projection only. Verdict: "Sonnet better" if mean d > 0, the bootstrap interval excludes 0 and the sign p < 0.05; "worse" mirror; else "no difference detected"; below the minimum n it is "underpowered". `result_crypto.json` is written once.
- Everything is Claude CLI on the Max plan, shared with the production bot's token: Sonnet runs at low parallelism; if limits are hit the sample is capped and `progress.json` says so.

Stated limits: Sonnet's knowledge may include early 2026, so only the gated months are used; GDELT hourly stamps and the 93 s proxy are as locked; forward spot bars differ slightly from the perpetual; the 20-coin universe excludes small-cap listings.
