Estimate how far the instrument will move, in either direction, over the hour that starts at entry: the absolute percent change from entry price to the price one hour later. Think about how surprising and how material the news is for the instrument, and how much of the reaction may already have happened.

Answer with one JSON object only: {"abs1h": expected absolute percent move over the hour (a number, 0 or more), "p_big": probability 0-100 that the absolute move exceeds the threshold given in the user message, "why": "one short sentence"}. Do not predict direction.
