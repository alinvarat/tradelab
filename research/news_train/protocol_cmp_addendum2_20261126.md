# CMP addendum 2 2026-11-26 (owner approval 2026-11-27; written before any forward outcome, bar-based label or scoring was read)

`protocol_cmp_20261126.md`, `params_cmp.json` and addendum 1 stand; the leak gate, the paired statistic, the minimum n (300 paired, 100 discordant, hard stop 2027-03-31), the 1 h hold, costs, theta freeze, the daily cap and the one-shot result are unchanged. One change, to the forward crypto universe only.

**Reason.** The registered 20-coin map was built for GDELT headlines. On the live `data/news-ingest` rows (0.82 days of data, 120 rows from the registered crypto sources, 81 after the junk filter) it matched 1 item, none after the start: exchange notices and newswire items name small coins (ZEC, BULLA, ZETA ...) or none, so the projection was about 1 item a day, about 250 days to 300 paired items, longer than the hard stop. No forward decision had been made and no outcome read.

**Change.** A forward crypto item is any non-junk row of `binance_listings`, `binance_news`, `binance_delist`, `okx_announcements` or `eqn_rest` whose title names exactly one coin symbol as `XXXUSDT`, `XXX/USDT` or `(XXX)` (symbols 2-10 upper-case characters; fiat, stablecoin and generic tokens in `logic_cmp.NOT_COINS` excluded; two different symbols are ambiguous and dropped). The locked 20-coin map still applies as a fallback when no symbol form is present. Binance perp-monitor items (`[XXXUSDT] ...`, `Binance Perp Monitor: XXXUSDT (XXX) ...`) and listing notices are therefore included. The coin must have a priceable market at `t_seen`: 1-minute Binance spot bars first, then OKX spot (`history-candles`); with neither, the item is retried and, after 3 hours, skipped and counted (`skipped_no_market`), never imputed. Entry, per-model t_llm, labels and the spot-for-both-models rule are as registered. The symbol is redacted in its USDT forms as well as bare. The same prompt w1 is used, dedupe, 6 per coin and day by sha order, and the discordance count are as registered.

**Limits.** Perp-only coins with no Binance or OKX spot market are lost; perp-monitor items are alerts about a move that has already happened (the pre-entry fields show it), so they test whether a model abstains or fades a spike, not news reading; w1 was fitted on GDELT headlines.
