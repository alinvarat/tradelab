# Verified traders with third-party track records, 2015–2026 (small accounts emphasis)

Research date: 24 Nov 2026. All figures are quoted as the named source showed them on the stated date; they move. "Verified" is split into four levels throughout: **(A) platform-computed from a real brokerage account on a regulated venue**, **(B) platform-timestamped but modelled/hypothetical fills**, **(C) organiser-audited contest account**, **(D) self-reported / broker-statement-only / unverified**.

---

## Which retail/prop traders have accounts verified by Myfxbook, FX Blue, Darwinex, Collective2, FundSeeder, broker statements or audited competition results — with size, period, return, drawdown, leverage, instruments, fees and links?

### Takeaway
The long, high-quality verified records are overwhelmingly **moderate-return** (15–25%/yr gross) systematic FX/CFD or multi-asset programmes with 13–20% peak drawdowns — not the 100%+/month claims. The verified *small-account* high-return records that do exist are almost all **commercial EA vendor accounts on Myfxbook** (affiliate-incentive conflict) or **contest accounts where only the best of several accounts is published**. No audited, independent, multi-year case of a sub-$500 account compounding into a large one was found for 2015–2026.

### Inferences
- The honest hierarchy for a retail reader is: Darwinex/FundSeeder broker-linked platform record > Robbins audited contest account > Collective2 timestamped signals > Myfxbook/FX Blue verified > broker PDF > screenshot. Each step down adds a failure mode that no badge removes.
- Verified long records cluster at **15–25%/yr gross** with **13–20% max DD**. A 20%/yr gross return before 1.2% + 20% fees is roughly 15%/yr net to an investor — the realistic ceiling observed, not 10%/month.
- The "small account compounding" stories that survive scrutiny are **vendor marketing accounts** whose gain is measured off a tiny first deposit, with no withdrawals and no published monthly distribution. That is survivorship plus deposit-relative maths, not evidence of a repeatable method at scale.
- Contest results are the best evidence of *capability under real money* but the worst evidence of *repeatability*: best-of-several accounts, 12-month windows, no drawdown penalty, and the organiser also sells subscriptions to the winners.

### Gaps
- Darwinex public pages render max drawdown and best/worst month only in the logged-in/app view; Closelook explicitly says "insufficient data" for both SYO and THA. The −13.09%/−14.47% figures appear only on Grokipedia (AI-generated, secondary) and are **not confirmed** here.
- No verified monthly return distribution (all 100+ months) was found for any small-account record; only averages and max DD.
- Leverage used by Darwinex traders is normalised away by the DARWIN risk engine, so the underlying account's leverage is not public.
- FundSeeder does not publish per-trader loss data or the failure rate of ranked traders.
- Myfxbook account pages for FXStabilizer/Waka Waka were not opened directly (vendor roundup only); the badge status should be re-checked at the source before publication.

## Which of them compounded a small account (under a few thousand USD) into a large one, over what time — and what was the monthly distribution and worst loss?

### Takeaway
Only two documented cases get from **$500 to five figures**, and both are EA vendor accounts on Myfxbook over 3–10 years, with no independently published month-by-month record. The best-documented "small → large" numbers are **$500 → $17,856 in 10 years** (FXStabilizer, ~3.0%/month) and **$1,000 → $165,883 in 24 months** (EuroStable — but its track record badge is unverified). Every sub-$500 claim encountered was unverifiable, demo, or a contest/vendor artefact.

### Cited Findings
- **FXStabilizer EUR Turbo:** $500 deposited Apr 2016, balance $17,856 by Mar 2026, +3,729%, 3.01% average monthly, max DD 13.26%, 2,465 tracking, **$0 withdrawals**, equity 100% of balance, 10 years live. 36× on the initial deposit over a decade. — [Oreshnikbot](https://oreshnikbot.com/most-profitable-forex-eas-with-myfxbook-proof/).
- **Waka Waka EA:** $500 initial, +3,088%, ~5%/month, 14.40% max DD, **$4,352 withdrawn** over ~3 years, multi-currency (not gold-only). The same source admits the displayed data is from 2023, not current. — [Oreshnikbot](https://oreshnikbot.com/most-profitable-forex-eas-with-myfxbook-proof/).
- **EuroStable EA Lite:** $1,000 → $165,883 in ~24 months, 2,418 trades, PF 2.59, 71–73% win rate, 22.94% DD, $18,400 withdrawn — but Track Record is **not verified**, broker is xChief, live update inactive. This is the clearest "too good to be true, and the badge says so" case. — [Oreshnikbot](https://oreshnikbot.com/most-profitable-forex-eas-with-myfxbook-proof/).
- **PrismAlgo:** $1,000 → $3,526 in 9 months (+252.53%), 952 trades, PF 2.14, 9.58% DD, with an explicit month-by-month table: Feb 2026 **+84.91%**, Mar +1.90%, Apr +6.58%, May +1.34%, Jun +8.38%, Jul −0.25%, Aug +0.94%, Sep +3.76%, Oct +13.67%, Nov +34.57% (partial). The first partial month is 34% of the total gain. — [PrismAlgo](https://prismalgo.com/results/).
- **Maros EA:** vendor advertises it "works with small deposits, runs from $100–$150"; live balance $3,167.62, +140.80%, DD 30.82%, "monthly gain 140.80%" — i.e. the headline is a single month. — [MT4 Systems](https://mt4systems.com/product/maros-ea/).
- **Seagull EA MT5** is often cited as a small-account winner but the deposit history is $23,100 with $15,433 withdrawn; the vendor-favourable "+2,237%" is deposit-structure-driven, while absolute gain is +8.60%. Not a small-account case. — [Oreshnikbot](https://oreshnikbot.com/most-profitable-forex-eas-with-myfxbook-proof/).

### Inferences
- $500 → $17,856 over 10 years is **~36×**, i.e. ~41.5%/yr compounded, which is above the 15–25%/yr band of audited Darwinex records. That gap is itself a warning: either the EA is exceptional, or the record benefits from a survivorship-selected account and zero withdrawals (no test of real capital extraction).
- Deposit-relative gain maths makes small accounts look spectacular: +3,729% on $500 is $17,356 of profit; the same percentage on $100k would require $3.7M of profit and would be capacity-constrained.
- Where month-by-month data exists (PrismAlgo, 9 months), returns are **lumpy**: one month +84.9%, another +34.6%, one negative. A "3%/month average" series almost certainly contains double-digit losing months and long flat stretches; no source published them.

### Gaps
- No source published a full monthly distribution or worst single month for FXStabilizer or Waka Waka. Without it, the "3%/month" figure cannot be stress-tested.
- No verified case of a **sub-$500** account reaching $10k+ in 2015–2026 was found; all such claims were screenshots, demo, or affiliate content.
- Leverage, broker, commission and swap details for the EA accounts are not disclosed in the accessible sources.

## What methods did they use — trend following, breakout, mean reversion, news, options, scalping, grid/martingale, carry? Exact rules where disclosed

### Takeaway
Disclosed methods split into three families: **systematic short-term trend/payoff-asymmetry** (THA, ares, Jagd), **systematic mean-reversion/value** (Easy Value, Weishar), and **discretionary contest trading** (Robbins winners, rules not published). Almost no high-return record discloses full entry/exit rules; the ones that do are the low-return, low-drawdown ones.

### Cited Findings
- **DARWIN THA (finbou):** systematic short-term, 18,756 trades, average hold **under 3 hours**, **30.6% win rate**, +20.47%/yr gross. Closelook: the low win rate with positive return is "the signature of a trend-style payoff distribution" — many small losses, few large winners. Exact entries not public. — [Closelook THA](https://closelook.net/records/darwin-tha-finbou/).
- **DARWIN SYO (Sersan Sistemas):** systematic/algorithmic FX/CFD, run by a Spanish systems firm; rules proprietary, no public entry logic. — [Closelook SYO](https://closelook.net/records/darwin-syo-sersan-sistemas/).
- **ares (Collective2):** automated short-term **trend following** on Micro Nasdaq (MNQ), long and short; disclosed rules: **1% risk per trade, no overnight positions, a stop on every trade**; 74 months, +32.4%/yr hypothetical, 14.1% max DD. — [Closelook ares](https://closelook.net/records/c2-ares-systemtrading/).
- **Easy Value (Collective2):** **value investing**, whole-portfolio, buys below estimated intrinsic value, US stocks, 72 months, +22.3%/yr incl. costs, 31.8% DD. — [Closelook Easy Value](https://closelook.net/records/c2-easy-value/).
- **Christian Jagd:** **trend following via trend channels and breakouts**, medium-to-long term, self-built signal tool, long/short ETFs for risk management, 12.5 years, +18.7%/yr. — [Closelook](https://closelook.net/records/).
- **Florian Riediger:** relative-strength momentum (TSI) + fundamentals, Nasdaq small/mid caps, 9.8 years, +23.7%/yr, **72.8% volatility**, −20.4% trailing year. — [Closelook](https://closelook.net/records/).
- **Simon Weishar:** value/dividend/turnaround, 6–12-month holds, own valuation algorithm, **no leverage, no ETFs**, German/EU equities only, 12 years, +24.7%/yr. — [Closelook](https://closelook.net/records/).
- **Robbins champions:** no method disclosure in the standings; CTE notes winners span "discretionary futures traders, systematic engineers and multi-market algorithmic portfolios". No exact rules. — [CTE WCTC](https://completetradersedge.com/world-cup-trading-championships/).
- **No verified record in this sample discloses grid/martingale rules as such.** The grid/martingale risk appears instead as a *warning pattern* in EA marketing (smooth equity curve, hidden floating losses), not as a disclosed method. — [Steven Imrich, "Can Myfxbook be faked"](https://www.stevenimrichbooks.com/trading/verify-myfxbook-ea-track-record/); [Forex EA Watch](https://forexeawatch.com/scams/fake-myfxbook-results/).
- **Carry/news/options scalping:** no verified 2015–2026 retail record with disclosed rules was found in this pass.

### Inferences
- The repeatable-looking edge is **payoff asymmetry plus strict per-trade risk**, not win rate: THA wins 30.6% and ares wins ~35–40% of trades yet both compound. Retail marketing sells the opposite (high win rate, small TP, wide SL), which is the negative-skew profile that blows up.
- Exact rules are disclosed only where returns are modest (ares 32%/yr hypothetical, Jagd 18.7%/yr). The higher the claimed return, the less rule disclosure — consistent with edge being either capacity-limited, fragile, or absent.
- Contest winners' methods are unknowable from public data, so a contest percentage cannot be converted into a repeatable rule set.

### Gaps
- No verified source disclosed the actual entry/exit parameters of SYO or THA.
- No audited record using **carry, news straddles, or options** at retail size was found for the period.
- Whether any of the EA accounts use grid/martingale internally is not disclosed; only the equity-vs-balance and trade-duration checks can flag it, and those were not available for the vendor accounts.

## Which records are fake, unaudited, demo, backtest or survivorship-biased — including known blow-ups and prop-firm challenge failure rates?

### Takeaway
The biggest lie is not a forged statement; it is a **real, verified record that answers a different question** — demo account, cent account, best-of-many accounts, or a date range chosen after the fact. Prop "funded" accounts are explicitly **demo accounts with fictitious funds** at major firms, and only ~7% of challenge buyers ever receive a payout.

### Cited Findings

**Verification limits (Myfxbook / FX Blue)**
- Track Record ✅ only proves the numbers match the broker; Trading Privileges ✅ only proves the poster holds the master password. **A demo account can legitimately carry both badges.** — [Myfxbook help](https://www.myfxbook.com/help/knowledge-base/verification/); [Steven Imrich](https://www.stevenimrichbooks.com/trading/verify-myfxbook-ea-track-record/).
- Known ways a real record misleads: demo dressed as live; custom date range in the URL; hidden trade list/open positions (hides floating loss); restarted history; one good account out of many; generic "Other (MT4)" server. — [Steven Imrich](https://www.stevenimrichbooks.com/trading/verify-myfxbook-ea-track-record/).
- Outright fabrication route: run your **own MetaTrader server**, disguise it as a broker, connect the tracker — every trade is whatever you decide. Also documented: broker-side injection in collusion with a shady broker. — [Mechanical Forex, 2014, quoted on Forex Factory](https://www.forexfactory.com/thread/588456-be-cautious-of-myfxbook-stats-they-can-be?page=2); [Forex EA Watch](https://forexeawatch.com/scams/fake-myfxbook-results/).
- Myfxbook itself acknowledged a historical upload bug where selecting specific days could temporarily show incomplete history; it says the system now back-fills missing tickets from the broker. — [Myfxbook forum thread, 2010](https://www.myfxbook.com/community/suggestion-box/fatal-flaw-track-record-verification/33132,1).
- **Cent accounts** display 100× the real money P&L (e.g. a $100 deposit shown as 10,000 cents), inflating gain% and making a $100 account look like a $10,000 one. — [Facebook/WillInvest example of a 1:1000 cent account](https://www.facebook.com/WillInvestPH/posts/implementation-of-forex-auto-trading-using-fx-scalper-x-account-up-by-624-since-/575669277904415/) (anecdotal, but the mechanic is standard).

**Prop firms: demo money, low payout base rates**
- **FTMO:** "all accounts we provide to our clients are demo accounts with fictitious funds and any trading is in a simulated environment only"; the FTMO Account uses real market quotes but fictitious capital. — [FTMO FAQ](https://ftmo.com/en/faq/how-does-the-ftmo-technical-infrastructure-work/); [FTMO Forbidden Trading Practices](https://ftmo.com/en/forbidden-trading-practices/).
- **FPFX Tech data via Finance Magnates (Sep 2024), 300,000+ accounts, 100,000 traders, 10 firms:** **14% passed** a challenge and got funded; **~45% of those received a payout → ~7% of all traders**; average payout **~4% of plan size**; average spend **~$800** across ~3 challenges. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/exclusive-only-7-of-300000-prop-trading-accounts-achieved-payouts/).
- Firm-published pass rates: FunderPro **7.35%**, FTMO **8–10%** (CEO, Forbes, no period defined), E8 Markets **17.7%** (Jan 2023–Mar 2024), Hola Prime **35%** (Nov 2024–May 2025), Topstep **16.8% of challenges / 51.8% of traders**. Definitions differ; none is audited. — [Forexop, checked 18 Oct 2026](https://forexop.com/learning/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rate/); [The5ers](https://the5ers.com/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rates/).
- **Goat Funded Trader (Oct 2026):** 594,997 challenge buyers → 28,859 live funded accounts; 22,279 traders paid ≈ **3.7% of buyers**. — [ThePropFirmGuide](https://thepropfirmguide.com/prop-firm-statistics/).
- **MyForexFunds / Traders Global:** CFTC complaint (Aug 2023) alleged a **$310M+ fraud on 135,000+ customers**, including misrepresenting simulated accounts as funded and hidden $3/trade commissions; the case was **dismissed in May 2025**, so these remain allegations, not adjudicated findings. — [CFTC complaint PDF](https://www.cftc.gov/media/9196/enftradersglobalgroupcomplaint082923/download); [De Silva Law, May 2025](https://www.desilvalawoffices.com/articles/blog/2025/may/cftc-case-dismissed-my-forex-funds-controversy-h/).

**Contest survivorship**
- Robbins entry agreements allow **multiple contest accounts and rank only the best one**; late entry is allowed and returns are not annualised; there is **no drawdown penalty**; the organiser is also the introducing broker and sells subscriptions to winners. — [CTE WCTC](https://completetradersedge.com/world-cup-trading-championships/).
- The organiser settled a CFTC failure-to-supervise case in 2003 ($120,000 penalty) tied to the Aurum Society commodity-pool fraud; a 2025 CFTC reparations complaint by a follower (*Morris v. Robbins*) was dismissed because the customer had been warned results might differ. — [CTE WCTC, citing CFTC 4791-03 and Morris 2025](https://completetradersedge.com/world-cup-trading-championships/).
- **Verified blow-ups:** DARWIN MIL −37.45% over 10.5 years; DARWIN BDR +2.21% over 11 years; ARKK −80% peak-to-trough 2021–2022 despite +152.5% in 2020. — [Darwinex MIL](https://www.darwinex.com/invest/MIL); [Darwinex BDR](https://www.darwinex.com/invest/BDR); [Closelook ARK](https://closelook.net/records/ark-invest-cathie-wood/).
- FundSeeder's own reviewer notes a leaderboard account with the best Sharpe (3.73) that had a **recent 50% drawdown** from a negative-skew strategy — a live example of a "verified" record hiding tail risk. — [Elite Trader, 2016](https://www.elitetrader.com/et/threads/fundseeder-my-experience.298394/).

### Inferences
- Apply a four-part filter before believing any number: (1) real vs demo vs cent; (2) full history, not a URL date range; (3) open positions visible and equity ≈ balance; (4) account age and number of sibling accounts. Badges answer none of these.
- The prop-firm funnel (14% funded, 7% paid, payout 4% of plan size) means the expected value of a $147 challenge fee is strongly negative before any trading edge; the fee alone exceeds the account.
- Contest percentages and verified small-account percentages share the same bias: **selection of the best surviving account**. Repeat titles (Unger ×3, Magala ×4, Seibert ×3, Carlile twice) are the only partial correction.

### Gaps
- No forensic teardown of a specific named 2015–2026 high-return Myfxbook account (trade-by-trade reconciliation) was found; the debunking literature is generic.
- No regulator (FCA/CFTC/ASIC) warning naming a specific forex signal seller in this sample was retrieved.
- Prop-firm payout figures are self-reported by firms or by one SaaS vendor (FPFX); none is audited.
- The MyForexFunds dismissal leaves the factual question unresolved; treat both the allegations and the dismissal as documented, not the underlying conduct.

## What constraints would prevent a $147 account from copying them — minimum size, leverage, fees, data, psychology, capacity?

### Takeaway
At $147 the binding constraints are **lot granularity and fixed costs**, not leverage or capacity. One micro lot (0.01) is the smallest FX position at most brokers and already risks ~$0.10/pip; with a normal 30-pip stop that is ~2% of the account per trade. Round-trip spreads alone can consume 10–15% of the account per month at typical retail activity levels. Every serious verified platform (Darwinex $500, FundSeeder $5,000, Robbins $5,000 forex/$10,000 futures) also has a minimum deposit above $147.

### Cited Findings
- **Lot granularity:** 0.01 lot = 1,000 units of base currency. At EURUSD ~1.08 that is ~$1,080 notional; a 1-pip move = **$0.10**. A 30-pip stop = **$3.00 = 2.04% of $147**. (Arithmetic from the standard contract definition; no single source states the $147 case.)
- **Leverage rules:** ESMA retail CFD limits are **30:1 on major FX** (3.33% margin), 20:1 on non-major FX/gold/indices, 2:1 on crypto; US retail forex is 50:1 on majors. At 30:1, the 0.01 lot above needs ~**$36 margin**; at 1:500 offshore it needs ~**$2.16**. Margin is not the constraint; per-pip loss is. — [ESMA product intervention](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-agrees-prohibit-binary-options-and-restrict-cfds-protect-retail-investors); [ESMA FAQ PDF](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/library/esma71-98-125_faq_esmas_product_intervention_measures.pdf).
- **Spread/fee drag:** 1 pip round-trip on 0.01 lot = $0.10 = **0.068% of $147**. Ten round trips/day × 21 days = 210 × $0.10 = **$21/month = 14.3% of the account** before any loss. (Own arithmetic; labelled inference.)
- **Platform minimums:** Darwinex first deposit **€/$/£500**; FundSeeder official ranking **≥$5,000** and 120 trading days; Robbins forex annual **$5,000**, futures **$10,000**, quarterly $2,500, monthly $500. — [Darwinex help](https://help.darwinex.com/deposit-or-withdraw-funds); [FundSeeder](https://www.fundseeder.com/how-it-works); [CTE WCTC](https://completetradersedge.com/world-cup-trading-championships/).
- **Prop challenge cost:** average ~**$800** spent across ~3 challenge purchases per account lifetime; average payout ~4% of plan size. A $147 account cannot buy a meaningful challenge, and the fee is ~5.4× the account. — [Finance Magnates/FPFX](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/exclusive-only-7-of-300000-prop-trading-accounts-achieved-payouts/).
- **Broker minimum deposits:** micro/cent accounts are widely marketed from $5–$100, so a $147 account is technically possible; the constraint is that the smallest trade is a large fraction of equity, not that the account cannot be opened. — [WillInvest cent-account example](https://www.facebook.com/WillInvestPH/posts/implementation-of-forex-auto-trading-using-fx-scalper-x-account-up-by-624-since-/575669277904415/).

### Inferences
- Effective risk at $147 is **quantised**: the trader cannot risk 0.5% ($0.74) with a 30-pip stop because the minimum lot already risks $3.00. The realistic choice is 1–3% per trade, i.e. 2–6× the intended risk of a professional model — the opposite of the 1%-risk discipline that makes ares and THA survivable.
- Fixed costs dominate: at 0.068% per round trip, a 5-trades/day habit costs ~7%/month; a 10-trades/day habit ~14%/month. Any scalping method verified on a $10k+ account is structurally uncompetitive at $147.
- Compounding maths: $147 → $10,000 requires **68×**. At a genuinely verified 20%/yr net that is ~23 years; at the vendor-claimed 3%/month it is ~14 years with no withdrawals and no blow-up. The pitch "small account, fast growth" requires returns outside every audited record in this sample.
- Psychology: with $3 per trade and a $147 balance, a normal 10-trade losing streak (−$30, −20%) is emotionally equivalent to a 20% drawdown on a $100k account — but with no capacity to size down. This pushes toward martingale/revenge trading, the exact profile that produces the verified blow-ups.

### Gaps
- No study was found that measures **actual retail outcomes by account size** (e.g. % of sub-$500 accounts profitable over 12 months); broker-published win/loss ratios are aggregate and often exclude small accounts.
- Commission and swap schedules for the specific EA accounts (RoboForex, JustMarkets, xChief) were not retrieved, so after-fee returns for those records cannot be computed precisely.
- Whether $147 can access **fractional/micro lots below 0.01** at any regulated broker was not confirmed; some brokers offer 0.001 lots on cent accounts, which would materially change the quantisation argument.
- Capacity is a non-issue at $147 and was not quantified; the constraint is entirely minimum size and fixed cost.
