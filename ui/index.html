Sentiment is a word-list score of each headline from -1 to +1. It misreads sarcasm and context; read the article.

Screener

Every symbol scored on ten daily indicators. Click a row to open its chart.

News shocks, last two sessions

Finds stocks that gapped 3% or more at the open on at least 1.5 times normal volume, with their headlines.

Research on 2010 to 2026 (research/METHODS.md): after a news gap down, large caps beat random-day entries by about 3% over the next 60 days, but as a portfolio this did not reliably beat holding SPY and had deeper drawdowns. Selling after bad news stopped working after 2018.
